Instructions

3. Use the Deposit section to add funds to your accounts.
4. Use the Withdraw section to take out funds. Watch for messages regarding your balance!
5. "Fast Withdraw" instantly takes out a preset amount (default $60).
6. Look for messages at the bottom of the page confirming your transactions or alerting you to issues.
