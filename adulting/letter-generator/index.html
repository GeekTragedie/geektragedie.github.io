Letter Generator

Write a letter of recommendation or a resignation letter in your own name. Pick the kind of letter, fill in the details, and the letter takes shape in the preview as you type.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Choose a letter type, then what the letter is for.
  2. Fill in your details and the details of the person or job the letter is about. Anything still missing shows up in the preview in [brackets].
  3. For a recommendation, tap the strengths that fit and add a story or two. Specific examples are what make a recommendation believable.
  4. Read the whole preview, then copy it into your email or document, print it, or download it as a .txt file. Edit the wording there so it sounds like you.

Not sure where to start? Load example fills in a made-up letter of the type you picked.

Private by design: your letter is put together right here in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored by this site unless you turn on Save in this browser or save a file.
