Assignments Organizer

A week-by-week view of every class: readings, assignments, and due dates in one place, with colors for what’s done, skimmed, or still waiting. Fill it in here, or download a blank spreadsheet.

  • ⏱️ ~30+ min the first time
  • 🔒 100% browser-local: no data leaves your device
  • 📤 Prints a clean PDF • Downloads .csv • Blank .xlsx / .csv templates • Saves .json • Saves in this browser (opt-in)
📖 How to Use This Tool▾

What it does

It puts every class’s readings, assignments, and due dates in one week-by-week grid, so nothing hides in a syllabus.

Step by step

  1. In Term, name the term, pick the day Week 1 starts, and set the Number of weeks (up to 20).
  2. In Courses, use + Add a course for each class and when it meets.
  3. In Week by Week, fill in each week’s readings, assignment, and due date from the syllabus. Set the status to Done, Skimmed, or Not finished as you go, and the cell color changes to match.
  4. Check the Printable plan. Weeks and classes with nothing filled in are left out.
  5. Use Print, Save .json to keep working on it later, or My plan as .csv to open it in a spreadsheet.

Works well with

  • ND Self-Retreat Builder: plan a low-demand rest break for after finals or a heavy week.

Tips

  • Prefer paper or your own spreadsheet? Grab Blank template (.xlsx) or Blank template (.csv) from the tool menu.
  • Copy as text copies your plan as comma-separated rows, ready to paste into a spreadsheet or notes app.
  • Fill it in from the syllabus once at the start of the term. After that, a weekly update only takes a minute.

Your privacy

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. It stays in this browser tab unless you turn on Save in this browser or save a file.
