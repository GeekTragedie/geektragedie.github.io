ND Self-Retreat Builder

A low-demand, zero-decision retreat planner for neurodivergent nervous systems. You set things up once during your prep week, and then each day runs on three simple anchors. Your entries stay on this device. Use Save .json to keep a backup.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Pick Creator's Example to see a real, filled-in retreat, or Build My Own to start yours. Each mode keeps its own entries, so switching never erases anything.
  2. Turn on the neurotypes that fit you (Autism, ADHD, CPTSD; diagnosed or self-identified). The suggestions throughout the binder change to match, and small tags show why each one is there.
  3. In Retreat setup, choose your retreat length, anchor times, morning cue, sanctuary space, communication plan, nature spot, movement, food rescue plan, and main sensory focus.
  4. During your prep week, work through the Prep week checklists and fill in your menus: books, crafts, projects, games, and playlists.
  5. During the retreat, fill in one Daily tracker sheet at each anchor time. Click any menu item to send it straight to today's sheet.
  6. Use Build printable binder, then Download PDF, to save a binder file with blank or filled-in pages, ready to print for a paper binder.

Saving your work: Save in this browser is on by default, because a retreat lasts several days. It keeps your entries on this device only, and nothing is sent to this site. Untick it to erase them from the browser. Use Save .json for a backup you can import later or on another device.

Import, save, print, and reset are in the tool menu.

Build your printable binder

Pick the pages you want, then click Download PDF to save a ready-to-print .pdf file. It takes a few seconds and needs an internet connection, because the PDF maker loads when you click.
