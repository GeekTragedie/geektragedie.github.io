Self-Care Check-In

A two-minute look at how you’re doing, with a few small, doable ideas for whatever is running low. Everything here is optional, and none of it is a test.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Move the sliders and check the basics you’ve covered today.
  2. Read the ideas for the areas that are lowest. Pick one, not all of them.
  3. Use Save today’s check-in to keep a short history, so you can notice patterns over time.

Your privacy: nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your history is only kept if you turn on Save in this browser or save a .json file.

Where the ideas come from: public health sources like NIMH, CDC, SAMHSA, the NHS, the World Health Organization, and the National Autistic Society, listed at the bottom of the page. They’re general ideas, not medical advice; skip anything that doesn’t fit your body or your life.

Need help right now? Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), text HOME to 741741, or call 911 if you’re in danger.
