Ways to meet people and try new things around Seattle and Bellevue. Check each group’s site for current times and costs.

## Book Clubs

- [Silent Book Club](https://silentbook.club/pages/chapter-map): read together quietly, then chat if you want. Find a local chapter on the map.

## Board Games and Art

- [Zulu’s Board Game Cafe events](https://zulusgames.com/pages/calendar): game nights and art events at their Bothell and Lynnwood locations.
- [Meetup: Seattle art groups](https://www.meetup.com/find/us--wa--seattle/art/)
- [Seattle Art Museum public programs](https://www.seattleartmuseum.org/)
- [Bellevue Arts Museum programs and events](https://www.bellevuearts.org/)

## Social Skills Groups

- [Aspiring Youth social skills groups](https://www.aspiringyouth.net/social-skills-groups): groups for neurodivergent kids, teens, and young adults (ages 5–26).

## Sports and Movement

- [YMCA of Greater Seattle adult sports](https://www.seattleymca.org/programs/health-fitness/adult-sports), including basketball
- [Vertical World adult climbing programs and classes](https://seattle.verticalworld.com/climbing/adult-programs-and-classes/)
- [Seattle Bouldering Project](https://boulderingproject.com/market/seattle-washington/)
- [REI classes and events](https://www.rei.com/events)

## Hiking

- [Washington Trails Association: finding someone to hike with](https://www.wta.org/go-outside/new-to-hiking/someone-to-hike-with-hiking-groups-and-resources)
- [Fat Girls Hiking](https://fatgirlshiking.com/): a body-positive hiking community. Check their site and social media for a chapter near you.

## Running

- [Seattle Running Club](https://www.seattlerunningclub.org/)
- [Oiselle Seattle Running Collective](https://www.oiselle.com/pages/seattle-running-collective)

## Biking

- [Cascade Bicycle Club](https://cascade.org/)
- [Everyday Rides groups](https://everydayrides.com/groups)
- [Cyclists of Greater Seattle](https://cyclistsofgreaterseattle.wildapricot.org/)

## More Support

If you need more than social connection, these Seattle-area programs offer intensive outpatient care:

- [Evidence Based Treatment Centers of Seattle](https://ebtseattle.com/)
- [Pathlight Mood & Anxiety Center, Washington](https://www.pathlightbh.com/treatment-centers/washington)
- [Thira Health](https://thirahealth.com/): programs for women and teens with anxiety, depression, and eating disorders.
