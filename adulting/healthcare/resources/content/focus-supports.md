Small changes that make it easier to start and stay on a task. For a step-by-step plan, pair these with Therapist Aid’s free [ADHD Focus Plan worksheet](https://www.therapistaid.com/therapy-worksheet/adhd-focus-plan).

## Focus Supports

- Remove or limit distractions.
- Name your current level of focus.
- Build up your focus time slowly.
- Do one task at a time.
- Take *scheduled* breaks.
- Check your stimulation level with every sense, and adjust it.
- Exercise during the day.
- Prioritize your tasks.
- Keep a distraction to-do list: if something comes up that you need to do but would pull you off task, write it down for later.
- Listen to music without lyrics.
- Make sure you’ve eaten.
- Organize your workstation.
- Put your phone in a drawer: out of sight, out of mind.
- Organize your inbox with labels so incoming email is easier to sort.
- Practice meditation and mindfulness.
- Use a separate, distraction-free browser for work searches.
- Drink electrolytes, especially if you’re drinking a lot of caffeine.
