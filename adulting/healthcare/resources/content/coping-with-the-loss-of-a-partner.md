Losing a partner, whether through a breakup, divorce, or death, changes your daily life as well as your heart. There’s no right way to grieve. These are small ideas to try; pick what fits.

## Let the Feelings Come

- Let yourself feel what comes up, without rushing it.
- Notice what you miss about them, and share those memories and feelings with someone you trust, or write them down.
- Talk about the thoughts, memories, and feelings that keep coming up.

## Notice What’s Different

- Put words to the small ways your days feel different now: mornings, evenings, weekends, meals.
- Think about the things you used to do together. Which ones would you like to keep, change, or let go of?

## Stay Connected and Keep Going

- Spend time with people whose values match yours.
- When you’re ready, get to know someone new or try a new experience, at your own pace.
- Keep a simple routine, even on hard days.
- Try something new, or pick up an interest you’ve set aside.

If grief makes it hard to get through the day for weeks at a time, or you have thoughts of not wanting to be here, reach out to a therapist or doctor. In a crisis, call or text 988.
