Emotional regulation has three parts:

- **Initiating** actions that emotions call for.
- **Inhibiting** actions that emotions call for.
- **Modulating** your responses to emotions.

## Six Useful Emotional Regulation Skills

| Skill | Ask yourself |
|---|---|
| **Self-awareness** | What am I feeling, thinking, and doing right now? |
| **Mindful awareness** | What’s going on around me and inside me? |
| **Cognitive reappraisal** | How am I thinking right now? Does my thinking support my values? |
| **Adaptability** | How flexible am I right now? What changes can I make that are in my control? |
| **Self-compassion** | What am I good at, proud of, or thankful for? |
| **Emotional support** | Can someone support me right now? Do I need help from someone else? |
