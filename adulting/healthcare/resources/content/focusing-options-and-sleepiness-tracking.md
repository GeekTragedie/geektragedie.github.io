## Ways to Try to Focus

- Catch sleepiness early, before it turns into a sleep attack.
- Take a short walk.
  - Refill your water and use the restroom on the way.
- Change your temperature.
  - Cold: cold water or a frozen drink.
  - Warm: a heating pad or a warm drink.
- Use a motor skill.
  - Fine motor: a fidget or tapping.
  - Gross motor: walk or dance in place, or stretch.
- Get a snack.
- Know your healthy caffeine routine.
- Do something exciting or engaging.
  - Talk with someone.
  - Watch a short video about something interesting.
  - Learn something new about one of your interests.
- Think about something you’re looking forward to.
- Drink water.
- Get some sunlight, or consider a sunlamp.
- Give your eyes a break from screens.
- Try a deep breathing exercise.
- Try a full-body muscle relaxation.
