Mental Health Resources

Hotlines, worksheets, and reading I recommend. Use the filters to find what fits you. Many resources open right here on the site: a full page, a PDF, or a short overview with a link to the original.

Looking for clinician material? See the Therapist Resource Library.

  • ⏱️ ~1–3 min
  • 🔒 100% browser-local: no data leaves your device
  • 📤 Interactive reference • Filters and search • Opens on-site pages, PDFs, and summaries
📖 How to Use This Tool▾

What it does

Helps you find hotlines, worksheets, videos, and reading that fit what you’re dealing with, and gives you a ready APA citation for each.

Step by step

  1. Type a word in the search box, or open a group under Filter Resources and tick a box. The count under the search box shows how many match.
  2. Pick a resource and open it. Read on this site opens a full page here, Open the PDF opens a copy hosted here, and Read the overview gives a short summary in our own words.
  3. Use Original source ↗ (or Open ↗) to go to the creator’s own site.
  4. Open Cite under a title to see its APA citation, ready to copy.

Works well with

  • Therapist Resource Library: the clinician side of the same list: theory cheat sheets, research, and clinical reading.

Tips

  • The filters list tags that two or more resources share. The search box also finds rarer tags, titles, and descriptions.
  • Ticking boxes in different groups narrows the list. Ticking more boxes in one group widens it. Use Clear All to start over.
  • Entries marked 🧩 Interactive Tool are things you fill in or click through, not reading.
