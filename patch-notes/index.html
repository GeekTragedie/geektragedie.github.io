For Therapists

November 3, 2026

  • Added Intake Packet Generator. Build a reusable client intake packet (first-time therapy guide, WA disclosure statement, informed consent, practice policies, telehealth consent, HIPAA notice and Good Faith Estimate, and intake questions), and write 90791 intake evaluation notes. It can pull your practice details from a Practice Info Sheet .json file.
  • Added Treatment Plan Builder. SMART treatment plans with an ICD-10 / DSM-5-TR toggle, goal and intervention suggestions matched to the diagnosis, up to 5 active goals with 5 objectives each, a SMART helper, and a record of achieved and archived goals. Session-only.
  • Added Crisis Plan Generator. Collaborative safety plans with six templates (Stanley-Brown Safety Planning Intervention, WRAP, Youth/Caregiver, DV/IPV, Neurodivergent Meltdown & Sensory Overload, and Relapse Prevention), editable contacts and national hotlines, APA citations, and a clinical layout or a one-page “fridge” layout for printing. Session-only.
  • Added Assessment Database. Evidence-based screening tools and outcome measures, sorted by diagnosis, symptoms, and age group, with scoring notes and APA citations. You can add your own assessments.
  • Added Referral Database. A list of the providers you trust, with specialty tags and availability. Private notes stay out of anything you share with a client, and it writes a referral email for you.
  • Added Caseload at a Glance. A de-identified view of your caseload by diagnosis, phase of therapy, time in therapy, age, and other fields you choose, with an intake fit check for new referrals.
  • Added Self-Care Dashboard. A check-in for clinicians: energy and DBT PLEASE skills right now, client order and end-of-day transitions, caseload weight and boundaries, recurring checklists, and a ProQOL-inspired burnout and vicarious trauma check.
  • Added Business Dashboard. A cash-basis P&L, an income and Schedule C expense ledger, a quarterly tax planner (federal estimates plus WA B&O), a monthly bank reconciliation checklist, and a business license tracker.
  • Added Practice Info Sheet. Your NPI, EIN, UBI, taxonomy codes, CAQH, ProviderOne, malpractice insurance, voicemail scripts, and tech/BAA list on one printable sheet, with Full, Therapist, Business, and Tech views.
  • Added CEU Tracker. Tracks the 2-year renewal cycle plus the 4-year health equity and 6-year suicide prevention cycles for WA licenses, with custom states. Clock hours aren’t double-counted when one course covers several requirements, and you can attach certificates.
  • Added Supervision Log. For interns, associates, and approved supervisors. Tracks in-person and telehealth hours (individual, group, and family), indirect hours, custom hour categories, time elapsed, and a supervision documentation checklist.
  • Added Practice Starting Aid. An 8-phase checklist for opening a private practice in Washington: DOH license, PLLC, EIN, NPI, DOR/UBI and city licenses, malpractice insurance, banking, choosing an EHR, a rate calculator, and an office sublet outreach script.
  • Changed The Therapist Tools menu now lists all 15 tools, grouped by Day-to-Day, Finance & Business, Licensure, and Starting a Practice.
  • Added APA 7 reference lists on every tool and the Therapist Tools page, 189 sources in all, grouped by topic (therapy approaches, diagnosis & coding, laws & regulations, WA licensure, tax, and more). This includes a source for 46 of the 47 therapy styles in the Note Generator. Each entry was checked against a publisher, DOI record, or official source, and anything that couldn’t be confirmed was left out.
  • Fixed Citations in the Letter, Note, Treatment Plan, Crisis Plan, and Assessment tools: removed two sources that couldn’t be found in any catalog, corrected the Hoy-Gerlach (2022), Baird & Mollen, and Zimmerman entries, added DOIs and missing dates, and switched diagnosis-code citations to ICD-10-CM (FY 2027).
  • Changed The Intake Packet Generator’s eight editable sections, plus longer tips in the Self-Care Dashboard, Business Dashboard, and Practice Starting Aid, now open with a click, so the pages are shorter and easier to scan.
