# Al Capone: The Geography of Confinement

**Associated Facilities:** [Eastern State Penitentiary](#locations/eastern-state-penitentiary), Atlanta U.S. Penitentiary, [Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary](#locations/alcatraz), Terminal Island FCI, USP Lewisburg  

---

### Overview

Al Capone went from celebrity gangster to Alcatraz inmate, and then to a man whose illness kept him confined long after prison let him go. These are the places along the way.

---

### Confinement Timeline & Sites

#### 1. The Open-Air Sanctuary: Hot Springs, Arkansas
*   **Site:** The Arlington Hotel (Room 311) & The Gangster Museum of America.
*   **Confinement Phase:** Prior to his federal tax evasion conviction, Hot Springs functioned as an open-air sanctuary and secondary headquarters where Capone sought medical treatment and evaded legal pressure.

#### 2. State Solitary Architecture: [Eastern State Penitentiary](#locations/eastern-state-penitentiary) (Philadelphia, PA)
*   **Confinement Phase:** Incarcerated in 1929–1930 on concealed weapons charges, highlighting the stark contrast between his privileged cell furnishings and the facility's foundational philosophy of strict solitary penance.

#### 3. Federal Custody & Urban Lore: Atlanta, Georgia
*   **Site:** U.S. Penitentiary Atlanta & Ponce de Leon Avenue (The Briarcliff Summit, The Clermont Hotel, The Georgian Terrace).
*   **Confinement Phase:** Sentenced to federal prison in Atlanta in 1932 before being transferred to Alcatraz. Local Atlanta urban lore generated extensive rumors of secret hideouts along Ponce de Leon Avenue, though historical records confirm his only actual residence in Atlanta was his federal prison cell.

#### 4. Maximum-Security Isolation: [Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary](#locations/alcatraz) (San Francisco, CA)
*   **Confinement Phase:** Transferred to Alcatraz in 1934 to sever his external communications and influence. It was during his years on the island that untreated neurosyphilis progressively deteriorated his cognitive functioning.

#### 5. The Medical Hand-Off: Terminal Island FCI (San Pedro, CA)
*   **Confinement Phase:** In early 1939, as neurosyphilis severely damaged his brain, federal authorities transferred Capone out of Alcatraz to Terminal Island (an active federal facility on a man-made island in Los Angeles) for the final months of his sentence—marking the point where the state recognized his mind was too deteriorated for maximum-security confinement.

#### 6. Trapped in His Own Body: The Palm Island Estate (Miami, FL)
*   **Confinement Phase:** Following his release in late 1939, Capone spent his remaining years sequestered at his private Miami estate with his cognitive capacity regressed to that of a 12-year-old child. Having outlasted state incarceration, his own biology became his final confinement.
