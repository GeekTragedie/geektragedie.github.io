# Native American Boarding School Site (Chamberlain)

**Era / Category:** III. State Confinement & Memorialization  
**Type:** Federal/Assimilative Indigenous Boarding School Site  
**Location:** Chamberlain, SD, USA  
**Current Status:** Historical Site  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Forced Assimilation & Confinement:** Part of the U.S. network of Native American boarding schools designed to forcibly separate Indigenous children from their families, languages, and cultural traditions under state-backed assimilation policies.
*   **Survivor Testimony:** Oral histories from survivors document the lived trauma, strict regimentation, and cultural suppression enforced within these residential institutions.
