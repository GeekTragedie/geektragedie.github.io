# Eastern State Penitentiary

**Era / Category:** II. Modern  
**Type:** Radial State Penitentiary  
**Location:** Philadelphia, PA, USA  
**Current Status:** Historic Site & Museum  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **The "Pennsylvania System" & Isolation:** Opened in 1829, Eastern State pioneered a strict regime of total solitary confinement, intended to induce penitence through complete isolation and silence. Sensory deprivation practices included hooding inmates whenever they were moved outside their cells, leading to severe psychological deterioration and blindness concerns.
*   **Notable Prisoners:** Held high-profile inmates including [Al Capone](#people/al-capone), whose cell was famously furnished far more comfortably than standard isolation cells.

---

### Architecture & Curation

*   **Radial / Panopticon Influence:** Pioneered the "radial" or "hub-and-spoke" architectural model, heavily influenced by Jeremy Bentham's 18th-century *Panopticon* surveillance theory, allowing guards at a central rotunda to monitor multiple cellblock corridors simultaneously.
*   **Museum Curation:** Preserved in a state of "stabilized ruin," featuring an audio tour narrated by actor Steve Buscemi.
