# Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary

**Era / Category:** II. Modern  
**Type:** Maximum-Security Federal Penitentiary & Military Prison  
**Location:** Alcatraz Island, San Francisco, CA, USA  
**Current Status:** National Park & Ecological Preserve  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Federal Penitentiary Era:** Operated as a high-profile federal prison designed to hold inmates considered the most difficult or dangerous within the federal system, including [Al Capone](#people/al-capone).
*   **Island Community:** In addition to the cellhouse and guard towers, the island simultaneously housed the families and children of the correctional officers stationed there.
*   **Indigenous Occupation & Reclamation (1969–1971):** Following its closure as a federal prison, the island was occupied by Native American protestors from tribes across the United States ("Indians of All Tribes"). The occupation demanded the return of Indigenous land and protested U.S. colonialism, the historical massacre of Native peoples, and stolen tribal territories.

---

### Preservation & Ecological Curation

*   **National Park & Bird Sanctuary:** Today, Alcatraz is preserved under the National Park Service, in significant part due to the protected seabird colonies that rely on the island for nesting and reproduction.
*   **Seasonal Conditions:** During late summer (particularly September), hatching bird eggs and nesting colonies create dense fly swarms across the island tour routes.
