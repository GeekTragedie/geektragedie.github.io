# Ancient Agora (State Prison Site)

**Era / Category:** I. Pre-Modern  
**Type:** Ancient Civic Prison / Dungeon  
**Location:** Athens, Greece  
**Current Status:** Archaeological Site & Plaque  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Historical Context:** Located within the archaeological grounds of the Ancient Agora of Athens, the foundational remains mark the civic prison (*desmoterion*) of ancient Athens, where state prisoners were held awaiting trial or execution.
*   **Site Preservation:** Marked today by a low stone ruin and an informational plaque within the Agora grounds.
