# Socrates' "Prison"

**Era / Category:** I. Pre-Modern  
**Type:** Rock-Cut Cavern / Symbolic Confinement Site  
**Location:** Filopappou Hill, Athens, Greece  
**Current Status:** Historical Monument  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Historical Context:** Carved into the rock face of Filopappou Hill near the Acropolis, these gated caverns are traditionally labeled as the prison where the philosopher Socrates was held prior to his political execution in 399 BCE.
*   **Symbolic Significance:** While archaeological consensus often places the official civic prison inside the Ancient Agora, this rock-cut structure serves as a physical monument symbolizing the story of Socrates' confinement, trial, and state-mandated execution.
