# National Museum of the History of Ukraine in the Second World War

**Era / Category:** III. State Confinement & Memorialization  
**Type:** War & State Atrocity Memorial Museum  
**Location:** Kyiv, Ukraine  
**Current Status:** National Memorial Complex  

---

### Overview & Architectural Setting

*   **Monumental Approach:** Located directly beneath the towering Ukrainian Motherland Monument, the museum complex is approached via a full tunnel lined with relief imagery setting a solemn architectural tone before entry.

---

### Key Exhibits & Memorialization

*   **Wartime Propaganda & Mobilization:** Includes iconic wartime recruitment imagery such as the "We Want You" woman poster.
*   **Documentation of Mass Graves:** Archival video footage confronts visitors with the reality of concentration camp liberation and atrocities, including bulldozers pushing bodies into mass graves.
*   **The Room of Cups (Hall of Memory):** A deeply affecting memorial space featuring cups placed on tables to represent empty chairs at empty tables, walls lined with photographs of the fallen, and musical instruments suspended from the ceiling—creating an emotional weight that parallels and intensifies the experience of the Room of Shoes at the USHMM.
