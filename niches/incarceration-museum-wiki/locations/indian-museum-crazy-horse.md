# The Indian Museum of North America at Crazy Horse

**Era / Category:** III. State Confinement & Memorialization  
**Type:** Indigenous Cultural Reclamation Museum & University  
**Location:** Custer, SD, USA  
**Current Status:** Museum, Memorial & University Campus  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Cultural Reclamation & Resistance:** Situated in the Black Hills (*Paha Sapa*), the museum and monument complex documents the regional history of Indigenous resistance against U.S. military confinement, treaty violations, and reservation encroachment.
*   **Educational Evolution:** Beyond historical preservation, the site has expanded to include the Indian University of North America, transforming a space of memorialization into an active center for Indigenous higher education and cultural continuity.
