# Kilmainham Gaol

**Era / Category:** II. Modern  
**Type:** County & Political Prison  
**Location:** Dublin, Ireland  
**Current Status:** Museum  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Irish Revolution:** Played a central role in Irish history as the detention and execution site for leaders of the 1916 Easter Rising and participants in the Irish Republican movement.
*   **Women's Confinement:** Notable history surrounding the women imprisoned at Kilmainham during the nationalist and revolutionary struggles *(additional research notes pending)*.
*   **Cultural & Cinematic Footprint:** The striking Victorian panoptic wing and stone corridors have been widely utilized for cinematography in historical films.
