# United States Holocaust Memorial Museum

**Era / Category:** III. State Confinement & Memorialization  
**Type:** Genocide & State Confinement Memorial Museum  
**Location:** Washington, DC, USA  
**Current Status:** National Memorial Museum  

---

### Overview & Curation

*   **Identification Card Journey:** Visitors receive an identification booklet upon entry that follows the real-life biography and fate of an individual targeted and confined in Nazi concentration camps during the Holocaust.
*   **The Room of Shoes:** One of the museum's best-known exhibits, displaying thousands of confiscated leather shoes taken from prisoners deported to Majdanek and other extermination and concentration camps, materializing the scale of mass murder through personal remnants.
