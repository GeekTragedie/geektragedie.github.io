# Burial Relocation Site (Snohomish)

**Era / Category:** III. State Confinement & Memorialization  
**Type:** Colonial Displacement & Burial Removal Marker  
**Location:** Snohomish, WA, USA  
**Current Status:** Barely Marked Historical Site  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Colonial Removal of Indigenous Burials:** A minimally marked site in Snohomish acknowledging the forced removal and displacement of Native American burial grounds to clear land for settler colonization and municipal development.
*   **Ongoing Physical Legacy:** Because development was built directly over traditional lands and waterways, ancestral remains have historically surfaced downriver and beneath constructed areas, highlighting the erasure and physical displacement of Indigenous communities even in death.
