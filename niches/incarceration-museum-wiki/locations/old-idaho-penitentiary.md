# Old Idaho Penitentiary

**Era / Category:** II. Modern  
**Type:** Territorial & State Penitentiary  
**Location:** Boise, ID, USA  
**Current Status:** State Historic Site & Museum  

---

### Overview & Historical Background

*   **Prisoner-Built Architecture:** Operating from 1872 to 1973, inmates were forced to quarry the sandstone from the nearby ridges and construct the prison buildings and walls that confined them.
*   **Populations Confined:** Housed adult male inmates, a dedicated teenage cellblock, and a separate Women's Ward built outside the main sandstone perimeter walls.

---

### Facility Directory

*   **"Siberia" (Solitary Confinement):** Extreme isolation cells used to punish inmates under harsh temperature and sensory conditions.
*   **The Dungeon Cell & Overcrowding:** Punitive confinement areas where up to five prisoners were locked into a single cramped room as punishment.
*   **Women's Ward:** Located just outside the main prison walls, isolating female inmates in a smaller enclosed compound.
