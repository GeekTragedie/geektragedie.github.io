# Celina Spookyboo

**Type:** YouTube Channel / Independent Investigator  
**Status:** Active  

---

### Background
Celina Myers, known online as Celina Spookyboo, is a massive presence in the paranormal YouTube community. Author of "The Haunted Estate," she frequently investigates locations solo, with her husband, or alongside other major creators. She is particularly known for her sensitivity to spirits and her sleepwalking adventures.

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Empathetic, spiritual, and deeply intuitive. She often tries to connect with the emotional residual energy of a location rather than just provoking for a response.
* **Key Equipment:** Dowsing rods, Spirit Boxes, Estes Method, and traditional audio recorders.
