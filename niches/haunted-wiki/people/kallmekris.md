# KallMeKris

**Type:** YouTube Channel / Independent Investigator  
**Status:** Active  

---

### Background
Kris Collins (KallMeKris) gained massive popularity for her comedic sketches, but her pivot into paranormal content alongside friends like Celina Spookyboo and Sam & Colby has brought a new dimension to her channel. She often acts as the skeptical but easily terrified counterpart in investigations.

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Cautious, inquisitive, and highly reactive. She balances fear with a desire to uncover the truth of a location, often pushing her own boundaries to communicate with spirits.
* **Key Equipment:** Spirit Boxes, REM Pods, EMF Detectors, and Flashlights.
