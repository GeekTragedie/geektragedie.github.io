# Project Fear

**Type:** YouTube Channel  
**Status:** Active  

---

### Background
Born from the ashes of the Travel Channel show "Destination Fear," Project Fear is an independent paranormal channel run by Dakota Laden, Chelsea Laden, Tanner Wiseman, and Alex Schroeder. They fund their road trips via Kickstarter and are famous for sleeping alone in the most active hotspots of the locations they visit.

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Fear-testing and isolation. Their primary tactic is to separate and sleep completely alone in terrifying areas to see what happens when they are vulnerable.
* **Key Equipment:** Sleep cameras, Spirit Boxes, REM Pods, and Ovilus.
