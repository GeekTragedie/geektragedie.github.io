# Can't Sleep

**Type:** YouTube Channel / Podcast  
**Status:** Active  

---

### Background
Can't Sleep is a collaborative paranormal project spearheaded by creators Kris Collins (KallMeKris) and Celina Myers (Celina Spookyboo). Blending humor, friendship, and genuine terror, they explore haunted locations while leaning into their natural chemistry and shared curiosity about the unknown.

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Highly emotional, friendship-driven, and often comedic, but deeply respectful of the spirits. They tend to focus on feeling the energy of a room and communicating directly.
* **Key Equipment:** Spirit Boxes, REM Pods, EMF Detectors, and occasionally the Estes Method.
