# Ghost Adventures

**Type:** TV Show  
**Status:** Active  

---

### Background
One of the longest-running and most influential paranormal television shows in history. Led by Zak Bagans alongside Aaron Goodwin, Billy Tolley, and Jay Wasley, the crew is famous for being locked down inside locations from dusk until dawn. 

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Aggressive, provocative, and highly scientific. They frequently use trigger objects and confrontational tactics to elicit responses from darker entities.
* **Key Equipment:** Digital audio recorders, SLS cameras, REM pods, Ovilus, and constantly evolving custom-built tech.
