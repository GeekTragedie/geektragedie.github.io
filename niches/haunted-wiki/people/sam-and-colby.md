# Sam & Colby

**Type:** YouTube Channel  
**Status:** Active  

---

### Background
Sam Golbach and Colby Brock are the biggest paranormal creators on YouTube. Originally starting as urban explorers, they transitioned into high-production ghost hunting. They are known for their cinematic "Hell Week" series and for bringing other major influencers into the paranormal world.

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Respectful but highly inquisitive. They heavily popularized the "Estes Method" and focus heavily on establishing intelligent communication rather than just finding jump scares.
* **Key Equipment:** The Estes Method (Spirit box + noise-canceling headphones), REM Pods, Flux Responses, and Cat Balls.
