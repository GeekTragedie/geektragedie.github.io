**Type:** YouTube Channel  
**Status:** Active

### Background
Garrett Watts is a YouTube creator known for his eccentric, highly entertaining, and chaotic approach to content. When he delves into the paranormal, he brings a mix of genuine fascination, sheer terror, and lighthearted humor, often dragging his friends into deeply unsettling situations.

### Investigation Style & Equipment
* **Methodology:** Curious, chaotic, and heavily reactive. He relies heavily on the atmosphere and his own intense emotional reactions to the environment.
* **Key Equipment:** Handheld cameras, EMF meters, and Spirit Boxes.
