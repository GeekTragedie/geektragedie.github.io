# Madison Seminary

**Type:** School / Hospital  
**Location:** Lake County, OH  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Madison Seminary is a historic building that has served many purposes over the years, leading to a complex and highly active paranormal environment. Throughout its history, the building has operated as a private school, a widow/veteran center, a mental hospital, and a police location. There has also been significant "drama" surrounding the location, including accusations of the owners "keeping" demons in the walls based on specific symbols found on the property.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (frequently reported); Past Rituals (specific symbols found on the property, tied to accusations of demons "kept" in the walls); Other (poltergeist activity; entities described as "anti-men"; attachments often stay at the location rather than following visitors home)
