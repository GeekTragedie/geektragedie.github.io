# SK Pierce Mansion

**Type:** Home  
**Location:** Gardner, MA, USA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Layout & Hotspots:** The furnace (site of an unsolved murder).
*   **Historical Background:** The original owner was SK Pierce, who is believed to haunt the location. There is also an unsolved murder of a 16-year-old in the furnace.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (shadow figures are seen); Felt Touch (visitors being grabbed); Other (severe poltergeist activity)
*   **Known Entities:** It is believed there are 17 distinct spirits in total, including non-human entities and SK Pierce himself.
