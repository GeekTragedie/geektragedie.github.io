# Preston Castle

**Type:** Home / Juvenile home  
**Location:** Ione, CA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 26 recorded deaths and 1 murder
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** The infirmary is considered a major hotspot.
*   **Historical Background:** Originally a school for troubled boys, officially the "Preston School of Industry," this facility was part juvenile detention center and part abandoned infrastructure. There are 26 recorded deaths and 1 murder associated with the site, including the head housekeeper who was beaten to death by an unknown assailant. There are also rumors of 2 individuals being buried alive. Unclaimed wards of the state were buried in a graveyard outside.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (shadow figures and both residual and intelligent haunts); Physical Mark (visitors frequently report being scratched); EVPs / Disembodied Voices (unexplained voices)
