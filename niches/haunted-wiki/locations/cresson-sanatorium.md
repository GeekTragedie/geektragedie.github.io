# Cresson Sanatorium & Prison

**Type:** Hospital / Prison  
**Location:** Lilly, PA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Over 40,000 deaths have been recorded on the property.
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Activity is particularly high in the Children's Ward, Block J, and the Grace Chapel.
*   **Historical Background:** Cresson Sanatorium is a sprawling, multi-use facility with a dark, layered history and an incredibly high volume of reported deaths. It is known for highly interactive and sometimes dangerous entities. The facility began as a TB hospital before transitioning into an asylum, and finally a prison.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (shadow figures are common); EVPs / Disembodied Voices (disembodied voices are frequently heard); Noises (unexplained noises; a bell can sometimes be heard ringing); Mimics / Doppelgangers (a "mimic" entity is reported to copy the voices of the living); Felt Touch (physical interactions are reported); Other (unexplained breezes; first-time visitors are warned to beware)
*   **Known Entities:** A "serial killer ghost"; a mimic entity.
