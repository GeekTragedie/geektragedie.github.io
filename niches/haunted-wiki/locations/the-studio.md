### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Unknown <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Main Floor (salon/studio space, front door, and back area); Basement <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Located in a 121-year-old building constructed in 1904 (consisting of historic buildings that were later connected together), The Studio is a hair, makeup, and nail salon owned by Garrett Watts' sister, Marianne Watts <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices; Noises; Moving Objects (a makeup cart was found fully moved to the Basement; objects fall over on their own; a balloon floated out from the back on its own); Electronic Interference (lights change and music changes on its own) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Unknown spirits attached to the 1904 building <sup>1</sup>
