### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 3+ (Edward B. Kreischer in 1894, a German cook who hung himself in a closet, and Robert McKelvey in 2005) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Parlor Room; Doll Room; Attic; Balcony; Grounds; Basement; Bar Room; Green Room; Seance Room; Floor 2 <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built in the 1880s by German immigrant Balthasar Kreischer—who founded the Kreischer Brick Manufactory after a massive 1835 New York fire created huge demand for firebrick—the Kreischer Mansion was originally one of twin mansions built for his two sons, Charles and Edward B. Kreischer (who had 7 kids), while a third brother, George, didn't get a house; Charles's mansion later burnt down, and the surviving mansion is Edward's <sup>1</sup>. Over 100 years ago, on June 8, 1894, Edward Kreischer was found dead with a gunshot wound to the head between the brick factory and the house in the forest—an unsolved mystery/murder <sup>1</sup>. At the time, George had been working with Edward to kick Charles out of the family company, and Edward's wife, Freda (Frida), was rumored to be having an affair with the local doctor—the very same doctor who pronounced Edward dead and whom the widow married 9 months later <sup>1</sup>. Charles subsequently held seances in the Parlor Room to try to reach Edward, which reportedly created a portal <sup>1</sup>. Over a century later, in April 2005, the mansion (which had also operated as a mob restaurant) was the site of a brutal mafia murder: Robert ("Rob") McKelvey owed money to the Bonanno crime family, and caretaker/hitman Joseph "Joe Black" Young killed him (drowning him in the reflecting pool, dismembering him, and stuffing his body in pieces into the Basement furnace) <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Basement); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Grounds, Basement; "The Hairy Man" on the Grounds; Freda's ghost residing on Floor 2); Portals / Hellmouths (Seance Room; created when Charles performed seances to reach Edward); Past Rituals (seances in the Parlor Room); Electronic Interference (Basement) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Edward B. Kreischer, Freda (Frida) Kreischer (resides on Floor 2), Henry (a German cook who died in the closet), "The Hairy Man" (on the Grounds), and Robert McKelvey <sup>1</sup>
