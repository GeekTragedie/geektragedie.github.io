# The Olde Angel Inn

**Type:** Lodging <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Niagara-on-the-Lake (Niagara Falls), ON, Canada <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 1 (Captain Colin Swayze) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** The General's Quarters; Governor's Suite; Captain's Room; Colonel's Room; Sweetheart's Room; Cellar; Cellar Bathroom <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** One of Ontario's oldest pubs and hotels, The Olde Angel Inn was rebuilt around 1815 after a massive fire burnt the surrounding town down during the War of 1812 <sup>1</sup>. Historically, the inn was owned by Dr. John Ross, and seances have been held on the property <sup>1</sup>. During the War of 1812, Canadian militia officer Captain Colin Swayze stayed behind to see his love (in the Captain's Room) and hid from invading American soldiers in a dark, scary hole (barrel) in the Cellar, where he was discovered and bayoneted to death <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Cellar Bathroom); Noises (Cellar; footsteps, noises, and door sounds associated with Captain Swayze); Moving Objects (The General's Quarters; shoes are tidied up when left in a mess) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Captain Colin Swayze, the American soldier who murdered Captain Swayze, Captain Swayze's lover (associated with the Captain's Room), and a very nice little girl spirit named Clementine <sup>1</sup>
