# Fort Henry

**Type:** Military  
**Location:** Kingston, ON  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** Fort Henry is a historic military fort known for its dark history of executions and intense paranormal activity. The fort has a history of hangings. It was also attacked by American forces during its active years.
*   **Reported Lore:** Moving Objects (unexplained poltergeist activity is frequently reported by visitors); Other (investigators and re-enactors frequently report firearm malfunctions while on the premises)
