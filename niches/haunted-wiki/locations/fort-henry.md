# Fort Henry

**Type:** Military <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Kingston, ON, Canada <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 5 + 37+ <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Officers' Guard Room; Holding Room; Tunnel (Reverse Firing Chambers); School Room; Bakery; Cellar <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Fort Henry is a 19th-century British military fortress tied to the 1837 Rebellion <sup>1</sup>. On December 8, 1838, Nils von Schoultz and members of the Hunters' Lodge were captured and hung here <sup>1</sup>. Another hanging at the fort—that of Martin Woodruff—infamously took 3 drops to complete <sup>1</sup>. Several accidental military deaths also occurred on the grounds: a soldier named Marsh died accidentally after shooting himself in the head while reloading too soon, and Gunner Smith was sent flying with a cannon and died while reloading <sup>1</sup>. Outside on the hill, people frequently see a structure that looks like it is being built and ask what it is, even though nothing is there—on the exact spot that used to be the gallows <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Officers' Guard Room); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Officers' Guard Room; phantom gallows structure seen on the hill); Noises (Officers' Guard Room; spirits in the Officers' Guard Room were once so loud that security guards called the police, and while searching, more loud glass breaking was heard and police pulled their guns); Moving Objects (Holding Room, School Room, Bakery); Electronic Interference <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** "Mary Ann" (a young girl spirit in the Bakery and School Room), Nils von Schoultz, Martin Woodruff, Marsh, and Gunner Smith <sup>1</sup>
