# The Perkins House

**Type:** Home <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Colfax, WA, USA <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** ~1 (Mr. Perkins' daughter may have died in the home) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Level 1: Sitting Room (containing Mr. Perkins' chair), Kitchen, Living Room, and Dining Room; Stairs (First Stair Landing); Upstairs: Bedroom and Doll Room <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built in 1886 by Colfax town founder and sawmill pioneer James A. Perkins, The Perkins House is a preserved historic Victorian home <sup>1</sup>. According to local lore, Mr. Perkins' daughter may have died in the home (possibly on a bed upstairs), and Mr. Perkins' original chair still sits in the Level 1 Sitting Room <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions; Noises (piano playing; footsteps of someone who walks down to the first stair landing and back upstairs) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Mr. Perkins' daughter (associated with the bed upstairs) and an entity who walks down the stairs to the first landing and back upstairs <sup>1</sup>
