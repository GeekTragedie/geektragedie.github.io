# Critter's Lane

**Type:** Road <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Woodstock, ON, Canada <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Unknown (Timothy Topping's wife and 2 children, plus Topping himself, who was murdered in prison) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** The Critter's Lane roadway and the surrounding woods <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Critter's Lane is an outdoor public roadway with a dark past, known for producing a vast and varied array of paranormal experiences for those who visit. In 1873, farmer Timothy Topping killed his wife and 2 children; he was subsequently murdered in prison <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices; Shadow Figures / Apparitions (50 years of stories: "The Man with the Lantern," women in white, children); Moving Objects (a second-hand story of a stuck car that felt pushed); Physical Mark (cuts all over the body; a child's handprint appearing on the back of a pushed car); Other (a never-ending loop while driving) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** "The Man with the Lantern," women in white, and children <sup>1</sup>
