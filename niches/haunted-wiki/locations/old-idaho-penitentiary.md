# Old Idaho Penitentiary

**Type:** Penitentiary <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Location:** Boise, ID, USA <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** ~129 total deaths (including 10 state executions, 1 suicide in Siberia, 1 murder in the Shower Room, and 119 deaths from disease, murder, and suicide) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Rose Garden (site of the previous gallows); Siberia (solitary confinement); Maximum Security (5 House, containing Death Row and the Gallows); Shower Room; Cell House 5; The Hole / Cooler; Cell House 3; Cell House 4; Drop Room; Women's Ward <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Housing over 13,000 male and female convicts (who originally were held together and performed most of the construction on the site), the Old Idaho Penitentiary was the site of 10 state executions and approximately 129 total deaths from disease, murder, and suicide <sup>1, 2</sup>. Inmates would make weapons out of anything and everything <sup>1</sup>. "Siberia" served as solitary confinement (with one small hole in the ceiling), where prisoners were locked away for 30 to 60 days—and some for over a year; several inmates went insane there, and one died by suicide <sup>1, 2</sup>. "The Cooler" (The Hole)—created after an escape led to insubordination—held 6 to 8 men (and up to 40 people) in a single dark cell <sup>1, 2</sup>. In the Prison Shower Room, an inmate was gang-raped to death <sup>1</sup>. In 1937, inmate Douglas Van Vlack—convicted of murdering his ex-wife and two police officers—was scheduled to hang, but after his mother visited and whispered in his ear, he jumped to his death from the top tier of Cell House 4 <sup>2</sup>. In October 1957, Raymond Snowden ("Idaho's Jack the Ripper")—who stabbed Cora Dean 35 times with a pocket knife after she rejected him and started to eat her—was executed at the Gallows in Maximum Security (Cell House 5); his neck failed to break, and it took him an abnormal 15 to 16 minutes to die by strangulation, which ended executions at the prison <sup>1, 2</sup>. Prior to the Cell House 5 gallows, executions were held outdoors in the Rose Garden in front of local audiences <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Maximum Security, Drop Room; male voices and a woman screaming); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Siberia, Women's Ward, Cell House 4); Noises (Maximum Security; unexplained banging); Temperature Spots (Siberia); Moving Objects (Maximum Security, Cell House 4; doors slamming on their own and doors locking/closing); Orbs (Rose Garden); Felt Touch (Maximum Security, Drop Room; pushes, hair touching, and hair pulling); Physical Mark (Drop Room); Physiological Symptom (Rose Garden); Electronic Interference (Maximum Security; breakers being switched) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Raymond Snowden, Douglas Van Vlack, and inmates who died in Siberia, the Shower Room, and the Gallows <sup>1, 2</sup>

**Ghost Adventures** - *"Old Idaho Penitentiary"*

*   **Investigators:** Zak Bagans, Nick Groff, and Aaron Goodwin
*   **Tour Guide:** Ellen Haffner
*   **Date Watched:** October 28, 2026
*   **Equipment Used:** Motion Detector (Cell House 3); Digital Recorder (EVP) (Shower Room, Siberia, The Cooler); Other (infrared camera photos in Maximum Security / Gallows; camera and photographs) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Methods Used:** Provocation / Taunting (Siberia, Gallows, Rose Garden); Other (ITC / Instrumental Transcommunication) <sup>1</sup>

#### Observed Evidence & Phenomena

*   **EVPs / Disembodied Voice:** Captured in Siberia (1x; an EVP asking *"Say what?"*) and The Cooler (unknown EVP caught; an EVP expert later identified *"Not my life..."* and *"I'm okay"*) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Shadow Figure / Apparition:** Caught in the Shower Room (1x) and Maximum Security / Gallows (1x; a shadow mist was caught on camera across 2 wide infrared camera angles) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Noise:** Heard in the Shower Room (1x) and Siberia (3x; unexplained banging) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Physiological Symptom:** Experienced at the Gallows and in the Rose Garden <sup>1</sup>
*   **Felt Touch:** In the Shower Room, an investigator felt a water bottle get pulled out of their back pocket <sup>1</sup>
*   **Other:** An ITC holographic image captured the image of a man wearing a hood <sup>1</sup>
*   **Evidence Findings:** <sup>1</sup>
    *   *Cell House 5 & The Cooler:* Cell House 5 yielded 0 hits. In The Cooler, a possible door closure occurred, and an unknown EVP was caught (analyzed by an EVP expert as *"Not my life..."* and *"I'm okay"*).
    *   *Siberia:* An EVP saying *"Say what?"* was captured alongside unexplained banging (3x).
    *   *Maximum Security & Gallows:* Using 2 wide camera angles with infrared camera photos, the crew caught a shadow mist on camera, and ITC captured the holographic image of a man wearing a hood.
    *   *Shower Room:* A shadow figure was seen, and an investigator felt their water bottle get pulled out of their back pocket (the digital recorder yielded 0 hits).
*   **Notes:** Peer review of the ITC and audio evidence confirmed the holographic image, shadow image, and Cooler EVPs <sup>1</sup>
