# Old Idaho Penitentiary

**Type:** Penitentiary  
**Location:** Boise, ID  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 129 recorded deaths, including 10 executions.
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Hangings took place both inside the facility and outside at the rose garden.
*   **Historical Background:** The Old Idaho Penitentiary is a historic prison known for its harsh conditions, numerous executions, and highly active paranormal phenomena. The prison has 129 recorded deaths and was the site of 10 executions. Hangings took place both inside the facility and outside at the rose garden, where visitors used to watch. The prison famously had a resident cat named Dennis. Raymond Snowden is one of the most notorious inmates associated with the location.
*   **Reported Lore:** Orbs (light anomalies and orbs are frequently captured); Felt Touch (visitors often report being physically shoved)
