# Farrar Elementary School

**Type:** School[cite: 24]  
**Location:** Maxwell, IA[cite: 24]  

---

### Overview
Farrar Elementary is an abandoned three-story school, now owned by paranormal investigators Sam and Colby. The building and its grounds, which sit across the street from a cemetery, are host to a highly diverse and potentially dangerous collection of spirits[cite: 24].

### History
* **Built:** Opened in 1922[cite: 24]
* **Original Purpose:** School and town event center[cite: 24]
* **Notable Events:** Aside from serving as an educational facility, the building was used for various town events. The location's darker history includes reports of previous child abuse[cite: 24]. 

### Reported Activity
* **Apparitions:** There are many different spirits inside and outside the school. Specific entities include a Principal, Janitor, and Librarian. Shadow figures and doppelgangers have been reported, specifically in the boiler room[cite: 24].
* **Poltergeist Activity:** Severe poltergeist activity and a dangerous "portal" are reported. There is also potential for possession. Investigators theorize there may be one powerful spirit pretending to be whatever the visitors want to see[cite: 24].
