# Reel Treats

**Type:** Shop <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Woodstock, ON, Canada <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Unknown <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Main Storefront with Elevator (Main Elevator); Basement / Downstairs (Storage) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Originally one of the first buildings funded by the Eden family in the 1800s, the Reel Treats candy store building features a haunted 1800s elevator <sup>1</sup>. During demolition and construction renovations, workers uncovered the old elevator shaft buried in the ground <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (seen during demolition and construction); Noises (voices heard coming from the elevator area); Moving Objects (during demolition and construction, things were falling over on their own) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Spirits connected to the 1800s elevator shaft and possible past brothel activity (John, David, Hannah, Kristina, and Sarah) <sup>1</sup>
