# The Comedy Store

**Type:** Club  
**Location:** Hollywood, CA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Comedy Store is a world-famous comedy club on the Sunset Strip that hides a much darker, mob-connected past behind its laughter-filled walls. Originally a nightclub / mob hangout, the building was a known Mafia location before becoming a comedy club. It harbors a dark history as a site where illegal abortions were conducted by mob associates.
