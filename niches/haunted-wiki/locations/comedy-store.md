# The Comedy Store

**Type:** Club <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Location:** Hollywood, CA, USA <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 3–4 (2 abortions, 1 comedian suicide next door, and 1 dismemberment/murder) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Top Floor / Upstairs (current offices and former mob boss office); Stairwell; Sound Booth; Crawl Space (leads to the Sound Booth); Main Floor: Mitzi Shore's Office, Performance Space, Main Hall, Belly Room, Main Room, and Richard Pryor Green Room; Basement (current podcast studio) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Founded on April 7, 1972, by Sammy and Mitzi Shore alongside Rudy DeLuca, The Comedy Store is a legendary venue where famous comedians perform <sup>1</sup>. Before becoming a comedy club, the building was the 1930s and early 1940s Sunset Strip nightclub "Ciro's," serving as a major mob hangout for Mickey Cohen and Bugsy Siegel <sup>1</sup>. During the mob era, the building was the site of gambling, murder, illegal abortions (in the Basement), bootlegging, bombings, partying, and torture, with rumors that victims are buried under the building <sup>1, 2</sup>. The Mob used the Basement to force people out, and the Upstairs and Sound Booth served as a mob boss's office; there are still holes throughout the club that hid guns or drugs <sup>2</sup>. In the Basement, a man named Helen performed work and abortions for the mob; Helen was rumored to have killed a woman, upset the boss, and gotten killed <sup>2</sup>. Someone was also reportedly dismembered at the top of the Stairwell <sup>2</sup>. Later, a comedian passed away by suicide next door by jumping off the building and trying to land in The Comedy Store parking lot <sup>1, 2</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Main Room; "Gus" EVP); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Main Room, Upstairs; full-body, angry apparitions of mob assassins Anthony and Gus; the ghost of Sam Kinison levitating in the Main Room and Belly Room; "Steve" seen in the club); Noises (Top Floor, Main Hall; knocking on the Main Hall door); Moving Objects (Richard Pryor Green Room, Basement, Stairwell; the Green Room door opening by itself; the Stairwell door opening and closing by itself); Orbs (Basement); Past Rituals (Mitzi Shore's Office; Kyra Gardner had her office exorcised each year and twice total had exorcisms performed) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Jeff Scott (a piano player), Brody Stevens (a comedian who researched the ghosts at The Comedy Store), Sam Kinison, Anthony and Gus (mob assassins), Steve, Helen (performed mob abortions in the Basement), and "Sharon" (a nurse who died in an abortion in the Basement) <sup>1, 2</sup>
