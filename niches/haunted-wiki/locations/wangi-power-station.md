# Wangi Power Station

**Type:** Factory  
**Location:** Lake Macquarie, New South Wales, Australia  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Wangi Power Station is an abandoned and highly dangerous industrial facility known for intense EMF spikes and dark historical events. During its operational years, various accidents were reported. Tragically, a 3-year-old was murdered and their body was found on the premises. In another incident, a skeleton was found inside the facility 7 weeks later while the plant was still running. The abandoned site is currently considered dangerous due to rusting stairs, still water containing chemical bacteria, and use by squatters and drug users.
*   **Reported Lore:** Electronic Interference (investigators note that the exceptionally high EMF output of the facility seems to correlate directly with an increase in paranormal experiences)
