# One of a Kind Antique Mall

**Type:** Shop <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Location:** Woodstock, ON, Canada <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Unknown <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Elevator; Floor 1; Floor 2 (windows, wood section, doll section, and colored glass); Floor 3 (window space / office space, wood section, and immediate right corner) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Spanning 80,000 square feet across 3 floors, the One of a Kind Antique Mall is Canada's largest antique mall <sup>1</sup>. Originally opened in 1906 as a garment factory—the Oxford Knitting Company, associated with Kenneth W. Harvey—the facility manufactured undergarments ("undies") until it closed down in 1989 <sup>1</sup>. Prior to this two-part visit, a paranormal investigation had never been conducted at the location <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices; Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Floor 3; people have had full conversations with 2 different people only for them to vanish); Noises (Floor 2, Floor 3); Moving Objects (Floor 3, 1x); Felt Touch (Floor 2) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Two vanishing figures who hold full conversations with visitors before disappearing, spirits associated with the factory and antique items, and entities on Floor 2 and Floor 3 <sup>1, 2</sup>
