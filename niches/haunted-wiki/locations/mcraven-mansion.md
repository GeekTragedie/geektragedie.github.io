# McRaven Mansion

**Type:** Home  
**Location:** Vicksburg, MS  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The grounds have a dark history, having been used as war grounds, a field army hospital, and burial grounds. It is also tied to an individual named Andrew Glass, an alleged hide-away and murderer.
*   **Reported Lore:** Moving Objects (general poltergeist activity, including a moving doll); Noises (unexplained noises are frequently heard)
