### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 10,000+ Native American people on the land + at least 4 bodies found by the pond (2 children and 2 adults) + 1 boy killed by a shotgun + 1+ murder victims of the brothers <sup>1, 3</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Living Room (and Beth's old room, exorcism space); Beth's (Betsy's) Room (1st floor); Mike's Room ("Little Boy" room); 2nd Floor Mary's Room (boarded up); 2nd Floor Bedroom / Parents' Room / Laura's Room; Kitchen; Stairs; Cellar / Basement (accessed via the bathroom; contains a crawl space); Property ("Haunted Forest," the Pond, the Special Offering / Giving Tree, and Ruth's Grave) <sup>1, 2, 3, 4</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Both the 1853 house and the surrounding forest property in the "Enchanted Mountains" are heavily haunted <sup>1</sup>. Historically, the land was taken during a Native American slaughter followed by a curse, with indigenous landowners building pyramid-like spirit mounds on the property <sup>1, 4</sup>. At least 4 bodies (2 children and 2 adults) were found on the land by the Pond, along with body anomalies around the house <sup>3, 4</sup>. The house itself was built in 1853 by the homicidal Everett Brothers, who allegedly robbed and murdered stagecoach passengers, stored their bodies in the Cellar/Basement, and buried them on the land (bodies were reportedly found, though this is also described as local legend) <sup>1, 2, 3</sup>; later, an argument broke out between the two brothers and one shot and killed the other <sup>1</sup>. In another tragedy, a boy was killed in a shotgun accident in the 2nd Floor Bedroom closet <sup>1</sup>. In the early 1970s, the Dandy family—parents Clara and Phil and children Mike, Betsy (Beth), Laura, and Mary—moved in and lasted only 1.5 years due to intense haunting experiences, including bee and fly infestations, bad luck, physical attacks, pets dying, a group of strange faces looking into the windows at night (who were actually inside looking out when the family went outside), and Mary becoming possessed (requiring them to move her to another room and board up Mary's Room) <sup>1, 2, 3, 4</sup>. On April 13, 1974, an exorcism was performed by Father Alphonsus Trabold (leaving a burn on a back), which failed to clear the house, and the family slept together in the Living Room before moving out completely <sup>1, 2, 4</sup>. Neighbors also experience paranormal activity, and a researcher named Ruth who studied the house is buried on the property (at Ruth's Grave) after she passed away <sup>1, 3</sup>. At the time of filming, the property was owned by Dan Klaes <sup>2</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (2nd Floor Bedroom closet, Living Room, Beth's Room, Crawl Space; chanting and screaming in the woods/Property); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Property, Living Room, Stairs, Mike's Room, 2nd Floor Mary's Room, Crawl Space; seen by the Dandys and current owner Dan Klaes; a photo of a creature in a tree on the Property captured by Dan; a woman in white / bug-eyed woman in white who dances around the Pond and is also seen in the house; human/animal hybrids; apparitions of Native American people; a dark entity; a boy with a shotgun seen on the Property and in the 2nd Floor Bedroom closet); Noises (Living Room, 2nd Floor Mary's Room, Crawl Space, Pond; scratching in the Crawl Space); Moving Objects (Cellar, Mike's Room, Living Room, 2nd Floor Mary's Room, Crawl Space; the Cellar door is known to open on its own; broken mirror; poltergeist activity and *"Get out"* in the Crawl Space); Felt Touch (Living Room, Property, 2nd Floor Mary's Room; punches, pushes, and burns); Physical Mark (Living Room, Property; burn on the back during the 1974 exorcism; scratching); Possessions / Trances (amnesia experienced by Phil Dandy and Mary); Mimics / Doppelgangers (entities known for tricks; Paul Kenyon waved at Clara Dandy inside the house, and seconds later she came around the outside of the house having never been inside); Portals / Hellmouths (vortex in the house and Crawl Space); Past Rituals (failed 1974 exorcism; Ouija board use); Electronic Interference (phone calls and lights); Other ("demonic presence"; communication is known to go quiet when discussing or asking about the exorcism; insect infestations of flies and bees in the boarded-up room; nightmares; visitors are told to "be mysterious and the ghosts will interact with you") <sup>1, 2, 3, 4</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** A woman in white / woman with pop-out (bug) eyes, a boy killed by a shotgun, human/animal hybrids (creature in a tree), spirits of Native American people, the Everett Brothers and their victims, Ruth, and a dark/demonic entity <sup>1, 2, 3, 4</sup>
