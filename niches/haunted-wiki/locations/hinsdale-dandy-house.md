# The Hinsdale / Dandy House

**Type:** Home[cite: 7]  
**Location:** Hinsdale, NY[cite: 7]  

---

### Overview
The Hinsdale House (also known as the Dandy House) is a notorious residential haunting involving a dark history and intense paranormal activity. It is famous for an exorcism that was performed on the property after a family of five was tormented by phenomena[cite: 7].

### History
* **Built:** 1853 (built by brothers who were murderers)[cite: 7]
* **Original Purpose:** Private residence[cite: 7]
* **Notable Events:** The property sits on the site of a 10,000 Native American massacre. The home has a tragic history, including a son who was killed in a saw blade incident. Four people (two adults, two children) are reportedly buried in the back, with an undiscovered burial mound. The property also features a "hanging" giving tree[cite: 7].

### Reported Activity
* **Apparitions:** Shadow figures have been reported, as well as claims of a "stack of bodies in the basement"[cite: 7].
* **Poltergeist Activity:** Severe poltergeist activity led to a previous exorcism at the home[cite: 7].
* **EVP/Audio:** Disembodied voices are frequently captured[cite: 7].
