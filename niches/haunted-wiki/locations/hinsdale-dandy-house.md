# The Hinsdale / Dandy House

**Type:** Home  
**Location:** Hinsdale, NY  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Hinsdale House (also known as the Dandy House) is a notorious residential haunting involving a dark history and intense paranormal activity. It is famous for an exorcism that was performed on the property after a family of five was tormented by phenomena. Built in 1853 by brothers who were murderers, the property sits on the site of a 10,000 Native American massacre. The home has a tragic history, including a son who was killed in a saw blade incident. Four people (two adults, two children) are reportedly buried in the back, with an undiscovered burial mound. The property also features a "hanging" giving tree.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (shadow figures, as well as claims of a "stack of bodies in the basement"); Moving Objects (severe poltergeist activity led to a previous exorcism at the home); EVPs / Disembodied Voices (frequently captured)
