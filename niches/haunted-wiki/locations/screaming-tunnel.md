# The Screaming Tunnel

**Type:** Tunnel <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Niagara Falls, ON, Canada <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 1 (a young girl who died in a fire) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** The interior of the limestone tunnel and the area immediately outside the tunnel entrance <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built in the early 1900s beneath the railway tracks in Niagara Falls as a limestone drainage passage and footpath, The Screaming Tunnel is tied to the legend of a young girl who died in a fire inside the tunnel <sup>1</sup>. Multiple variations of her story exist—ranging from a farm girl who ran into the tunnel after her nearby farmhouse caught fire, to a more sinister account that she was set on fire by her own father (or another attacker) inside the tunnel <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (a girl screaming after a match is blown out); Other (if you light a match inside the tunnel at midnight, the flame will be blown out, followed by the sound of a girl screaming; Celina Spookyboo previously had 2 matches go out here, and others also report it happening) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** A young farm girl who died in a fire <sup>1</sup>
