# Old Washoe Club

**Type:** Club <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Virginia City, NV, USA <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** ~3,100+ (up to 3,100 bodies stored in the Crypt over the winters, plus at least 77 bodies held in the Crypt during the winter of 1874, 22+ deaths inside the building, and 1 suicide on the 3rd floor) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Basement: The Crypt; Floor 1: Saloon and Spiral Staircase; Floor 2: Access Outside, Main Room, Ballroom / Billiards Hall / Law Office, and Shadow People Room; Floor 3: Red Room <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Established in 1862 as an elite "Millionaires Club," the Old Washoe Club hosted many famous and wealthy guests (including Mark Twain) and was the site of gambling, prostitution, disease, fights, and murder <sup>1</sup>. A massive fire (the Great Fire of 1875)—which local millionaire and "Washoe Seeress" Eilley Orrum Bowers predicted through scrying—wiped out much of the town, and haunted activity reportedly ramped up 100 years later <sup>1</sup>. In one historical tragedy, a butcher was hit by a carriage, carried to the doctor's office inside the building, and died (the original poker table is still on Floor 2) <sup>1</sup>. During the harsh winter of 1874, the Crypt in the basement held 77 dead bodies, and across its history as a winter morgue, an estimated 3,100 bodies were stored over the floor of the Crypt <sup>1</sup>. In 1984, a resident set a mattress on fire inside the building <sup>1</sup>. Additionally, this building famously inspired the creation of *Ghost Adventures* (after their 2004 documentary brick-throwing/apparition capture) and got the crew their show on the Travel Channel <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Floor 3, The Crypt); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Ballroom, Shadow People Room, The Crypt; many photos of shadow figures; Mark Twain's ghost seen on the street; a "Creeper" with many legs crawling on the walls and floor); Noises (The Crypt; doors slamming in the Red Room); Moving Objects (The Crypt, Floor 3, Red Room; doors slamming in the Red Room); Felt Touch (a woman pushed down the stairs); Physical Mark (Floor 3; many people have been scratched on Floor 3 by a negative, angry entity); Past Rituals (exorcism in The Crypt; seances in the Red Room) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** "The Prospector" (a drunk ghost in the Saloon), Lena / the "Lady in Blue" (associated with the Spiral Staircase), Gretchen (a little girl on the stairs and in The Crypt, connected to a possessed doll and a doll named Annabelle), Mark Twain (seen on the street), the "Creeper" (a multi-legged entity crawling on walls and floors), and demons / a negative angry spirit on Floor 3 (especially in the Red Room, the most haunted room) <sup>1</sup>
