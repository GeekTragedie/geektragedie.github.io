# Bobby Mackey's Music World

**Type:** Bar  
**Location:** Wilder, KY  
**Status:** Demolished: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** Bobby Mackey's Music World is a highly volatile paranormal location famous for demonic claims and a supposed "portal to hell." The original building has recently been demolished for renovations. Originally a slaughterhouse before becoming a bar, the location is tied to previous mobster activity and the site of the "Bloody Bucket." History includes a murdered woman and two murderers who were sentenced to death.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (frequently spotted); Physical Mark (severe scratching); Possessions / Trances (claims of possession); Portals / Hellmouths (the opening of "portals" or a "demon portal"); Other (extremely aggressive poltergeist activity)
