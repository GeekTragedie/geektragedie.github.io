# Bobby Mackey's Music World

**Type:** Bar <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Location:** Wilder, KY, USA <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: Yes (full reconstruction in progress) · Open to Public: No

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 4–5 <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Men's Room; Upstairs Apartment (Carl's Apartment); Basement (where bodies were thrown, containing a holding cell); Outside Stairs; Main Room (with a creepy corner); Main Stage; Kitchen; Catwalk; Well Room; Room of Faces; Tunnel; Stairway to Nowhere; Dressing Room; Spotlight Room <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Bobby Mackey's Music World is a highly volatile paranormal location famous for demonic claims and a supposed "portal to hell." Built on soil sitting over a fault line and a nearby river that flows north (resulting in a supposed charged magnetic field), the site was originally an 1840s whiskey distillery before being taken over by criminals during Prohibition <sup>2</sup>. A former slaughterhouse and speakeasy, the location is tied to previous mobster activity, the site of the "Bloody Bucket," multiple murders, and satanic activity <sup>1, 2</sup>. In the 1930s, it operated as the Primrose Country Club, where the owner was killed by the mob and a tunnel was used to hide bodies <sup>2</sup>; from 1947 on, it operated as "Latin Quarters" (mob), featuring a cell in the basement for those who didn't pay their debts and the Tunnel used for murder and illegal activity <sup>2</sup>. Bobby Mackey felt drawn to the building and created the music venue in 1978, eventually adding a sign to the door warning that management is not liable for the ghosts' behavior after someone claimed to be beat up by a ghost in the bathroom <sup>2</sup>. In February 1896, Pearl Bryan—who was 5 months pregnant—went to see Scott Jackson, who had teamed up with Alonzo Walling to cause a miscarriage; in a panic, she was murdered (beheaded) by Walling and Jackson, and while her headless body was found, her head was never found and was allegedly thrown into the well in the basement (which possibly served as a ritual site for satanic rituals) <sup>1, 2</sup>. Both men struggled to actually hang at their execution <sup>2</sup>. Janet Mackey refuses to talk about the location and does not go back after being pushed into a sink (where she saw blood in the sink) and thrown down the basement stairs toward the well <sup>1, 2</sup>. Caretaker Carl Lawson used to live in the Upstairs Apartment and experienced heavy hallucinations of spiders and a drowning delusion before becoming possessed, requiring a 6-hour exorcism in 1983 (recorded on tape) during which he claimed spirits "Charlie" and "Sam" had existed for 1,000 years <sup>1, 2</sup>. Donna Clutter: Johanna, a dancer formerly at Mackey's, is intertwined in the story. She fell in love with singer Robert Randall; her father (a cruel sheriff) had him killed, so she poisoned her father and killed herself in the Dressing Room, leaving a journal/poem in the Spotlight Room (Carl later wrote a song about her and found a note) <sup>1, 2</sup>. (Bobby Mackey's full name is also Robert Randall; his mom added Mackey to the end after the bar story.) <sup>1</sup> The Well Room in the basement is connected to a 14-foot drop to the Licking River. The well is called "To Hell" / "Hell's Gate" and acts as a portal/vortex, alongside a "stairway to nowhere" ("heaven") <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Main Room, Basement, Men's Room); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Main Room, Main Stage, Kitchen, Well Room, Stairway to Nowhere, Dressing Room, Basement; frequently spotted); Noises (Main Room, Kitchen, Dressing Room, Stairway to Nowhere, Catwalk); Temperature Spots (Upstairs Apartment, Room of Faces); Moving Objects (Main Stage, Kitchen, Well Room, Men's Room; the "Die Game" in the Men's Room; a record player playing without electricity; pool tables in the creepy corner); Orbs (Main Room, Main Stage); Felt Touch (Basement, Well Room, Dressing Room, Outside Stairs, Catwalk); Physical Mark (Basement, Dressing Room, Well Room; severe scratching and pushes); Possessions / Trances (Upstairs Apartment; Carl Lawson); Portals / Hellmouths (Well Room; the "Hell's Gate" well portal/vortex in the basement); Past Rituals (an exorcism in the Upstairs Apartment; satanic rituals in the Well Room and Basement); Electronic Interference (Main Room; drained batteries; video and audio interference with previous film crews; the jukebox playing *"Anniversary Waltz"* without electricity); Other (smell of roses; a fire caught in the Kitchen during the exorcism and the fire department couldn't find the source) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Approximately 40 spirits, including Pearl Bryan, Alonzo Walling, Scott Jackson, Johanna (questioned whether she is really a good spirit), the cruel Sheriff (Johanna's father), a child spirit, Charlie, Sam, and an "army walking through" (seen in the Men's Room mirror) <sup>1, 2</sup>
