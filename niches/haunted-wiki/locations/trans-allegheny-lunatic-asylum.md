# Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum

**Type:** Hospital / Mental Hospital <sup>1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6</sup>  
**Location:** Weston, WV, USA <sup>1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 20,000+ people (with over 5,500 buried onsite in a ~5-acre graveyard) <sup>1, 3, 4</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** 13 Buildings across 666 acres of land (242,000 sq. ft. main building across 4 floors); Basement: Lobotomy Recovery Room; Floor 1: Women's Ward and Lily's Room; Floor 2: Offices and Dining for Workers/Physicians, Juvenile Ward, Children's Ward, Bathroom, Double Suicide Room (2.1), and Emily's Room / Party Room (2.2); Floor 3: Ward F (Violent Men's Ward, containing Dean's Room and The Cage), Ward T (Substance Recovery), and Ward for the Criminally Insane / Criminal Hall; Floor 4: Solitary / Seclusion Wards, Violent Men's Ward, Violent Female Ward, and Ward R ("Fear Factor" Ward, the most haunted floor, containing the Party Room and Room 717); Civil War Area; Criminally Insane Building (located behind the main building, with holding cells on the higher-level floor); Onsite Graveyard <sup>1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Opening in 1864 (including a Civil War area) and operating for 130 years until it closed in 1994, the Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum was originally intended for mental health support, housing all ages and genders <sup>1, 3, 4, 5, 6</sup>. However, it suffered severe overcrowding—originally built for 250 patients, it crowded 2,300 to 2,400 patients inside (nearly 10x its expected legal capacity)—as society locked away people who needed help alongside those cast out by society, including orphans, the criminally insane, people with traumatic brain injuries (TBI), asthma, or "female hysteria," and wives abandoned by their husbands <sup>1, 3, 5, 6</sup>. Because of rampant malpractice, mistreatment, neglect, forced sterilization, cages, ice baths, waterboarding, insulin shock therapy, induced comas, "inhumane" electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) that caused broken bones, and transorbital lobotomies (largely experimented with by Dr. Walter Freeman, including lobotomies performed on 4-year-old children, after which patients would "cry tears of blood" post-lobotomy), most patients suffered severe brain damage, and thousands died from natural causes, suicide, and patient-on-patient murder <sup>1, 3, 4, 6</sup>. Video evidence of conditions was even shown <sup>1</sup>. On Floor 2, two teenage patients were found dead hanging by sheets in a double suicide pact, and a possible sexual advance was followed by an attack and death in the Floor 2 Hall/Bathroom <sup>3, 4</sup>. On Floor 3 in Ward F (the Violent Men's Ward, which had hooks on the walls for hands and feet to prevent hanging), patient Dean Metheny—who was known to be nice—was hung and bludgeoned to death with a metal bedpost (and jumped on) by two other inmates, "Big Jim" (and Frank and Larry) <sup>1, 3</sup>. On Floor 4, the solitary/seclusion and violent wards housed TBI patients and violent criminals with unopenable doors where people were chained up; the Violent Men's Ward had female nurses, and in the Violent Female Ward, women used violence more smartly and plotted more to get what they were wanting <sup>5</sup>. Behind the main complex, the Criminally Insane Building functioned more like a prison for 240 of the most dangerous, lost, and forgotten convicts; patient-on-patient murders occurred there, and some inmates escaped by holding guards hostage <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Floor 1, Floor 3 Ward F, Floor 3 Substance Recovery, Floor 4 Ward R; talking in the Party Room); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Floor 1, Floor 1 Hall, Lily's Room, Floor 2, Floor 3, Floor 3 Ward F, Criminal Hall, Floor 4, Floor 4 Ward R, Criminally Insane Building; Lily seen on Floor 1; the shadowy figure that killed Dean in Ward F; "Big Jim" seen as a shadow figure on Floor 4 and in the hall between the violent men's and women's wards; shadow figures on the floor, walls, and ceiling in Ward R); Noises (Floor 2, Floor 3, Floor 3 Ward T, Floor 3 Substance Recovery, Floor 4 Ward R; knocking, fun folks, and door jiggles in Substance Recovery; knocking and talking in the Party Room); Temperature Spots; Moving Objects (Floor 3, Floor 3 Ward T, Floor 3 Substance Recovery, Criminal Hall, Floor 4 Ward R; door jiggles in Substance Recovery); Felt Touch (Criminally Insane Building); Physical Mark (Lobotomy Recovery Room, Floor 3 Ward T, Floor 4 Ward R, Room 717; a woman was scratched in Room 717; choke marks and claw/face scratches on Floor 4); Mimics / Doppelgangers (Floor 3 Substance Recovery, Criminally Insane Building; multiple groups, including Destination Fear, have experienced another group member calling them on their phone when they weren't—even when the other member's phone was dead of battery or not on them—just to answer and hear breathing or screeching); Physiological Symptom (Lobotomy Recovery Room; passing out on Floor 2); Electronic Interference (Floor 1, Floor 2, Floor 3 Ward F; equipment drain and melted equipment); Other (smells on Floor 1; drafts of wind on Floor 1 and Floor 2) <sup>1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** "The Creeper" / "Brownie" (maybe separate?; a multi-limbed, non-human/demon entity—or a doctor—on Floor 4 Ward R that crawls on walls, floors, and ceilings, possibly connected to a portal), Lily (an 8-to-9-year-old little girl on Floor 1 in Lily's Room—or possibly a demon), the "Top Hat Man," Emily (8–9 years old, Floor 2), John and Charlie (Party Room), Frank and Larry (in the Party Room; they like playing cards and cigarettes, and knock and talk), "Sly Man" (from a poem by a patient), Dean Metheny and "Big Jim" (Floor 3 Ward F and Floor 4 hall between the violent men's and women's wards, alongside the shadowy figure that killed Dean), a Mimic / Doppelganger, and "The Joker" (Criminally Insane Building) <sup>1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6</sup>
