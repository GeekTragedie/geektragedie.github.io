# Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum

**Type:** Mental Hospital <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Weston, WV, USA <sup>1</sup>  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Originally designed to hold 250 patients, the asylum became massively overcrowded, housing up to 24,000 individuals at its peak <sup>3</sup>. There are an estimated 20,000+ deaths associated with the property <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Historical Background:** Operating from 1864 until its closure in 1994, the 4-floor, 666-acre facility housed all ages and genders, including the criminally insane <sup>1</sup>. The site has a dark history of malpractice, mistreatment, murder, suicide, and natural deaths <sup>1</sup>. Patients were subjected to waterboarding and lobotomies; historical lore states patients would "cry tears of blood" during post-lobotomy recovery <sup>1, 3</sup>. Specific tragedies include a double suicide pact and two patients found dead by hanging from their bedsheets <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Known Entities:** "The Creeper" (identified as a former doctor, also referred to as Brownie, sometimes seen with a top hat), Big Jim, a Mimic/Doppelganger, Frank & Larry, Lily (an 8-9 year old girl), Emily, Gabriel, Denise/Dean, Hardy, Rabbit, Sina, Ed, Timothy, Lisa, Anthony, Francis, Fred, Carlton, Christina, Freddy, Alan, and Oliver <sup>1, 2, 3</sup>.

---

### Location Directory & Investigation Evidence

#### Floor 1 (Women's Ward & Lily's Area)
*   **Historical Lore:** Known for shadow figures, disembodied voices (EVPs), strange smells, and sudden drafts of wind <sup>1</sup>. 
*   **Investigation Evidence (KallMeKris):** Investigators recorded temperature drops and significant equipment drain/interference <sup>1</sup>. The Estes method and flashlights were highly responsive <sup>1</sup>. 
*   **Names Received:** Gabriel, Denise/Dean, and 7 unnamed spirits <sup>1</sup>.

#### Floor 2 (Offices, Dining, Juvenile & Children's Wards)
*   **Historical Lore:** Historically known for drafts of wind, footsteps, knocking, equipment drain, and causing visitors to pass out <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Double Suicide Room:**
    *   *Investigation Evidence (KallMeKris):* An Alice Box and a motion-activated Music Box were triggered <sup>1</sup>. 
    *   *Names Received:* Hardy, Rabbit, and Sina (identified as a grandmother) <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Emily's Room:**
    *   *Investigation Evidence (KallMeKris):* An Ovilus and a Teddy Bear light were utilized and active <sup>1</sup>.

#### Floor 3 (Criminally Insane Ward)
*   **Historical Lore:** Known for shadow figures, footsteps, and severe poltergeist activity, including moving objects <sup>1</sup>.
*   **The Cage:**
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - P2):* Shadow figures and footsteps were documented <sup>2</sup>. Investigators used a REM Rope and an Alice Box <sup>2</sup>. 
    *   *Names Received:* Ed and Timothy <sup>2</sup>.
*   **Criminal Hall:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* Known for poltergeist activity and moving objects <sup>3</sup>.
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - Demonic Asylum):* The group documented footsteps, tapping, and unexplained noises, noting the spirits "gave the group a run-around" <sup>3</sup>. A Spirit Bridge and bell were active <sup>3</sup>.

#### Floor 4 (The "Fear Factor" Ward)
*   **Historical Lore:** Widely considered the most haunted floor <sup>1</sup>. It housed solitary and seclusion wards <sup>2</sup>. 
*   **Violent Men's Ward:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* Housed criminals and patients with Traumatic Brain Injuries (TBI) <sup>2</sup>. Patients were chained up, and doors were rendered inoperable <sup>2</sup>. Despite the violent male population, it was staffed entirely by female nurses <sup>2</sup>.
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - P2):* Investigators recorded shadow figures and footsteps, utilizing a REM Rope <sup>2</sup>.
*   **Violent Female Ward:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* Lore suggests the female patients plotted more often and used violence "more smartly" to achieve their goals <sup>2</sup>.
*   **Connecting Hallway:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* A massive shadow figure known as "Big Jim" is seen roaming the hall between the violent men's and women's wards <sup>2</sup>.

#### Unknown Floor / Other Areas
*   **Lily's Room:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* A highly active area utilized for lobotomy recovery <sup>3</sup>.
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - Demonic Asylum):* Physical movement was observed <sup>3</sup>. Equipment interference that had impacted a previous visit occurred again <sup>3</sup>. Cat balls, a music box, and a REM pod were triggered, and a balloon with a light was utilized <sup>3</sup>.
*   **Lobotomy Recovery Room (LR):**
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - Demonic Asylum):* A bell and a Spirit Bridge were actively responding <sup>3</sup>. 
    *   *Names Received:* Francis and Fred <sup>3</sup>.
*   **Party Room:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* Haunted by the spirits of Frank and Larry <sup>2, 3</sup>. Activity frequently mimics the sounds of a party, including knocking, talking, playing cards, and smoking cigarettes <sup>3</sup>.
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - Demonic Asylum):* EVPs were caught <sup>3</sup>. An Infinity Deck and an Alice Box were responsive <sup>3</sup>. 
    *   *Names Received:* Oliver <sup>3</sup>.
*   **Room 17:**
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - Demonic Asylum):* An automatic writer was utilized <sup>3</sup>. A voice was caught on camera, and interactions were recorded on the Spirit Bridge and a bell <sup>3</sup>. One investigator felt a physical touch on their back <sup>3</sup>. 
    *   *Names Received:* Carlton, Christina, Freddy, and Alan <sup>3</sup>.
*   **Ward R:**
    *   *Historical Lore:* Features a "well cell" and is known for a shadow figure manifesting on the floor <sup>3</sup>. Associated with poltergeist activity, EVPs, and footsteps <sup>2</sup>. "The Creeper," identified as a former doctor, resides here <sup>3</sup>.
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - P2 & Demonic Asylum):* Investigators experienced temperature drops, tapping, and equipment interference <sup>2, 3</sup>. An Infinity Deck, Alice Box, and REM pod were used <sup>2, 3</sup>.
*   **Sub / Ward T (Substance Area / Civil War Area):**
    *   *Historical Lore:* Known for door jiggles, knocks, and "fun folks" <sup>2</sup>. A Mimic/Doppelganger has also been reported in this area <sup>2</sup>.
    *   *Investigation Evidence (Celina Spookyboo - P2):* Investigators captured knocking, thuds, and stomps on camera, and felt physical breath on their faces <sup>2</sup>. Heavy equipment interference was noted while using an Alice Box and REM Pod <sup>2</sup>. 
    *   *Names Received:* Lisa (noted as abused) and Anthony <sup>2</sup>.

---

### General Investigation Methods
Across these active investigations, the groups frequently utilized an interview-style questioning technique to communicate with the spirits and maintained dream journals to monitor attachments <sup>1, 2, 3</sup>.
