# Saratoga County Homestead

**Type:** Hospital  
**Location:** Middle Grove, NY  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Between 800 and 1,000 deaths occurred here during its time as a TB hospital.
*   **Historical Background:** The Saratoga County Homestead is an abandoned medical facility that transitioned from a tuberculosis hospital to a nursing home. The facility was eventually abandoned starting in 1979. It is currently being taken care of by Haunted Nights and is known for housing "not nice" spirits.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (spirits of men, women, and children have all been reported, with some characterized as distinctly malevolent or "not nice")
