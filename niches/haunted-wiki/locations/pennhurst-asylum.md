# Pennhurst Asylum

**Type:** Hospital  
**Location:** Spring City, PA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Over 5,000 people died here during its operation.
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** The expansive property once featured 32 distinct buildings and a network of underground tunnels; unexplained noises are heard particularly in the tunnel system.
*   **Historical Background:** Pennhurst Asylum was a massive and notorious state school and hospital known for its dark history and sprawling campus. The expansive property once featured 32 distinct buildings and a network of underground tunnels. Over 5,000 people died here during its operation. There is currently a question of whether the remaining structures will be demolished forever.
*   **Reported Lore:** Noises (unexplained noises are frequently heard, particularly in the tunnel system); Other (poltergeist activity is reported by visitors)
