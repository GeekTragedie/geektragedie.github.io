### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 5,000+ (out of 10,000+ total patients, including children) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** 32 Buildings total; Mayflower Building (Floor 3, featuring the Hours Room / Toy Room, and Basement, featuring the Red Room); Devon Building (Basement → Candyland, a previous physical therapy room); Tunnels (connecting all buildings, with the Crossroads in the center) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Known as the "Shame of Pennsylvania," Pennhurst State School and Hospital opened in 1908 with the stated goal of helping people with developmental disabilities, though it also housed orphans and became connected to eugenics practices <sup>1</sup>. Operating for 79 years, the facility housed over 10,000 people across its history (including children) and became severely underfunded and overcrowded, peaking at 3,000 patients with only 9 doctors <sup>1</sup>. Rooms were set up like cubicles with no ceilings, children were chained to walls and kept in cages, and staff referred to patients of all ages as "little children" <sup>1</sup>. Widespread physical and sexual assault occurred between patients, and staff abused and killed patients; most notoriously, Resident Head Physician Dr. Jesse G. Fear injected misbehaving patients with painful chemicals specifically chosen to cause excruciating pain without killing them <sup>1</sup>. In the Mayflower Basement, "music therapy" was conducted by a patient/figure known as "King," while "Candyland" in the Devon Basement served as an ambulatory/physical therapy room and the Tunnels connected all buildings to transport patients <sup>1</sup>. Following NBC reporter Bill Baldini's groundbreaking 1968 exposé documentary *"Suffer the Little Children,"* it took over a decade of legal battles to finally close the asylum in 1987 <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (Mayflower Basement, Tunnels); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Mayflower Basement); Noises (Tunnels); Moving Objects (Mayflower Basement; Howie, a child spirit on Mayflower Floor 3, opens windows and moves a toy plane); Felt Touch (Crossroads); Portals / Hellmouths (Ouija board activity); Physiological Symptom (Mayflower Basement); Other (smell of cigar smoke) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** "Howie" (a child spirit on Mayflower Floor 3 who opens the window and interacts with a toy plane), "King" (in the Mayflower Basement; an asshole / unknown demon or possible old maintenance worker), a demonic presence in the Devon Basement, and spirits at the Crossroads in the Tunnels <sup>1</sup>
