# Elis Hotel (Bully for You / Colfax Mercantile)

**Type:** Hotel <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Colfax, WA, USA <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: No  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Unknown <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Hotel rooms; a "creepy hallway to nowhere"; a blocked-off door; the downstairs Mercantile ("Bully for You" / Colfax Mercantile) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built in the 1800s (1893), the Elis Hotel is an abandoned historic hotel above the Colfax Mercantile ("Bully for You") that was shut down and never reopened <sup>1</sup>. Currently owned by Austin (with Val owning the other half), the building features a "creepy hallway to nowhere" and a door that is blocked off <sup>1</sup>. Prior to this visit, the hotel had never been investigated, and the name of the hotel was listed/given in the *Perkins House* video <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (a witness saw a tall angry man and a child ghost); Noises (doors; kids playing); Moving Objects (cups and chairs move in the Mercantile) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Mostly unknown due to a lack of prior investigation experience, though a tall angry man and a child ghost (kids playing) have been reported <sup>1</sup>
