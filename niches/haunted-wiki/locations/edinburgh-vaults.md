# The Edinburgh Vaults

**Type:** Storage  
**Location:** Edinburgh, Scotland  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Edinburgh Vaults are a series of underground chambers originally used for storage. They have a dark history and are considered one of the most haunted subterranean locations in Scotland. The vaults have a history of fires and deaths. There is also a notorious circle where a group allegedly summoned something intentionally.
*   **Reported Lore:** Past Rituals (a notorious circle where a group allegedly summoned something intentionally); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (frequently seen); Felt Touch (visitors and investigators report being physically pushed); Physical Mark (unexplained physical markings on their bodies); Noises (unexplained noises echo through the underground chambers)
*   **Known Entities:** "Mr. Boots"
