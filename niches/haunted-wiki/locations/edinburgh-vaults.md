# Edinburgh Vaults

**Type:** Storage <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Location:** Edinburgh, Scotland, UK <sup>1, 2</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** High (exact number not named in video) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Whole Vault (most active); Witches Room; Wiccan Chamber; Mr. Boots' Room (where a young girl's body was found); Cobbler's Room; Double Vaulted Room; Jack's Room; White Room; Hall; Waiting Area; Room with Ladder; Room with Bottles; Empty Vault <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built in 1788 beneath South Bridge and Niddry Street (an area with a history of 1500s witch trials), the Edinburgh Vaults consist of 120 underground vaults <sup>1, 2</sup>. Originally used by businesses and taverns, the vaults were abandoned by store owners after water and waste oozed through, creating poor conditions with no sanitation, running water, or light <sup>2</sup>. Low-socioeconomic-status residents, squatters, and Irish famine survivors subsequently moved in, enduring a high mortality rate (with many children born and dying there) and rampant crime with no punishment <sup>1, 2</sup>. Infamous criminals and body snatchers Burke and Hare operated here, aided by the vaults' connection to the nearby medical school and graveyard <sup>1, 2</sup>. During the Great Fire of 1824, people went into the vaults for safety and barricaded the entrance; however, the vaults became an oven instead and killed everyone inside (with some parents killing their own children in morbid ways) <sup>2</sup>. Sealed with rubble in the 18th/19th century for over 100 years, the vaults were rediscovered in the 20th century (1988) by a landowner and can have varying temperatures <sup>1, 2</sup>. Additionally, a woman who was meant to bless the area passed away before performing the blessing, which is possibly connected to the activity, and the Wiccan Chamber was used in 1995 and offered to a bad spirit <sup>1, 2</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices; Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Witches Room, Cobbler's Room, Mr. Boots' Room; a shadow figure in the Cobbler's Room); Noises; Felt Touch (2x; a YouTuber's sister experienced a hair tug while visiting as a child; Jack holding hands and tugging on coats; people being pushed in the Cobbler's Room); Possessions / Trances (Wiccan Chamber); Past Rituals (Wiccan Chamber; used in 1995 and offered to a bad spirit; Witches Room used by previous witches); Physiological Symptom (2x; pregnant women feeling uncomfortable in the Cobbler's Room) <sup>1, 2</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** "The Watcher" / "Mr. Boots" (a slumlord, murderer, or body snatcher who yells *"Get out"* and said *"My name is Edward"*); Woman in Black (who lost her child); Woman with long dark hair (murdered by men who lured her in; seen with her face in blood); Jack (a child spirit from around 1810—possibly tied to unsolved cases at the time—who holds hands and tugs on coats); and the "Mackenzie Poltergeist" (in the nearby Edinburgh graveyard, where a homeless man was attacked by a shadow figure) <sup>1, 2</sup>
