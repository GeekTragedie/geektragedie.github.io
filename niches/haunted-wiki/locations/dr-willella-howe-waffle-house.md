# The Dr. Willella Howe-Waffle House

**Type:** Home  
**Location:** Santa Ana, CA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Dr. Willella Howe-Waffle House is a historic property that served dual purposes as both a home and a bustling medical practice for a prominent female physician. The property was owned by Dr. Willella Howe-Waffle (a hyphenated name). Serving as her medical office, over 1,000 babies were delivered inside this home.
