# Casa Loma (aka Hill House)

**Type:** Residence <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Toronto, ON, Canada <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 0 (no one died at the location, but spirits are connected to the building) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Sir Henry's Office; Lady Mary's Room; the Tunnel; the Stables; the Maids' Room; Floor 2 <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Casa Loma was Sir Henry Pellatt's home. He owned most of the electricity in Canada until the government took it away; because he couldn't pay back his loans and his bank was shut down, tens of thousands of people lost money <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices; Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Lady Mary's Room, Sir Henry's Office, the Tunnel; the White Lady in the halls); Noises; Moving Objects (the Tunnel); Felt Touch (the Tunnel); Electronic Interference (Floor 2; Lady Mary is associated with battery drain and cameras breaking) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** A little girl, Sir Henry, Lady Mary, and the "White Lady" (seen in the halls; follows visitors) <sup>1</sup>
