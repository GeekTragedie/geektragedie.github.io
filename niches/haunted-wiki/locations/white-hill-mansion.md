# White Hill Mansion

**Type:** Residence <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Fieldsboro, NJ, USA <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** Multiple (Robert Field found dead in the water, 7 children who died in the house, a man killed in the 1st-floor bathtub, and people who died during a basement poker game and bar stabbing) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Floor 1: Front Stairs, Parlor, and Bathroom (bathtub); Floor 2 / Attic: Attic (noted as a dangerous place), Room in Attic, and "Shy Man" Room; Basement (former speakeasy) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built around 300 years ago (to date), White Hill Mansion is one of the earliest homes built in the United States and stood during the Revolutionary War, spanning 1,000 acres at its height <sup>1</sup>. The original owners were Robert and Mary Field; Robert was involved in shady business with guns and was found dead in the water in Pennsylvania in an unsolved murder mystery, leaving Mary to take care of the house during his absence in the Revolutionary War <sup>1</sup>. The couple also lost 7 children <sup>1</sup>. On the 1st floor, a man was murdered in the bathtub <sup>1</sup>. Later, during Prohibition, the Basement became a speakeasy tied to organized mob hangouts and secret tunnel systems; in the Basement, someone was stabbed at the bar, and people died during a poker game <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** EVPs / Disembodied Voices (groaning); Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Parlor, Attic; a woman in white Victorian clothing in the Parlor; a "Crawler" shadow figure on the ground in the Attic); Noises (Parlor, Basement; footsteps in the Parlor); Moving Objects; Felt Touch (Basement; hair, objects, and clothes being touched) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Mary Field (the "Lady of the House," who continues to protect the home), a woman in white Victorian clothing (Parlor), the "Crawler" (a shadow figure on the ground in the Attic), and "The Mobster" (in the Basement, who is not nice and is mean) <sup>1</sup>
