# Crescent Hotel

**Type:** Lodging / Hospital <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Eureka Springs, AR, USA <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 94+ (94 documented deaths due to Norman Baker's treatments, though almost all patients died; plus a woman who fell from the 3rd-floor balcony and Irish stonemason Michael who died during construction) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Elevator; Room 202; Room 419 (Theodora's Room); Room 218; Dig Site (downstairs); Morgue; Meat Locker; Crystal Dining Room / Ballroom; Floor 2; Floor 3 <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Originally built in 1886 as a luxury hotel (and later home to a general manager who held a large funeral for his cat, Morris), the Crescent Hotel previously served as a college and conservatory for young women before being turned into a fraudulent cancer hospital in the late 1930s by conman Norman Baker—the "purple man" who operated without a doctor's license <sup>1</sup>. Baker lied to everyone about healing and curing cancer; in reality, at least 94 deaths occurred due to his treatments, and almost all of his patients died <sup>1</sup>. He would have patients sign away their money and send them home, and he maintained a psych ward section to lock away loud or questioning patients so they could not tell anyone the "cure" was fake <sup>1</sup>. Baker knew all of his patients would die, and federal authorities eventually caught him, though he received little punishment <sup>1</sup>. There is also a rumor of a head nurse who became a patient <sup>1</sup>. Baker had secret passages and hid body parts in jars and bottles (which were uncovered at the Dig Site in 2019), while the Meat Locker in the Morgue was where dead bodies were stored so nothing would smell (using hooks for strings) <sup>1</sup>. Additionally, an Irish stonemason named Michael died during the construction of the hotel in 1885–1886 near Room 218, and a woman fell off the 3rd-floor balcony into the garden <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** Shadow Figures / Apparitions (Elevator, Floor 3, Ballroom, Room 202; a person seen at the end of the bed in Room 202; a woman in a Victorian outfit; a possible shadow of a man in the window; Theodora in Room 419); Noises (Morgue); Temperature Spots (Morgue; 2 people on a locker had a heat signature); Moving Objects (Room 218, Room 419, Morgue; a bellhop delivering food; people riding the Elevator even though no one is there; Theodora in Room 419 looking for her purse or keys and tidying up the room); Felt Touch (Floor 2, Room 218; people have been locked or almost locked inside the Meat Locker) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Theodora (Room 419), Michael the Irish stonemason (died during construction of the hotel near Room 218), a bellhop, a woman in a Victorian outfit (Room 202), and a woman who fell off the 3rd-floor balcony <sup>1</sup>
