# Myrtles Plantation

**Type:** Home  
**Location:** St. Francisville, LA  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Historical Background:** The Myrtles Plantation is an infamous historic property steeped in tragedy, disease, and murder, making it one of the most widely recognized haunted homes in the South. Originally a plantation home, the property has a dense history of tragedy. A child died of yellow fever on the premises. In a separate incident, the original owner's husband, wife, and two children were accidentally murdered by a slave, who was subsequently killed as punishment. Additionally, previous owners murdered a consultant who had incorrectly claimed their child would survive yellow fever.
