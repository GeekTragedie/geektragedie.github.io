# Comedy Mothership

**Type:** Club <sup>1</sup>  
**Location:** Austin, TX, USA <sup>1</sup>  
**Status:** Demolished: No · Open to Public: Yes  

---

### Overview & Historical Lore

*   **Estimated Death Toll:** 8 (historically on the street; plus a rumored death in the 1970s) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Layout & Hotspots:** Main Stage (Fat Man); Smaller Stage (Little Boy); Balcony; Green Room; Mitzi's Bar <sup>1</sup>
*   **Historical Background:** Built in 1929 as "The Ritz Theatre," the building has operated as a rowdy cowboy theatre and multiple other venues—including a pool hall, a nude movie theatre, a punk music hall, and the Alamo Drafthouse until 2021—before becoming the Comedy Mothership in 2023 <sup>1</sup>. Prior to this visit, the building had never yet been investigated, though staff describe that the "building feels alive" <sup>1</sup>. There is a rumor of a death occurring inside during the 1970s, and historically in 1885, the Austin serial killer known as the "Servant Girl Annihilator" (possibly Nathan Elgin) killed 8 people on the same street <sup>1</sup>.
*   **Reported Lore:** Noises (Main Stage); Other (Main Stage; three security cameras detected a person at the exact same time while tour guide Keno heard a noise) <sup>1</sup>
*   **Known Entities:** Victims or spirit connected to the 1885 Servant Girl Annihilator (possibly Nathan Elgin) <sup>1</sup>
