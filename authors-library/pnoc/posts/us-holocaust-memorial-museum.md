### What I Noticed
* **The ID booklet:** At the entrance you're handed an identification booklet that follows the life and fate of one real person who was targeted and confined during the Holocaust. [who was yours?]
* **The Room of Shoes:** Thousands of shoes taken from prisoners deported to Majdanek and other camps. It's one of the most visceral things in the museum, because it makes the scale of the killing personal.
