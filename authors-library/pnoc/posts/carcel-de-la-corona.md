# Cárcel de la Corona: Descending Beneath the Elder Living Facility

**Visited:** April 24, 2023 | **Location:** Calle de la Cabeza, Lavapiés, Madrid, Spain | **Category:** I. Pre-Modern

---

Walking through the front doors of an active elder living facility in Madrid to descend a steep, unblocked stone stairwell into subterranean cells built for monks who broke their vow of silence...

[your reflection here]

### What I Noticed
* **Getting in:** The building is an elder living facility now. The stairwell down to the cells is open, in the hallway directly to the left of the front entrance.
* **The stairs:** The stone steps are steep and worn smooth, and they're slippery even when dry.
* **The cold:** The air and the walls got noticeably cooler as I went underground.

### The History
Monks and clerics who broke church rules or their vows of silence were confined in these cells, and water was channeled down from the street to keep them standing in it.
