### What I Noticed
* **The way in:** You walk to the museum through a tunnel lined with relief imagery, beneath the Motherland Monument. It sets a solemn tone before you're even inside.
* **The Room of Cups:** Cups set out on tables for the empty chairs, photographs of the fallen on the walls, and musical instruments hanging from the ceiling. It carried an emotional weight that echoed, and went beyond, the Room of Shoes at the [US Holocaust Memorial Museum](#posts/us-holocaust-memorial-museum).
