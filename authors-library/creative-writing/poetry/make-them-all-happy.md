# Make Them All Happy

**Course / Context:** Creative Writing, English 283 (Holly Baker), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** 7 August 2015  

---

<pre class="poem">
        You wish I was around
            But never make an effort to come down.
        You want to help me move in
            But getting rid of my old stuff would help you win.
        You say you’ll be there
            But all I see is the money you spare.
    I know being in your life seems crappy…
    But all I want to do is make you happy.

        You say you want space
            Then you’re clingy as sewn on lace.
        You say you like my hair curly
            Then you want it as straight as a Barbie.
        You want to spend more time together
            You never make the time, however.
    I know being in your life seems crappy…
    But all I want to do is make you happy.

        You say I should spend more time reading
            No, now running is what I’m needing.
        You want me to get good grades
            But you act like they have AIDS.
        You tell me to follow my dreams
            Then you say I’m following others’ schemes.
    I know being in your life seems crappy…
    But all I want to do is make you happy.
</pre>
