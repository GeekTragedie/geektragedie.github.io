# Walking on Egg Shells

**Course / Context:** Creative Writing, English 283 (Holly Baker), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** 7 August 2015  

---

<pre class="poem">
<span style="display:inline-block;min-width:11em">You say:</span>I want:
<span style="display:inline-block;min-width:11em">Procrastinator.</span>Self-driven.
<span style="display:inline-block;min-width:11em">Phony.</span>Intelligent.
<span style="display:inline-block;min-width:11em">Vulnerable.</span>Independent.
<span style="display:inline-block;min-width:11em">Self-Centered.</span>Trustworthy.
<span style="display:inline-block;min-width:11em">Lethargic.</span>Motivated.

Our relationship is like
    An old roller coaster.
        It should be fun,
    But the whole ride
You feel you will crash.
    Most of the time,
        I wish it really would.
</pre>
