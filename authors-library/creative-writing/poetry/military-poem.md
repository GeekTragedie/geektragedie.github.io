# Military Poem

**Course / Context:** Multicultural Perspectives & Creative Arts Therapy, Antioch University Seattle  
**Date:** 2018–2019  

---

<pre class="poem">
Your personality
    doesn’t matter here.
Your intelligence
    doesn’t matter here.
Your work
    doesn’t matter here.
Your opinion
    doesn’t matter here.
Your emotion
    doesn’t matter here.
Your time
    doesn’t matter here.
Your pride
    doesn’t matter here.
Your kind
    doesn’t matter here.
You don’t matter here and that’s fine.
</pre>

</aside>
