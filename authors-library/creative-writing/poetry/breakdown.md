# Breakdown

**Course / Context:** Creative Writing, English 283 (Holly Baker), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** 7 August 2015  

---

<pre class="poem">
Eyes
    Swollen like an over-itched mosquito bite
    Red like the blood pooling from a rose pricked finger
        As the tears collide with her
Mouth
    Salty like ocean water from shipwreck
    Whimpering like a dog missing their deceased owner
        As the tears stream down her
Face
    Speckled like an arm after an allergy test
    Tired like soldier after a long night’s work
        As the tears plummet onto her
Shoulders
    Heaving like a shipman pulling a sail on a Viking’s ship
    Tense like a juvenile waiting for the judge’s sentence
        As the tears get wiped away by her
Hands
    Shaking like a person with anxiety being pulled over
    Clutching like a father’s hands on a bat looking for an intruder
        As the tears continue in an endless cycle.
</pre>
