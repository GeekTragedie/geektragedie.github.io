# Feminism Poem

**Course / Context:** Multicultural Perspectives & Creative Arts Therapy, Antioch University Seattle  
**Date:** 2018–2019  

---

<pre class="poem">
Nothing is as it should be!
    We must band together against the oppressors!
    Demand pro-choice!
Nothing is as it should be!
    Punish the man!
    Demand equal pay!
Nothing is as it should be!
    Fight sexism!
    Increase self-esteem!
Nothing is as it should be!
    Cease body shaming!
    Become education!
Women are created equal
But are not at all treated that way!
Nothing is ever going to be fine!
</pre>
