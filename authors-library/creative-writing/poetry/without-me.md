# Without Me

**Course / Context:** Creative Writing, English 283 (Holly Baker), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** 7 August 2015  

---

<pre class="poem" style="text-align: center;">
<b>Maine</b>
Loading up the suit cases
Off to enjoy the family
Soon to be picture perfect moments
<i>Without me.</i>

<b>Wisconsin</b>
Two to a seat times two
Everyone fits like a new pair of pants
Picture perfect moment
<i>Without me.</i>

<b>Nebraska</b>
One room fit for four
Bursts of laughter and smiles
Picture perfect moment
<i>Without me</i>

<b>South Dakota</b>
Grinning with his mouth and eyes
On our hero’s shoulders
Picture perfect moment
<i>Without me.</i>

<b>Florida</b>
Walking down the trail framed by the forest
Hand in hand in hand in hand
Picture perfect moment
<i>Without me.</i>
</pre>
