# Traditional Poem

**Course / Context:** Multicultural Perspectives & Creative Arts Therapy, Antioch University Seattle  
**Date:** 2018–2019  

---

<pre class="poem">
Sit patiently and always
    Look pretty.
    Look skinny.
    Look happy.
        Everything’s Fine.

Be the ideal wife and always
    Do the washing.
    Do the cooking.
    Do the cleaning.
        Everything’s Fine.

Uphold Christian ideals and always
    Be Pro-Life.
    Be conservative.
    Be evangelistic.
        Everything’s Fine.
Comply to traditional womanhood and always
    Stay soft-spoken.
    Stay unopinionated.
    Stay in the background.
        Everything’s Fine.
You are fine.
</pre>
