# Life Suitcase

**Course / Context:** Creative Writing, English 283 (Holly Baker), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** 7 August 2015  

---

<p class="content-note"><strong>Content note:</strong> self-harm imagery.</p>

<pre class="poem">
Theory pounding your brain with a hammer.
Judgment’s eyes burning through your soul.
Anger seeps out your mouth.
Family squirming up your throat.
Anxiety wrapping its hands around your neck.
Society yanking your arm out of its socket.
Steel-toed boots walking all over your back.
Love meticulously sawing your heart in half.
Schedule etched into your skin as the blood runs.
Imperfection cripples your legs.
Impatience seeps between your toes.

Each night
You unload as much as possible
Into Mary Poppins’s bag.
Some things refuse.
Some things release.
Some things retaliate.

It sits at the end of the bed
Your life suitcase.
Like a black jaguar
Ready to pounce on its
Prey.

Somewhere towards the bottom
Is the child you used to be
Gasping for air.
</pre>
