# The FoO Paper Monologues

**Course / Context:** Family of Origin Systems & Creative Arts Therapy (Janice Hoshino, Ph.D. & Bobbi Kidder, MA), Antioch University Seattle  
**Date:** November 7, 2018  

---

<p class="content-note"><strong>Content note:</strong> grief and the loss of a sibling.</p>

<p class="piece-note">Written alongside my Family of Origin Analysis. Family names and places have been changed or generalized.</p>

### Author’s Note

The monologues written in this collection are me speaking directly to a family member in relation to the paper I have written. Throughout the process, I realized the amount of depth I go into reflects the relationship I have with the person I’m talking to. I also noticed I was very selective regarding certain topics and how I would explain the ideas to the family member. The family members I chose to write a monologue for are those who I would normally talk about school with. They are also family members who, after writing my paper, I realized had an important role in shaping my life.

### Mom-ologue

Hey mom. I just wanted to let you know some information that’s in my Family of Origin paper. We were supposed to analyze our family using some theories we learned in class. Um… I would prefer you to not read it because some of the analysis may not make you too happy. Our relationship has been starting to kindle, and I don’t want this paper to get in the way of where we are right now. Maybe when we have a more stable, consistent relationship and we are more open to sharing our feelings, I’ll share the paper with you. It’d be nice to get to that point someday.

Some of the information included in the paper is about you, grandma, grandpa, and your sisters when you moved overseas and how that impacted our family. I think part of the reason our family is so close (centripetal) is because of all the traveling you did and how you didn’t have very many people to talk to that knew English well. I then explain my aunts’ families a little, but not too much in detail.

I also talked about you marrying my dad and how Dad’s family was pretty awful towards you during the divorce. I said their cruelty was due to them wanting to keep social appearances and wanted the family balance to not dwindle. They took out their anxiety towards change on you instead of themselves.

I then talked about you meeting daddy, getting married and having Thomas and Daniel. I tell of the stories you shared about me never wanting to let T go. I discuss the close sibling relationships we tend to have in our families: you and your sisters, me and my brothers.

It was hard, but I also talked about my dad and the frustration I have at him for cutting me off. I feel as though he has almost started a new family without me.

The last section is about patterns in our family from generation to generation. I found gender patterns of women typically sticking to the traditional role portrayed in society. I know how you’ve complained about having to do most of the cleaning and cooking and we both know that grandma can cook to feed an army.

You should check out my genogram! It’s from when I was 17 years old, but it’s only changed a little bit since then. I think it’s pretty cool to look at all the connections this way on paper.

I love you mom and miss you a ton. I can’t wait for you to come and visit me! I can’t wait to share my experiences I’ve had here with you and it sucks that my school had to be so far away from home – especially when there are times when I just want to be with my family.

### Gramonologue

Hey grandma! Yeah, I finished that genogram I was working on for class. Your pictures were super helpful! I’m so glad you keep track of all of that information for the family. We should sit down over Christmas and map out more of the family on the program I used so that we have more copies of it for the next generations. I think that would be really fun. Hey, can I talk to you about the paper I had to write for that class? I just kind of want to decompress from it and talk it through with someone. It brought out a lot of feelings. So the paper we had to write was about our family of origin and we had to use theories we learned in class to analyze our family and see how the family influenced us.

I start out explaining the different people in the family on both my mom and my dad’s side and then had to go into talking about different family relationships. I started out talking about my parents’ divorce and how my dad’s side of the family was awful to my mom: threatening and nasty and such. I related that to my mom throwing off the family balance and that my dad’s side didn’t want their balance to be ruined, so to prevent anxiety towards the situation, they took out their anger on my mom, saying everything was her fault. It’s kind of nice to have some sort of theory to back up their behavior… it does not in any means make it right, but it’s kind of reassuring that there’s some sort of explanation for people I view as nice to lash out at someone in such a terrible way.

I then start talking about how my mom and her sisters are really close. I sort of related it to you all moving overseas where many of the people there spoke less English, so my mom and aunts mostly relied on each other for relationships.

I also talk about how I’m close to my siblings and how that sort of runs in the family – all the siblings are close and get along. I always saw that as the norm for families.

I also talk about my relationship with my mom. How it was good for a while, but then we started to have conflict with each other. You know what I’m talking about… I don’t really know how the conflict started or why, but I semi theorized it was when Thomas started school and my mom started to pay more attention to his education, but I didn’t like that? Maybe? I don’t really remember that far back because I have such a bad memory, Thomas was always the one that remembered things. He was so good at that… In my paper I talk about how I would talk to Tom about my problems with my mom… I realize now that probably caused more anxiety in him and feel a little bad about that. Nothing I can really do now though. At least the two of us had a good relationship – I wish that for all siblings, especially now that I know what it’s like to lose one.

Um… anyways, I then talk about adding my stepdad to the family and how I enjoyed being a parental child towards my brothers – carrying Tom around all the time and teaching him and Daniel math at night before bed.

I also talk about my struggle with my dad and how I was once really close to him and my other grandma until he got remarried and had my little brother, which is kind of when he stopped talking to me.

Finally, you will think this is kind of neat, we had to trace back patterns in our family from generation to generation. There were so many in our family, which I thought was cool! We all went to college and got degrees, which is awesome. Another pattern I saw was gender roles; most of the family members fit to the traditional – woman cook and clean while men do the lawn work sort of stereotype. I also noticed that our family doesn’t really talk about certain topics. We keep away from politics, morals, and feelings towards one another… I’m not sure why, probably to not deal with conflict. We don’t really like conflict… which makes sense, but it is important to talk to one another about our feelings. I think we’ve gotten better at that since Thomas passed away.

You can see why I wanted to talk through this with someone?? There were times when I had to stop writing my paper and work on other homework because I was getting too emotional to be objective. I’m doing well out here in Seattle and my husband is taking good care of me…

### Bro-logue

You’re probably looking down at me and laughing at my inability to just write this right now. Maybe not, you may try to comfort me and say that I can do it. Knowing you, you’d be more worried about me finishing the paper on time and doing well in school and taking time for myself than think about yourself. Of course, you have all the time in the world now… I guess worry may not be something you feel now, that’s gotta be nice. I hope you finally have peace with that and don’t have it cause you so much pain. I just wish you felt that longer here. I would have loved to share this paper with you. Of course, you probably read it and helped me even write it. Heck, I know you helped me write it! I just think having a face to face conversation with you about these theories and helping to understand our family together would have been fun. We could have just sat at a coffee shop, drank coffee or tea and discussed the way we analyze our family. Then we would probably go off on some sort of tangent about how our family cares too much about what society thinks and was impacted too much by other people, but that’s one of the things I loved about you. You were not afraid to speak your mind and you really helped me learn to talk about those topics in our family we keep hidden under the rug. I really wish you could have come to visit me in Seattle. I would have loved to share my campus/school building with you. You would have loved the way grades are dealt with and the school’s mission. If only more schools were like this, maybe students like you wouldn’t have had so much worry about school…

Hey, can you keep watching over Mom and my relationship? I think it’s getting so much better, but I just want it to continue to get better. Maybe some day I can share this paper with her too.

I miss you like crazy and I know you just want me to remember you in a positive light… but I’m going to cry sometimes and that’s alright. I just gotta let it happen, but don’t worry about me, I’ll live through it. You give me the strength to live through it.

### Dad-ologue

Hey dad. There’s not a whole lot of too new things happening since the last we talked. I wrote this paper for my family of origin class. We had to use theory and apply it to our family to better understand what’s happened. It was interesting to take our readings from class and think about things in our own lives. It helped me learn the concepts better and remember them. I’m also writing a paper for my writing in psychology course…

### Gramonologue

Hey grandma! School is good! I like my **counseling** program. I’m still in Intro to Drama Therapy, yeah they have that as a class here! No, I haven’t officially done this already, I need a certificate…. It’s more ethical if I had a certificate, Grandma. I’m also taking Writing in Psychology. I know I work in a writing center… yes I’m learning a ton in the class and I’m even having some fun in it! They let me write about what I want to write about which is fun. I’m also am taking Family of Origin systems which they teach us theory about families and we think about our own families. We had to write a paper using our families to give examples of some of the theories. It was just for helping us learn our class information. I talked about our family and who married who and when. I talked to my stepmom about dates because she makes the family calendar. Yeah, my husband is doing well…
