# Angry Car Ride

**Course / Context:** Playwriting (Dr. Joe Stollenwerk), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** Fall 2017  

---

<p class="cast-list"><strong>Characters:</strong>
William - husband
Elly - Wife</p>

*(William gave Elly shellfish, which she is allergic to. The couple lives out in the middle of nowhere and it takes a while for them to go anywhere. They are on their way to the hospital in the rain.)*

**WILLIAM**
How are you feeling, darling?

**ELLY** *(no response)*

**WILLIAM**
Can you see okay with the rain and your eyes?

**ELLY** *(no response)*

**WILLIAM**
Look, I’m really sorry that -

**ELLY**
How long have we been married?

**WILLIAM** *(hesitates, too long)*

**ELLY**
Five Years!

**WILLIAM**
Of course. I was gonna say -

**ELLY**
How long did we date before then?

**WILLIAM**
I think it was -

**ELLY**
I had to wait six years for your ass to ask me!

**WILLIAM**
…  Yes. *(William looks at the floor)*

**ELLY**
Where did you take me on our first date?

**WILLIAM**
Red Lob- What was that?

**ELLY**
Accident.

**WILLIAM**
What do you mean accident?

**ELLY**
I mean I just caused that car to drive into the ditch because I blew through a stop sign!

**WILLIAM**
Why did you blow through a stop sign??

**ELLY**
Because I can’t see!!

**WILLIAM**
Let me drive!

**ELLY**
After the last time you drove? No! I trust my allergy-ridden self driving in the rain more than I trust <em>you</em> driving any day!

**WILLIAM**
Well, should we at least see if they are okay?

**ELLY** *(through her teeth)*
I can’t at the moment, darling, because, frankly, I cannot turn around. *(William takes the wheel)* Don’t you Dare do that again!

**WILLIAM**
Elly, you are not thinking clearly! You cannot see! You are starting to slur your - Wow, another one.

**ELLY**
Yeah.

**WILLIAM**
It’s really coming down now. Like razors.

**ELLY**
And you wonder why I didn’t see the stop signs!

**WILLIAM**
No, darling, you can’t see the stop signs because your eyes are practically swollen shut!

**ELLY**
Oh, and why did THAT happen, William??

**WILLIAM**
Because I love you!

**ELLY**
You love me. Right. And you decided to show your love to me by poisoning me?

**WILLIAM**
No, I love you, so I made you a beautiful wedding anniversary dinner with your favorite type of food! I played your favorite music. I went and bought you flowers and candles! I wanted you to know how special you are to me!

**ELLY** *(crying)*
William… I’m so sorry. It did look beautiful. *(whimpers)* Now I certainly can’t see the road because there is rain coming from the sky AND my eyes…

**WILLIAM**
Please pull over, dear. *(the sound of a car pulling over and stopping)*
