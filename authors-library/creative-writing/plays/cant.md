# Can’t

**Course / Context:** Playwriting (Dr. Joe Stollenwerk), University of South Dakota (Draft 2)  
**Date:** 27 September 2017  

---

<p class="content-note"><strong>Content note:</strong> a homophobic slur, bullying and violence, juvenile detention and restraint, police use of force, and a suicide attempt with a gun.</p>

<p class="cast-list"><strong>Cast (in order of appearance):</strong>
Henry - A guy looking for freedom, Henry starts the play tall and then, throughout the play, grows shorter and shorter as he gets older
Isaac - Henry’s friend and significant other
Mom - Enforces house rules
High Schoolers - Enforce status quo
Teacher - Enforces school rules
JDC Staff - Enforces JDC rules
Police Officers - Enforces parole rules &amp; safety
Abe Lincoln - Enforces personal morals</p>

*(Setting: All scenes take place in separate locations. Each scene has its own description.)*

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 1: “Mom’s Home”</p>

*(The scene starts in a living room of a dingy dining room. Henry and Isaac are working on a school project at the table surrounded by bills and dirty plates.)*

**HENRY**
Abe Lincoln rocks! He fought to make sure slaves were free! More people should follow him and all people should be free!

**ISAAC**
If people were free to do what they wanted, so many things would be so much easier, but I bet that’ll never happen.

**HENRY**
Like asking a person out! That can be so frustrating and nerve-wracking! People are always so nervous about saying the wrong thing all the time. They really should just feel free to do and say what they want.

*(ISAAC looks at HENRY admiringly and starts to speak, but is cut off by MOM.)*

**MOM**
Henry, it’s time for Isaac to leave and you need to go to bed.

**HENRY**
Mom, it’s only 7:00. I don’t go to bed until 9.

**MOM**
I don’t care what time it is. When I say that it is time for you to go to bed, you go to bed.

*(HENRY stands on his chair.)*

**HENRY**
I am fourteen years old. I deserve freedom to stay up as late as I want.

**MOM**
Well, when you are in my house, under my roof, your freedom is mine. Now get off my chair, see Isaac out the door and go to bed.

*(MOM exits.)*

**HENRY** *(to Isaac)*
Man, I can’t wait until I’m older and can do whatever the hell I want.

**MOM** *(offstage)*
No swearing in my house!

*(HENRY sees ISAAC to the door. ISAAC goes to give HENRY a high five and HENRY goes to give ISAAC a hug. The two give each other an awkward high five and ISAAC exits the stage.)*

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 2: “Teacher and Student’s School”</p>

*(The scene opens to a lunch room full of high schoolers at tables with a table of STUDENTS at the center. The room has linoleum floor squares that are dirty. It smells of bad lunch food and pubescent anxiety. HENRY and ISAAC emerge between the center groups with their lunch trays.)*

**HENRY**
Isaac. This idea is genius! They would never know what hit them!

*(HENRY, engrossed in the idea, sits at a table with STUDENTS. ISAAC stands back and does not sit down. STUDENT 1 looks at HENRY in disgust.)*

This is the best idea I’ve ever heard. Following those who come before us. It’s genius!

**STUDENT 1** *(to HENRY)*
Um… excuse me. What do you think you’re doing?

**HENRY**
Yeah, Isaac. Come and sit down.

**STUDENT 2** *(to HENRY)*
No, not him. You.

**STUDENTS**
You can’t sit there.

**STUDENT 3**
Especially not with him.

**STUDENT 4**
We have a reputation. You fags sitting here ruins that. Move.

**HENRY**
You can’t dictate us by telling us where to sit. There is no assigned seating. There is no rules telling us where to be.

**STUDENT 5**
It’s not a written rule, dumbass. You just follow it.

**HENRY**
How am I supposed to follow something that isn’t written?

*(STUDENT 6 walks up to HENRY.)*

**STUDENT 6**
Well, you best figure it out and get out of my seat.

**ISAAC**
Come on, Henry. We can sit somewhere else. It’s fine.

**HENRY**
No, Isaac. It’s not fine. They can’t talk to us like this! We have the right to sit here!

**STUDENT 7**
If you don’t move, I’ll have to make you.

**HENRY**
It’s my body and I want to sit here. Deal with it.

*(STUDENT 8 &amp; 9 grab HENRY and ISAAC and continually beat them up. The other STUDENTS ignore the situation. School bell sounds. STUDENTS exit the stage and leave HENRY and ISAAC on the floor. HENRY sits up holding his bruises. HENRY crawls over to ISAAC and grabs his hand.)*

**HENRY**
I’m sorry, Isaac.

**ISAAC**
You just have to listen to people.

**HENRY**
But... we can make them listen to us.

**TEACHER**
No dilly-dallying! Get to class!

**HENRY**
But Isaac can barely stand up.

**TEACHER**
I don’t need to hear any of your coulda-woulda-shoulda excuses. When you’re at school, you follow the school’s rules. Now, pick yourself up and get to class. Now!

*(HENRY helps ISAAC up and the two exit the stage.)*

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 3: “Staff’s JDC”</p>

*(The scene starts in a juvenile detention center. All YOUTH are wearing JDC attire and are quietly sitting at tables. There are walls of books, a single radio, and a rolling TV stand with a big TV. There is a big door on stage with two locks. There are JDC STAFF members in staff attire watching the youth. HENRY and ISAAC are sitting at opposite sides of the room.)*

**STAFF**
Finish up your homework and I’ll sign your incentive sheet so you might get a chance to have freedom time this evening.

*(HENRY stands to give STAFF, by ISAAC, his assignment. While STAFF looks over the sheet and scoffs at answers written on the page.)*

**HENRY** *(whispering to Isaac)*
Hey, how are you holding up?

**ISAAC** *(without looking up)*
I’m not really doing so hot. Last night…

**STAFF**
Hey, no talking! Henry, back to your seat. I really don’t need any trouble today and you two know better than to be by each other when you’re on separation.

**HENRY**
I’m just asking how he’s doing.

**STAFF**
I don’t care what you’re saying. You can’t talk to him.

**HENRY**
This place is the worst. All you guys do is tell us “don’t do this” “don’t do that” “don’t talk” “get away from each other.” Isaac is my best friend and I can’t stand not talking to him.

**STAFF**
Well then maybe you should have thought about that before you decided to break the law! I don’t have the patience for you today!

**HENRY**
The law according to who? This is a free country and I should be able to do whatever I want to!

*(ISAAC stands and tries to calm HENRY down.)*

**STAFF**
Isaac. Sit. Down. Henry, you’re on separation from what you did the other day and are blue-shirted, so no talking and no going near Isaac. You’ll be in lockdown in your room for the rest of the day.

**HENRY**
No! You can’t tell me what to do! I have the right to talk and be with who I choose!

*(HENRY gets out of control in frustration. He throws items in the room and STAFF try to restrain him. HENRY attempts to flip a table, but the table is weighed down and HENRY doesn’t have the ability to flip it. HENRY cries and surrenders. Restrained, STAFF take HENRY off stage.)*

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 4: “Officer’s Streets”</p>

**ISAAC** *(off stage, breathing heavily)*
Henry. I need Henry.

*(The scene opens as HENRY is frantically riding his bike down a street. HENRY has on an ankle monitor. POLICE OFFICER pulls up next to HENRY.)*

**POLICE OFFICER**
Son, what do you think you’re doing? I need you to *stop* now! Get off your bike!

**HENRY**
I can’t! My boyfriend was in a car accident and I need to get to him and never leave his side and make sure he’s alright and calm him down and…

**POLICE OFFICER**
I don’t care what you *think* you need to do. You are breaking your parole and I need you to stop or I’m going to have to stop you myself.

**HENRY** *(through tears)*
No, officer. I’m sorry. I can’t do that. I need to get to Isaac.

*(POLICE OFFICER pulls his car up on the sidewalk and HENRY hits the car with his bike. HENRY attempts to keep running. POLICE OFFICER pulls out his taser and HENRY falls to the ground.)*

**HENRY**
Isaac. I need Isaac.

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 5: “America’s Grocery Store”</p>

*(Scene opens inside a grocery store. HENRY and ISAAC have a cart full of groceries and a bottle of wine at the cash register. There is a long line of people behind HENRY and ISAAC. The next person in line looks very impatient.)*

**CASHIER**
Can I see some ID?

*(HENRY takes out his wallet and gives it to CASHIER.)*

**CASHIER**
I’m sorry. You’re only 20, so I ain’t gonna sell ya this bottle of wine.

**HENRY**
We just want to have a nice dinner. My birthday is in one week. I can legally drink with my parents in some states. It’s legal in most countries to be able to drink alcohol before the age of 21. Why is this country any different? Aren’t we the land of the free? I guess we’re the land of the free except for a ton of exceptions that the government gets to regulate!

**CASHIER**
I’m sorry, darlin’, but I’m just tryin’a do my job. I could get the shaft for sellin’ ya this. I have three kids at home that I need to feed and you just ain’t worth me losin’ my job ova.

*(CASHIER talks into a microphone.)*

Can I get a manager to register 7 please?

**ISAAC**
Henry, we just have to listen to them. You can wait one more week. We can have a nice dinner without wine tonight and it’ll be fine.

*(To CASHIER.)*

We can do without the wine, thank you. I would like to pay with a credit card.

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 6: “Abe’s Cabin”</p>

*(The scene opens in front of an old-fashioned log cabin. HENRY and ISAAC enter the scene and knock on the door of the cabin. ABE LINCOLN comes out of the cabin.)*

**HENRY**
Hello Abraham. We are here for your dinner party. I’m really excited to pick your mind regarding freedom in the United States of America.

**LINCOLN**
I am truly honored you made it, Henry. Who is this gentleman accompanying you?

**HENRY**
Oh, this is my significant other, Isaac. We met in middle school when we were studying you during the Civil War. You are a real idol.

**LINCOLN** *(appalled)*
Well, Henry, my apologies, but I cannot allow you into my home. What you are doing is unnatural and does not follow religious beliefs. You need to reconsider your choices in life. I am going to have to ask you to depart in this current moment. I did not fight for American freedom to give rights to those who do what is unnatural to human species.

*(LINCOLN shuts the cabin door. HENRY and ISAAC stand in front of the door, speechless.)*

**HENRY**
I… he… it… No! This isn’t right! Lincoln! You fought for freedom! You’re supposed to be on our side! You can’t go around and tell people how to live their lives! You fucking hypocrite!

*(HENRY cries.)*

Why can’t people be free to do as they please! I am 25 years old and should be able to live as I want!

**ISAAC** *(consoling Henry)*
Henry, you really need to stop this. You cannot control everything that happens. Other people make rules in this society and we must follow them. We will deal with this together, like we always have.

**HENRY**
It’s not fair. All I want is freedom to do as I please and be in control of my own life. How can I have control over my life if other people are continuously taking it away?

**ISAAC**
I don’t know, Henry. I just don’t know.

<p class="scene-heading">Scene 8: “Isaac’s Henry”</p>

*(The scene opens to a studio apartment. The room looks as though the inhabitants attempted to make the room look nice, but the space itself just won’t allow anything to look right. HENRY sits at a desk with a note in one hand and a picture frame in the other. There is a handgun sitting on the desk. HENRY sets the picture frame face down on the desk with the note on top. HENRY looks at the gun. Pause. HENRY picks up the gun and feels it in his hands. HENRY takes a deep breath and brings the gun towards his head. ISAAC enters the room holding a briefcase, keys, and a jacket. ISAAC sees HENRY at the desk. ISAAC drops everything in his hands and runs to HENRY, taking the gun. ISAAC falls to his knees, unloads the gun, and stares at it. HENRY holds his head in his hands. ISAAC takes out his phone and dials 911. No voice is detected.)*

**HENRY**
Why?

**ISAAC**
“Why”? What gives you the right in this moment in time to ask *me* why? I should be asking *you* why!

**HENRY**
Why not?

**ISAAC**
Because you love me. Because so many people love you. Because I love you. Because life will get better, it just takes time…

**HENRY**
Life will get better? Are you blind? We’ve been saying since middle school that life would get better. We’re fucking 33 years old now and life is still *shit.* We can’t live anywhere nice. We can’t go anywhere nice. We can’t keep good jobs. We can’t even start a family. How is life going to get better? How? Because it sure as hell doesn’t seem like it’ll happen during our lifetime.

**ISAAC**
We just need to try. We just need to follow the system. We just need to work together and…

**HENRY**
No! I’m tired of you and your “system.” I’m tired of waiting for things to get better. I’m tired of being told what to do just to be rejected and criticized for doing what I want. But now? Now I’ve figured it out, Isaac. Now *I* can have control. The only way for me to do what I want is to just make everything stop. No one can tell me what to do if there is no one around to reject me and criticize me. I’m the one making up the rules. I can make all the outside voices shut the fuck up!

**ISAAC**
No. I can’t let that happen, Henry. You can’t just get out of life so easily! That’s not how it works! You live life and you suffer and *then* you die, just like everyone else. You try to make the best out of the good things you have in life. Like us. But that’s never been good enough for you. I try to let you do what you want to do. I work my ass off so you can follow your dreams, but I’m so tired. I’m tired of working all the time to support *you*. I’m tired of worrying about you all the time. I just can’t live this way anymore!

**HENRY**
Then let me go. Let me finally find my freedom.

**ISAAC**
I can’t. That’s not how life works.

*(There is a knock at the door. ISAAC picks up the gun and opens the door. POLICE OFFICERS enter the studio. ISAAC points towards HENRY without lifting his head.)*

I’m sorry, Henry.

**HENRY**
No, Isaac. No. I can’t get locked up. I just can’t!

*(POLICE OFFICERS grab HENRY and restrain him. HENRY fights back.)*

**ISAAC**
I don’t know how else to protect you! The doctors are there to help.

*(POLICE OFFICERS force HENRY out the door.)*

**HENRY**
The only thing that can protect me is letting me go! I don’t want help!

*(HENRY is taken out of the door. ISAAC is left in the room alone.)*

**ISAAC** *(to himself)*
But that’s not how freedom works.
