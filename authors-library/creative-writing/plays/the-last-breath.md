# The Last Breath

**Course / Context:** One-act play written for a playwriting course; workshopped with actors from the University of South Dakota theatre department as a partially staged reading  
**Date:** December 15, 2017 (draft)  

---

<p class="content-note"><strong>Content note:</strong> a suicide attempt, its aftermath in a hospital ICU, end-of-life decisions, and grief.</p>

<p class="piece-note">All names in the play have been changed.</p>

### Artist Statement

*The Last Breath* was written to showcase the emotions a family goes through during the death of a loved one. The play was written in the Fall of 2017 as part of a playwriting course. The play was then workshopped with actors from the University of South Dakota theatre department and put up for a partially staged reading. The play also discusses the struggle of someone who is hurting and what can happen to them when they lose their will to live. Many plays regarding suicide deal with the actions and feelings of the person who attempts to commit suicide. My play gives multiple perspectives through the course of the play depicting people with different relationships saying their final goodbyes, one of the most difficult situations one may have to experience.

<strong>Daniel</strong> – Brother to Thomas, in middle school
<strong>Steve</strong> – Father to Thomas, early 40s

**Playwright note:** There are to be no sounds of a heart monitor in the production of this script.

**Setting:** Small room in the ICU of a hospital with a window.

*(Blackout.)*

*(**1:23pm.** Slam of a door. A frustrated cry followed by a deep breath.)*

*(**1:27pm.** Sound of a light knock on a door and a door handle jiggle.)*

**LAURA**
Thomas?

*(The shaking of a belt buckle.)*

*(**1:37pm.** Sound of a knock on a door.)*

**LAURA** *(worried)*
Thomas, can you at least tell me you’re okay?

*(**1:40pm.** Sound of a screw driver at work and then a door opening. LAURA gasps and whimpers as if in shock.)*

**LAURA**
Daniel! Help me get him down and call 9-1-1!

**DANIEL**
Hi… uh… we need an ambulance at…

*(DANIEL’s voice is drowned out by the sound of LAURA performing CPR.)*

*(**1:45pm.** Sirens are heard. The movement of large furniture.)*

**PERSON**
He’s unresponsive. Stabilize the neck.

*(Sound of PERSON performing CPR.)*

**PERSON**
We cannot risk driving him in an ambulance. He might not make it. We need to land the helicopter here.

*(**1:50pm.** Sound of a helicopter landing and then it drones out.)*

**PERSON** *(heartfelt.)*
Laura, when I saw the helicopter land on our street, I knew something awful had happened. I don’t know what is going on, but I want you to know that you are in my prayers.

*(**3:21pm.** Lights up on Thomas in a hospital bed. Thomas is hooked up to a respirator that is helping him breathe. The sound of the respirator is heard. Thomas has on a neck brace and is in a hospital gown. Thomas is unresponsive. LAURA, STEVE, and DANIEL are around Thomas. LAURA holds Thomas's hand. STEVE stares out a window. DANIEL sits on the floor with his head in his hands, unable to understand that his brother is lying in a hospital bed. ANNA enters in shock. She waits by the entrance and then slowly approaches Thomas.)*

**LAURA** *(to Thomas)*
Hey, Thomas. Anna is here now. *(To Anna)* You can take his hand if you want. The nurse said he can still feel peoples’ touch.

*(LAURA moves Thomas’s hair away from his forehead.)*

**LAURA** *(cont.)*
You can talk to him too. *(sniffle)* They say he can still understand you.

**ANNA** *(hesitantly)*
Tom? Thomas? Hey, T. I’m here. I’m here for you.

*(ANNA, with shaky hands, takes Thomas’s hand.)*

*(**4:49.** DANIEL sits in a chair, looking away from Thomas, angry that he has attempted to take his own life. . ANNA kneels on the floor, still holding Thomas’s hand. LAURA is on the opposite side of Thomas than the children. STEVE is still by the window.)*

**LAURA** *(looking at Thomas)*
You know, I don’t think other people really realized how bad it is… No one really wants to know.

**ANNA**
Mom, I just talked to him this morning. I… I didn’t even know he…

*(ANNA struggles to talk. LAURA goes over to ANNA and comforts her.)*

**LAURA**
I know sweetie. He didn’t want you to know. He didn’t want people to worry. He keeps so much bottled up that we will never even begin to comprehend. He is the last thing he wants you to worry about. He cares so much about you.

*(**5:38pm.** LAURA sits in a chair next to DANIEL. ANNA stands next to DANIEL, who appears angry. The three stare at Thomas who is now propped up on one side to prevent bed sores. LAURA puts a hand on DANIEL’s takes his shoulder away from LAURA, not wanting attention. STEVE enters with GRANDMA. GRANDMA shaky, hugs STEVE.)*

**ANNA**
Hey, T. Grandma is here to see you and say hi.

*(ANNA sits next to DANIEL. GRANDMA goes up to Thomas’s side and strokes his hair.)*

**GRANDMA**
Tommy? Hi Tommy, it’s… it’s Grandma. Can you hear me?

**LAURA**
The nurse says that he can still hear you.

**GRANDMA** *(voice is shaky)*
Tommy, you are one of grandma’s good-lookin’ guys. Tommy. We all love you so much. Can you let me see those big brown eyes?

*(GRANDMA kisses Thomas’s forehead and then takes Thomas’s hand.)*

**GRANDMA** *(cont.)*
Let’s all hold hands and all say the Lord’s Prayer.

*(Family all take hands. **6:00pm.** GRANDMA sits next to DANIEL in a chair. DANIEL looks at Thomas. DANIEL looks sad and drained. ANNA sits on the floor. STEVE looks at Thomas from behind. LAURA enters.)*

**LAURA**
The neurologist came back with the results from the CT scan. He said that… it doesn’t look good. Thomas’s oxygen was cut off for too long and there are parts of his brain that are swelling. He said that he probably isn’t going to make it through the night.

*(**6:45pm.** All exit. Following lines said while PERSON comes and does a checkup that includes checking his pupillary reaction.)*

**STEVE** *(to audience)*
It was a summer night, and I was working the evening shift when I got the call. Your mother called to say that she was on her way to the hospital because you were about to be born. At that moment, my world seemed to turn upside down. You took your time to meet me though and made me wait until the following day to become a part of my life. Years later, on that same night, I got the call that you had attempted to take your life. My world seemed to turn upside down. But I was given another day to spend with you until I had to say goodbye on your birthday.

*(**7:27pm.** ANNA and LAURA reenter. ANNA stands by Thomas and holds Thomas’s left hand while her other hand in on Thomas’s left leg. LAURA looks over at ANNA.)*

**ANNA**
His leg is convulsing… He must be practicing soccer.

**LAURA**
*(slight smile)* It’s his left leg too. He must be practicing his left side kicks. You remember when he spent a whole summer practicing his left side because his soccer coach told him he would be a more valued player if he could play left field? He would leave in the morning, take his own lunch with him, and not come back until dinner. He would even go if it was pouring rain. *(shakes her head, pause.)* It hurt my heart the day he quit…

*(ANNA motions to Thomas’s left hand as though it was also twitching.)*

**ANNA**
He must be practicing taking pictures too. He is so great at photography. He has just the perfect eye for it.

**LAURA**
His counselor suggested that Thomas should keep a journal, so we got him one, but he never wrote in it. I think his photography was his way of journaling. Taking photos of nature helped him see the world through a different lens. It helped him see something that was… actually beautiful, you know?

*(**8:22pm.** Family is in the room except STEVE. Steve enters.)*

**STEVE**
They said that his pupils reacted and that it isn’t normal for a brain that has swollen like Thomas’s. They said he should make it through the night and they are going to send him in for another CT scan tomorrow morning as soon as the neurologist comes back in.

*(**12:00am.** All family is in room. DANIEL is sitting in a chair by the door looking at the floor.)*

**ALL EXCEPT DANIEL** *(gentle whispers, singing)*
Happy birthday to you. Happy birthday to you. Happy birthday, dear Thomas. Happy birthday to you.

*(GRANDMA goes to DANIEL and leads him off stage.)*

*(**1:42am.** LAURA, ANNA, and STEVE are onstage. STEVE is in a chair and his eyes are closed.)*

**LAURA**
After things got so bad the first time, Daddy and I would alternate nights sleeping with Thomas to make sure he was okay. Eventually it got better and Thomas didn’t want us to do that… but there were still nights that he would come upstairs like he used to when he was little. He would ask me to come downstairs and scratch his back to help him fall asleep. I would go downstairs and sing to him, try to comfort him and calm him down enough so he could fall asleep…. That’s all I could do. I felt so helpless. He said his mind just couldn’t stop racing. He would ask me to reassure him that everything was going to be okay… So I did. *(To Thomas, stroking his head.)* Everything is going to be okay, Thomas, don’t you worry. Mom’s right here…

*(**3:15am.** LAURA sleeps in a chair holding Thomas’s hand. ANNA is asleep on the floor against the wall. Following lines are said while PERSON props up Thomas on other side.)*

**STEVE** *(to audience)*
Thank you for being such a great son and know that as much as I tried in life to teach you, I also learned from you. Thank you for being a great brother to Anna and Daniel. I know they couldn’t have asked for a better brother. Know that the joy they brought to you, you also brought to them.

*(**8:15am.** LAURA and ANNA are on both sides of Thomas. STEVE stands by LAURA.)*

**LAURA**
His hands are so tense after his CT scan. Thomas, you can relax now. We’re back in the room sweetheart.

*(LAURA and ANNA massage Thomas’s hands. LAURA motions to ANNA.)*

**LAURA** *(cont. to ANNA)*
Do you remember that video you made for him in the third grade when he was the star of the week?

*(ANNA nods, remembering.)*

**ANNA**
I compiled all of these pictures of him and put them to his favorite songs like the SpongeBob SquarePants Theme song and Cheeseburger in Paradise.

**LAURA**
He really appreciated it… Then he came home, devastated because of one picture that you included of him cleaning a pair of pants in the sink. We didn’t fully understand what was wrong because it was just one picture… Even in third grade he was carrying so much… and we didn’t even know it. It all just kept on building up over the years. It got so bad in high school that there were days I had to call the school counselor to go to his truck and help him actually get inside the school.

*(**9:15am.** GRANDMA and DANIEL enter. DANIEL appears at ease. He is ready for what is to come. DANIEL goes over to Thomas and looks him in the face, mentally saying goodbye. GRANDMA accompanies DANIEL.)*

**GRANDMA** *(to LAURA)*
Do we have an update?

**LAURA**
Not yet. They said they should get us the results in about half an hour.

*(**10:20am.**)*

**LAURA** *(to audience)*
We are still waiting on what the neurologist has to say about his CT scan this morning.

*(**11:58am.**)*

**STEVE** *(to audience)*
The neurologist was supposed to be here as soon as he could.

*(**1:45pm.**)*

**GRANDMA** *(to audience)*
The neurologist had to help with a different case that was a little more urgent.

*(**2:27pm.**)*

**ANNA** *(to audience)*
We’re still waiting on the CT scan results from this morning.

*(**3:08pm.**)*

**PERSON**
So I heard you had a question regarding the CT scan this morning?

**LAURA** *(frustrated)*
No… We would *like* to know the results from the CT scan from *this morning* that was supposed to take *half an hour.* *(calms down.)* If you could kindly give us an update on his condition, that would be great.

**PERSON** *(not empathetic)*
Well, the results from last night still stand. Thomas is either going to stay in this state or his brain will continue to swell, and he will slowly pass.

*(**3:31.** All have exited the room. ANNA remains.)*

**ANNA** *(to Thomas)*
I’m standing here, looking at you. I don’t think I’ve ever held your hand for so long. You were always on the go and had things to do. *(scoff laugh)* I don’t think you know how much I appreciated those late night talks that we had. You just always understood what I was going through because we both felt as though we needed to live up to such high expectations… High school is such a terrible time… You just can’t fully conceptualize it until you’ve left and you are *so* close, T. I don’t know why, but there is this song that keeps going through my mind all day… I’ve been trying to get the courage to sing it to you, but I just can’t do it. I’m not nearly as strong as you are. *(deep breath)* Now, if I sing it, you have to promise to not make fun of me. Okay? I’ve been out of choir for three years, I haven’t warmed up, and I’ve been crying. *(Pause, ANNA holds Thomas’s hand and looks at Thomas, attempting to sing while holding back tears.)* You are my sunshine. My only sunshine. You make me happy when skies are grey. You’ll never know dear, how much I love you. So please don’t take my sunshine away. *(Quieter.)* Please don’t take my sunshine away. *(Whisper, not sung.)* Please don’t take my sunshine away. Thomas. Can you just open your eyes? *(pause.)* Tom? Squeeze my hand, just a little? *(shaky breath in, trying to stabilize.)* God, I’ve never wanted something so much in my life.

*(ANNA breaks down crying. She falls to her knees, still holding onto Thomas’s hand. GRANDMA and STEVE enter. STEVE stands there and stares at his son. Slowly, STEVE feels guilty. GRANDMA goes to and holds ANNA.)*

**ANNA** *(cont.)*
Tom, Grandma and Daddy are here. Grandma, I can’t do it. I can’t let go. I just can’t. I’m not strong enough.

**GRANDMA**
Oh, my gorgeous gal. You don’t need to be strong. I’m glad you’re crying, you wanna know why?

*(GRANDMA looks ANNA in the eyes.)*

**GRANDMA** *(cont.)*
Because if you hold it in, it hurts even more.

*(GRANDMA hugs ANNA.)*

**GRANDMA** *(cont.)*
I’m going to go get your Mom for you and I’ll be right back.

*(GRANDMA exits.)*

**ANNA** *(to Thomas)*
Don’t worry, T. She’ll be right back.

**STEVE** *(holds back tears)*
Oh, God. What have I done?

*(STEVE holds his head in his hands and cries. DANIEL, GRANDMA, and LAURA enter. DANIEL sits, content and at ease. LAURA hugs her husband and then goes to ANNA. GRANDMA comforts STEVE. LAURA holds ANNA.)*

**LAURA**
It’s time to say goodbye darlin’.

**ANNA**
Mom, I can’t.

**LAURA**
It’s time to get up. The nurses are coming in to remove his tubes. We are going to let nature take its course.

*(LAURA puts her hand over ANNA’S. The sound of the respirator is heard. LAURA and ANNA stand and go over next to GRANDMA, STEVE, and DANIEL. LAURA and GRANDMA stand by STEVE as ANNA holds DANIEL’s hand. DANIEL accepts his sister’s hand.)*

*(**6:56pm.** Everyone looks at Thomas. PERSON enters the room and is seen grabbing the breathing tube as lights fade. The respirator sound stops. The sounds of a person struggling to breathe is heard. Silence.)*

**STEVE**
Thomas I want you to know that I understand that you are no longer in any pain. If the pain you always struggled with and so badly wanted me to understand was at all like the pain I feel now, know that you are one of the strongest people I have ever known. I love you Thomas.

**ANNA**
I will always remember
The rhythm of your
feet scaling the steps as you came to dinner.

I will always remember
The rhythm of your
Respirator, making sure you stayed alive long enough for me to say goodbye.

I will always remember
Your legs move as you
Fooled other players to get the soccer ball past them.

I will always remember
Your leg muscles contracting
In the hospital bed as I imagined you were dreaming of practicing soccer again.

I will always remember
Your deep breaths you took
To steady yourself to take the perfect picture.

I will always remember
Your last shaky breath you took before going to
Climb the stairs to heaven,
Play soccer with the angels,
And capture the most breath-taking pictures
In heaven.

*(LAURA reads a poem written by a relative.)*

*(END OF PLAY)*
