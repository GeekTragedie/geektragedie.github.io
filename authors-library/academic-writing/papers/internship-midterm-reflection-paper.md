# Internship Midterm Reflection Paper

**Degree / Program:** AIH Certificate  
**Course / Context:** Arts in Health internship (Sanford Arts), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** Spring 2018  

---

<p class="piece-note">Site and unit details have been generalized; no patient information or staff names are included.</p>

<div class="paper">

<p class="paper-title">Internship Midterm Reflection Paper</p>

I have been interning at Sanford Arts for about two months and am very glad I have had the opportunity to work there thus far. Working with my supervisor, co-workers, and fellow interns has been a great experience and I believe we have all learned from each other. I appreciate the ability to have two other interns working alongside me, so we can all learn from each other because we all started at the same time. Through this internship process thus far, I feel as though I have grown in my abilities and achieved multiple milestones though I still believe there are many ways I can still grow in my abilities.

## Overview of the Site

At my site, I have had the privilege of learning under expressive arts therapists and artists in residence (AIR). Learning different techniques of how different AIR interact with patients is very interesting. Each AIR has their own invitation strategy and their own modality to match. It has been very interesting to see the different modalities at work and the various strengths each modality contains.

Each week I have readings needed to be completed regarding the following topics: symbolism, neuropathy, chemo brain, arts in healthcare techniques, and the difference between AIR and art therapists. After doing the readings, I select a topic of interest to me and find my own article. I believe this requirement helps my research abilities, but also helps spark interest in the topics discussed in the readings.

Having the opportunity to come up with my own project ideas has also been a great experience as well. I have been able to work with other interns to create projects and have also worked on my own to create projects as well. It is through the process of working on my own that I believe I have been able to learn the most using the process of trial and error. While it is one experience to learn in a classroom environment, it is completely different when applying that knowledge to the floor. I believe this internship has helped me connect my knowledge learned in class to the practice and field of arts in medicine. Through the various experiences I have had at my internship, I have noticed my abilities have grown.

## Areas of Growth

I have grown in my abilities as an arts in healthcare professional through my internship experience. I believe I have better communication skills when working with diverse populations. While all the populations I have worked with are cancer patients and family members, there are different populations within this group such as diverse ages, races, ethnicities, and socioeconomic statuses. I believe I have grown in my knowledge of adapting to working with different populations and this experience has helped me grow. Not only has my communication skills grown through working with different populations, it has also grown by explaining my ideas to others. Before I bring a project onto the floor, I pitch my idea to the other AIRs. By pitching my ideas, I am able to receive feedback from others and learn how to adapt that feedback to my project, but I am also able to communicate my ideas to others in a professional setting.

I also feel as though I have grown in my knowledge of material usage. Throughout my time at the cancer center, I feel as though I have had the ability to explore more materials than I was able to in my Material Use and Safety course. There are a wider variety of materials at the site I am able to use to create different pieces. I have also received advice from my site supervisor as to which materials are better to use together. One example of this during my internship is when I was putting gesso on tiles. The gessoed tiles then were decorated with an aspect from a story. At first, I used permanent marker, but once I was using it on the floor, participants were pressing harder on the gessoed tile than I was, and the gesso was getting rubbed off. I could have used paint, but paint is more intimidating to others, so I asked my supervisor what she would use. She recommended I use paint markers because it would look like a permanent marker but stay on the tile easier. After her recommendation, I used the paint markers and they worked perfectly for the project. I have also learned from other people’s projects materials that work well.

One other area of growth I have been able to witness is my ability to adapt my projects to different populations. I used an improvisation game with both children in the cancer unit and also with elders in the rehabilitation center. The children had more energy and were able to move around more; however, the elders were less able to move, and some were less able to fully comprehend what was being said. I learned to be flexible and adapt the way the game was played to adhere to both populations.

## Areas to Improve

While I have seen many areas of growth, I also believe there are areas that can use improvement. One area that can be improved is my ability to use my theatre modality with people in the waiting rooms. I have started playing theatre games with people in the children’s wing and in the rehabilitation center and it went well! However, I have yet to bring these games to other areas of the hospital. I do believe that the success I had in the other areas increases my aspiration to bring my theatre activities other places.

I also believe I can grow more in being independent in my projects. I have seen growth in the number of people served while being in a group, but I feel as though I have used it more as a crutch. I feel I need to be more confident in my abilities to do projects by myself, though I find it difficult to do theatre projects alone.

One final area I would like to improve upon is my ability to keep energized throughout the day. After a full morning or full afternoon on the floor, I am usually very exhausted and drained. I would like to try to find some sort of self-care tactic I can do on the floor, so I do not feel this way at the end of the day.

## Update on Internship Goals

I had four goals upon entering this internship experience at the cancer center: one, learn about the populations I am working with at Sanford Arts; two, create my own projects and bring them to those at Sanford Arts; three, keep self-care tactics throughout the semester to ensure my own health; and four, be confident in my theatre abilities throughout the internship. I feel as though I have certainly increased my knowledge regarding the populations I work with at the cancer center with the help of my supervisor’s readings and through observation of the patients while I work with them. I also feel as though I have been achieving my goal of creating my own projects and bringing them to the floor by doing exactly what my goal states.

While I feel confident I am achieving my first two goals, I feel as though my last two goals can receive more attention. I am keeping a consistent counseling schedule and am actually receiving enough sleep each night, but I have not kept a consistent work out schedule. I also feel as though these objectives are not enough to fulfill this goal anymore. I feel as though I need to add more to my self-care plan. I think I need to practice more mindfulness and add mindfulness stretching to my schedule. I feel as though these actions will help me stay in tune with my body and help me overall.

I also feel as though I need to be more confident in my theatre abilities in my internship. I want to do more theatre activities on the floor but have only created two theatre projects. I fully believe that I need to find more theatre activities for patients in the waiting rooms to participate in, as stated in above in my areas of growth needed to be improved.

## Needs from Supervisors

I feel as though my supervisors are really helping me prepare for my future. While I sometimes become frustrated with all of the expectations placed on me, I feel as though they will help me in the future. I believe my only need from my supervisors is reassurance at the moment that I am going down the correct path, if I am. With my lack of theatre confidence, I sometimes wonder if I am doing the right activities. I find using theatre rather difficult and I think I hesitate more because most of my supervisors do not have theatre as a modality. While I do not believe my supervisors are not equipped to help me, I think this reaction makes me feel as though I would like more reassurance that my projects and ideas are good.

## Conclusion

Overall, I have really loved this internship experience and am happy to have had the opportunity to participate and be at my site twenty hours a week. Having had multiple graduate school interviews now, I feel much more prepared to enter this field than other undergraduate students I have met at my interviews. I look forward to the rest of the semester and what I will learn along the way.
