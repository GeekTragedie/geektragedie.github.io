<p class="paper-title">Artist Statement</p>

I thought of aspects of my life that I deem important, part of my life, or wish were a part of my life. Then I ordered them based on how important they are to my life at the moment. I then thought of a way that I could connect these ideas together. So, I thought of how I expressed myself in my last art piece; I then enhanced and elaborated this idea. I had to think about the relationship between the different things in my life and create a connection between them instead of just thinking. I realized that the aspects that I had written all pertain to things that I did not put into my mind by myself, so I knew I had to include an aspect of someone else giving me these aspects. So, I started my artist piece knowing that I was going to combine the aspects of myself in the way that I had created my art before, and include someone else giving me the parts.

For my first piece, representing the now, I decided to do a head representing myself, like I had done with my previous art piece. I decided to write in the words that are aspects of myself because I am a logical person. To incorporate another party giving me the aspects, I added the hand at the top to give the allusion of someone dropping these things into my mind. Through the organization of my words, the things that are more centered are the items that are higher priority or more important to me now. The items towards the edges are things that I feel are not as centered as other items. Items that are falling are things that maybe should be important, but are being lost for me at the moment. The head is being threated of falling into a black and blue abyss or coming out of the abyss. The idea of “Future” being dropped into my full mind is a looming idea that has not been concretely added, but is threatening to be there. This is why it is not completely connected to the rest of the words.

I chose chalk to do this piece in because I have not had the chance to use it often. I wanted to be able to mix colors together and blend. I also wanted to use my hands more, because I wanted to be more involved in my piece. I chose specific colors for each word that I felt represented that word. “Health” is in red for the representation of the hospital. “Gender” is blue and pink because of the gender binary; however, this changes to a neutral green for the future. This is the only idea that changed color.

For my second piece, I wanted to have the more control over the aspects of my mind. I wanted to be the person with the ideas in my own hands with the idea that I was giving the ideas to other people. I did not want to be dropping the items into other people’s minds, as people have done to me. I wanted to nicely hand these ideas to other people.

I combined my two pieces together with a video that showed my mental process of my now to my future. I want to be able to hold the words in my hands in the future. To do this, I need to collect the ideas that are there now and then hold them in my hands. My video shows me collecting the words and then holding them in my hands as an offering.

This process connects to arts in health through personal thought, creativity, and self-expression. A person has to think about where they are now and know the self. The artist should understand how these aspects of connect and play into who they are. The person then must think of who they want to be in the future, or the ideal self. These two ideas take a lot of thought process and personal knowledge or self-concept. It is important for a person to understand who they are, so they can be more calm and centered, personally, to deal with the events that happen in the healthcare facility. The process evokes creativity because a person must then take these ideas of the self and ideal self and must portray them through art. It takes creative thought to choose which modality to use and then how to connect the two art pieces together. This project enhances self-expression through the connection of personal thought and creativity. A person must take their self-understanding and express it in a creative way.
