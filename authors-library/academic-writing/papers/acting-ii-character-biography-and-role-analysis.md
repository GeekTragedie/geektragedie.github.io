# Acting II Character Biography and Role Analysis

**Degree / Program:** BFA in Theatre  
**Course / Context:** Acting II, University of South Dakota  
**Date:** Spring 2014  

---

<p class="piece-note">Written in character as Amanda Van Helsing, a gender-flipped Van Helsing in a stage version of <em>Dracula</em>.</p>

## Character Biography

**Name.** My name is Amanda Van Helsing.

**Heredity.**

- Physical- I am medium height have a curlier hair. I was born with poor vision and need glasses. I’m not skinny, but I’m not large.
- Mental- I was born very smart and witty. I have a knack for leadership and a way of getting people to understand me quickly.

**Race.** I am Caucasian.

**Ethnic heritage.** I am Dutch English.

**Environment: where am I?** I am in Purley, England. I am in the library in Dr. Seward’s Sanatorium. There is a fireplace, a bookcase, a couch, big double doors, and two armchairs, one with a desk and one without.

**Date, day, and time.** It is an evening in early fall.

**Occupation.** I am a professor specializing in diseases.

**Social status.** I am a higher social status because of my education level and my occupation, especially for being a woman in my position.

**Political orientation.** *(left blank)*

**Religious orientation.** I am a loose Christian. I do not firmly believe in set ways like Catholics and Lutherans, but I still believe in God.

**Relationship to the people I am talking to.**

- Well I was Dr. Seward’s teacher long ago and we have been friends ever since. We are old friends that know each other well and have each other’s backs. I feel as though he trusts me a lot, enough to trust me with Lucy’s life. I feel as though we would do anything for each other.
- I do not know Renfield at all. All I know is that he is an interesting specimen that I would love to know more about. I find him fascinating and want to know more about him. I also believe that he knows a lot of information about what is happening to Lucy and want to continue talking to him to gain this information.

**Temperament.** I am very smart and people know that I am. I observe and gain as many clues as I can before acting. I plan ahead to make sure everything goes smoothly. I also have a way of letting people know that I am in charge without specifically saying it. I have a take-charge personality that I feel is important.

**Health.** I am very healthy. Swift on my feet and have good control of my body.

**Age.** I am 45 years old and act this way as well. I act mature and am full of knowledge and wisdom.

**Physical appearance.** I have wavy-curly hair and wear glasses. I have professional looking clothing to show my intelligence to those around me, especially because I am a sophisticated woman in this time period. I wear a suit coat with a neutral colored undershirt, black pants, and a small heel to show my superiority. I have a watch to show that my time is important. I have light make up that can go unnoticed easily.

**Movement style.** I have a very smooth way of moving that contrasts with my take-charge stature. My posture is very aligned and mighty. I have small gestures that are to the point. I have high energy that is built up and released at certain times.

**Speech style.** I have a very sophisticated way of talking and have a way of getting to the point of what I want to say quickly. I get my point across fast.

## Role Analysis

### 1. Environment of the scene

**Place.**

- I am in Purley, England. I am in the library in Dr. Seward’s Sanatorium. There is a fireplace, a bookcase, a couch, big double doors, and two armchairs, one with a desk and one without.
- The significance of this place is that it seems very welcoming and intellectual. I feel relaxed in this environment and also feel at home because I am a professor and this surrounding makes me feel at ease.

**Temperature and weather.** The weather is not too hot, but not too cold. It is early fall, so it is coming out of summer, but not too close to winter. It is windy outside to add an eeriness.

**Time.** It is September in the early to mid-1900s. It is fall and the days are shorter while the nights are longer. This adds to the exigency for me to figure out what is wrong because I find out during the scene that everything bad happens at night.

### 2. The moment before

I just arrived from Holland and just finished being welcomed by Dr. Seward and introduced to Harker. I also am just getting introduced to one of Dr. Seward’s patients, Renfield. I finished getting caught up on Lucy’s case and am looking for clues to what has happened to her.

### 3. Sensory reality and physical condition

- The sensory reality present is an eeriness created by Renfield. The eeriness is painted on thick enough to feel.
- My physical condition is alert. I feel, hear, and see everything that is going on. I have very good posture so that my body is alert as well. I am very engaged in my surroundings and those around me.

### 4. My role in the scene

**Goal.** I want more information about everything that is going on. I want to figure out what is going on around me with this information so I can figure out what I need to do to fix and save Lucy.

**Obstacle.** Seward is in my way by trying to make Renfield leave. I need to get my information from Renfield and Seward is trying to make him leave. However, Renfield will not tell me any information either.

**Tactics.** I am using observation to listen and understand everything that is being said. I use smaller sentences to get my point across to other so they do not become confused with what I am trying to do. It also makes the words I say more important because I do not say as much. I also use a forcefulness to make Seward know that I am superior and that I know what I am doing.

**Expectation.** I will have control over everyone so that my plan that I create will work out smoothly. I get all the information I need to solve the mystery of Lucy and save the day. I will gain popularity from my amazing discovery and save many lives from future harm. I will be a hero.

### 5. Super objective (spine) for the whole play

My overall goal is to save Lucy and prevent this dangerous disease from happening in the future.
