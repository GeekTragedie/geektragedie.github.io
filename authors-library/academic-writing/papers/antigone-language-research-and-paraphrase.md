## Part I: Language Research

### Vengeance

- The act of avenging oneself or another; retributive infliction of injury or punishment; hurt or harm done from vindictive motives (OED Online, 2018).

### Bull-headed

- Having a massive head, broadheaded; fig. blindly impetuous, blockheaded (OED Online, 2018).

### Bitterness

- To the mind or feelings
- Deep sorrow or anguish of heart (OED Online, 2018).

### Brutal

- Inhuman; coarsely cruel, savage, fierce (OED Online, 2018).

### Happiness

- The quality or condition of being happy.
- The state of pleasurable contentment of mind; deep pleasure in or contentment with one's circumstances (OED Online, 2018).

### Bear

- I. to carry; II. to sustain; III. to thrust, press; IV. to bring forth (OED Online, 2018).

### Calamity

- The state or condition of grievous affliction or adversity; deep distress, trouble, or misery, arising from some adverse circumstance or event (OED Online, 2018).

### Marriage bed

- The bed used by a married couple; (hence) the sexual relationship of a husband and wife. Frequently used in fig. context with reference to marital fidelity (OED Online, 2018).

### Fear

- In Old English: A sudden and terrible event; peril.
- The emotion of pain or uneasiness caused by the sense of impending danger, or by the prospect of some possible evil.
- An instance of the emotion; a particular apprehension of some future evil.
- A state of alarm or dread.
- A mingled feeling of dread and reverence towards God (formerly also, towards any rightful authority) (OED Online, 2018).

### Wretched

- Living in a state of misery, poverty, or degradation; sunk in distress or dejection; very miserable or unhappy (OED Online, 2018).

### Condemns

- To pronounce an adverse judgement on; to express strong disapproval of, censure, blame.
- To give judicial sentence against; to find guilty, convict.
- To pronounce guilty of
- To doom or devote to some (unkind) fate or condition; in pass. to be doomed by fate to some condition or to do something (OED Online, 2018).

### Fate

- The principle, power, or agency by which, according to certain philosophical and popular systems of belief, all events, or some events in particular, are unalterably predetermined from eternity (OED Online, 2018).

### Misfortunes

- (n) Adverse fortune; bad luck.
- (v) To come to grief, miscarry (OED Online, 2018).

### Evil

- Morally depraved, bad, wicked, vicious.
- Doing or tending to do harm; hurtful, mischievous, prejudicial.
- Causing discomfort, pain, or trouble; unpleasant, offensive, disagreeable; troublesome, painful.
- Of conditions, fortune, etc., also (rarely) of persons: Unfortunate, miserable, wretched (OED Online, 2018).

### Kindness

- As a count noun: an instance of this; a kind or courteous deed or act; a good turn, a favour; (also) a gift
- Fondness, affection, or love for someone or something (OED Online, 2018).

### Evades

- To get away, escape
- To escape by contrivance or artifice from (attack, pursuit, adverse designs; an assailant, pursuer, or adversary); to avoid, save oneself from (a threatened evil or inconvenience); to elude (a blow), avoid encountering (an obstacle) (OED Online, 2018).

### Cursed

- To utter against (persons or things) words which consign, or are intended or supposed to consign, them to evil spiritual or temporal, as the wrath of God or the malignity of fate; to damn.
- That has had a curse pronounced or invoked upon him or it; excommunicated, anathematized; under a curse, blasted with a curse. (OED Online, 2018).

### Wisdom

- Capacity of judging rightly in matters relating to life and conduct; soundness of judgement in the choice of means and ends; sometimes, less strictly, sound sense, esp. in practical affairs (OED Online, 2018).

## References and Allusions

### Hades

- The oldest name of the god of the dead, also called Pluto.
- The kingdom of Hades, the lower world, the abode of departed spirits or shades (OED Online, 2018).

## Part II. Paraphrase

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    Here comes the king, troubled
    By the information that is obvious to me:
    His grief wasn’t created by someone else’s actions
    Am I allowed to say? – except his father’s fault.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    Ah! What poor choices I decided to make!
    Deadly, blind mistakes.
    You understand it – a father
    Who killed, and a son
    Now dead. My actions were not thought out and wrong!
    You died at a young age, son,
    Your death was so fast!
    Your life was shortened
    It isn’t your fault,
    It’s mine.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    You knew the correct decision
    But made the choice way too late</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    I understand that now—
    And all the feelings that go with it.
    A while ago, a god hit
    My head really hard,
    It led me
    To behave cruelly,
    Actions that ruined
    All of my deep pleasure.
    All of the concern and emotional pain
    People go through and live with.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">MESSENGER:
    Leader, you are very sad,
    You feel it very deeply.
    However, you have more bad news--
    You’ll know the bad news soon, and it comes from your house.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    Can any new
    Trouble make
    Me feel any worse than I am right now?</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">MESSENGER:
    Your wife died  -- she was
    A very loving mother,
    Poor woman, sad that she died
    From pain that just happened.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    Ugh Hades, god of the dead, you are not easy
    To please! We give
    Your world so many people, but you want more people from us.
    Why do you aim
    To break me down?
    What are you here to tell me
    Now? – you tell
    Only bad news.
    I was suffering before, but now, friend,
    You’ve come to make me feel worse.
    What news do you have?
    What new information
    That makes me anguish in pain? My wife is dead.
    Murder after murder.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    You understand her death now. Her body is no longer hidden by the house.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    There are so many awful deaths. What
    Other consequences does fate have for me? I
    Just cradled my dead son
    And next to me is the dead body of someone I loved.
    Goodness, troubled mother, goodness son.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">MESSENGER:
    At the altar she stabbed
    Herself with a sharp weapon.
    Her eyes slowly lost their light of life
    And she shut her eyes, the window to the soul.
    Before her death, she mourned out loud.
    Her virgin
    Dead son, Megareus.
    Using her last words,
    She wished evil wrath upon you, Kreon,
    The one who killed his own son.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    Ahh! Your words make me feel pain and dread
    Everywhere in my body.
    Can someone
    Kill me with a weapon through my heart?
    I am a miserable coward.
    Filled with dread.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">MESSENGER:
    Your wife, here,
    Pronounces you guilty of killing her sons.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    Tell me how she killed herself.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">MESSENGER:
    She stabbed a blade under her liver,
    To feel the same pain
    That caused the death of Haimon, that she mourns.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    I cannot blame anyone,
    No human being.
    Only myself.
    I killed you, for real.
    People around me, help me get inside,
    I have no feeling or worth at all.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    Your actions are correct,
    If you can make a right choice at the moment
    Due to all of the bad luck and grief.
    You don’t need to say a lot
    With all of the recent pain and misery.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    I take the responsibility now—
    I accept what fate has in store
    And let me die now.
    I would find good fortune in death right now.
    Please let me die, right now.
    I do not want to see
    The sunrise tomorrow.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    You are talking about the future. We
    Need to worry about what is happening right now.
    What will happen later is affected
    By people who have control over it.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    All I want and ask for is to die.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    Stop asking
    No one
    Escapes misery
    After it has happened.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">KREON:
    Please let me die and get me out of this misery.
    I am a stupid, man who does not think through his actions
    Who has killed his son without thinking,
    And also killed my wife.
    I have been condemned by evil spirits and don’t know
    Who to look to for advice
    Or who I can
    Rely on in times of need.
    My actions ruin
    Everything that encounters them;
    And there,
    Unalterable power overtakes
    All the thoughts in my mind.</pre>

<pre class="verse-quote">LEADER:
    Being smart is needed
    For people to be happy.
    Always respect the power of the gods,
    Because the words of over powering men
    Always have consequences—
    And the consequences, later in life,
    Help the proud men understand.</pre>
