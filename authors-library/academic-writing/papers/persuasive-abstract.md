## Persuasive Abstract

The purpose of this project is to persuade King County Juvenile Detention administration to include a drama therapy program into their resocialization process. There are many systemic barriers for these children to get access to the mental health care they need from governmental policies to individual resistance. Resocialization is important for youths to ensure that they are able to successfully transition back into the community and it also assists in decreasing recidivism rates. Drama therapy, already shown to decrease recidivism, can also improve… A drama therapy program can mentally, emotionally, and socially support the youth in their resocialization process. Drawing from a personal narrative approach and from the work of Michael Rohd, the 12-week program will focus on cultivating hope, building resiliency, and developing self-compassion so the youth can focus on finding themselves in relationship to the community that incarcerated them. Having a healthy relationship with the community is important for incarcerated youths as it influences what they may do after their release. The program would start with youths working to better understand themselves and share their personal stories. Afterwards, the youth will engage with their relationship to the community that marginalized them. Then the youths will collaborate in how they would like to share their stories with their community. The conclusion of the program will include a performance piece created and run by the youths in the group. The performance will take whatever form the youths feel best supports their story(ies). Through the implementation of this drama therapy program into the King County Juvenile Detention’s resocialization program would largely benefit the youth incarcerated, the administration team at the center, and the community the youth will transition back into. As Tim Robbins says about the inmates in his theatre program, “People are going to get out of prison, whether you like it or not, but I would think that a smart society would want people with better tools than they had going in.”
