<p class="paper-title">Reflection Paper 1</p>

Overall, the experience I obtained from this project was beyond what I was expecting from the beginning of rehearsals. When we were told to simply find somewhere to start and go, I was extremely nervous because that is not how my mind usually works. I decided that this project was going to be something that I would unleash the spontaneous side of myself. Our group got together and we each had something to bring to the rehearsal. [One groupmate] brought a song, [another] brought objects to play with, [a third] brought an idea, and I brought a let’s-just-do-it attitude. We then just played with what ideas we had and decided to show that step to the class.

After our first rehearsal and performing this in front of the class, we were able to receive feedback on how our work was being perceived. This information was very important for when we were going to work next. Our group was able to focus on specific things and create more of a relationship with that object. This idea was later coordinated in our final scene. It was difficult for me to create a connection with an object. I really did not know how I felt about it. I had the object of a textbook. While I love to learn about the class that the textbook is for, a lot of the homework for class makes me feel overwhelmed. This caused me to feel chained down by the book and caused negative feelings towards the book.

We were unable to meet as a group outside of class before moving into another class workshop. I think this was fine because we were ready for a sit-and-discuss rehearsal to hammer out what we wanted to say with our piece. This reflection time was really important because each of us were able to see how each other’s perceptions of procrastination and busyness. This grew us closer to each other on what we really wanted to do. I think if we had not had sat and discussed this topic, we would not have had the same presentation as we did. This discussion is where [one groupmate] told us his feelings of procrastination and busyness related to that of Cinderella. Without that starting point, we never would have started our princess theme of narration.

During this second class rehearsal we were asked to explore procrastination as if it were a disease to be passed on. This idea was later added to the mix, but I really did not have a connection to it because I was not really included in this exercise. The sickness was passed on to people in my group, but not me. This made it difficult and maybe was the cause for unclarity in the performance. I realized at a later date that I was not getting sick and procrastinating, I had been continuing what I was doing before. I was sort of embarrassed by this during our discussion after our performance.

There was one great rehearsal that we were able to create the basis for our performance. We started out with [his] idea of Cinderella and involving the audience. We then found out that some of the other members did not feel connected to the performance at all. Our group then sat down and discussed how we each feel procrastination again and worked out our own scenes. This, in my belief, was great. We had an idea and briefly discussed it; then we got on our feet and physically did it. I think this rehearsal was the one that really had me accepting the idea of play as a way of exploration. It was fun working with the group on this level.

I think our group had a great dynamic to it when we were all together. I really did not want to do any work without the whole group together. This made it difficult when one member of the group would consistently try to discuss the group project with me without the group. I did not want to dishearten ideas, but I really did not want to talk about ideas without the whole group. I also did not want to discourage the flow of ideas though. It was an awkward place for me to be, I believe. I also found it difficult to put down ideas that I did not really believe were good ones. There were some ideas that we had in the performance that I did not think were the strongest way of performing. Most of these ideas had to do with other people’s narrations, so I did not feel obligated to share my feelings on these topics.

Overall, I believe the performance went very well. I think it would have gone better with a larger audience and more energy, but that is not something that was achievable. I am very proud of what my group and I created, whether or not they feel the same way. I guess that was one thing we should have worked on during the rehearsal process: being more open to one another. I think we did do a great job of being creative and our final performance of this piece was certainly something to be proud of.
