# Group Leading: Emotional Focus

**Degree / Program:** Drama Therapy Specialization  
**Course / Context:** Introduction to Drama Therapy (Fred Landers, Ph.D. & Pella Weisman), Antioch University Seattle  
**Date:** November 12, 2018  

---

- Stand in a circle
    - Close your eyes
    - Self check in – holding any tension
        - Any area where you feel tension, I want you to breathe into that area to release the muscles
        - Head – face – neck – shoulders – arms – hands – upper back – lower back – hips – legs – knees – ankles – toes
- Breathe in - Reach up – grab at stars
    - Slowly let parts of your body go limp from top to your knees, until you are folded in half over like a rag doll
    - Add some movement – In your knees, neck, hips
    - Slowly roll to standing as a group while counting from one to ten and after every number we are going to snap
- Sound round
    - One time around with a random choice
    - Second time around, pause think of how you feel today, express it to the group with a sound and movement
- Walk around the space
    - Increase and decrease your speed
    - Think of how you are feeling, whether you resonated with anyone’s feelings in the group
    - Relate your feeling to a sound and release that sound
    - Play with volume
    - Relate your feeling to an emotion and play with that emotion
        - Play with speed & volume & movement
    - Freeze
    - Pick a different emotion
    - Play with that emotion’s speed, volume, & movement
    - Interact with others in the room – how does your emotion interact with the other person’s emotion?
- Choose a partner
    - Interact with just them
    - Freeze & Shake off that emotion
    - Choose another emotion
    - Interact with your partner with that emotion without words
    - Give your emotion a line of text
    - Freeze
- Pick the partners for a scene
    - State the relationship between the two
    - Location for them to be
    - Two actors enact the scene
        - Free speech
    - Freeze – give an actor a new emotion
        - Think about the various ways of which an emotion can be expressed
            - Volume & Tempo
    - Continue the Emotions game
