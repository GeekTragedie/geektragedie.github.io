<p class="paper-title">2016-2017 Reflection Paper</p>

Over the course of this year, I believe I have been doing well at my personal growth as a thespian. I increased personal understanding in directing, critical thinking, organization, and future planning. During my fall semester, I increased my knowledge of directing through Advanced Directing. The largest improvement I made in this class was finding the type of shows I like to perform. I found that I like to do shows that bring awareness to mental health issues or situations that cause these issues.

During this semester, I also increased my critical thinking skills through my Shakespeare class and submitted my research paper to a conference and was accepted. I also attended this conference and had stimulating conversation on various theories to increase my own studies and personal knowledge. I also increased my critical thinking skills through applying the information I learn in my psychology course to theatre. Whenever I take a psychology course, I try my best to apply it to theatre or my future career.

I increased my organizational education through planning the trip for UCB Tour Co. I ensured their contract was fulfilled. I organized committees for advertising and event organization and made sure to delegate tasks. I also ensured their stay and transportation. I think this increased my knowledge of professional improv companies. I also learned from the performers themselves when they were here.

Finally, I learned a lot this semester about my future and took steps towards what I am looking forward to doing. I have researched graduate schools and talked to advisors regarding my future. I have been working at a facility that counts towards clinical hours for graduate school and have been accepted to do research at a JDC in Sioux Falls by implementing a theatre program. Next year, my goal is to increase my theatre knowledge in the technical theatre realm. I also will expand my arts in healthcare knowledge with theatre through my internship experience class. I also want to attempt to facilitate a devised theatre piece with the improv club based on issues we ask college students about. I am really looking forward to next year (my final year!).
