Throughout this semester, I have learned a lot about myself and my personality. There were multiple levels that have played into this experience, two of which are counseling and this course. I have felt more comfortable with how I act in different situations and I feel it has helped increase my self-esteem and certainly has increased my self-image and self-concept. I am more able to understand why I do what I do, which is a relief. While all of the assignments have certainly helped me think of myself in different ways, I feel as though the MBTI, Life Story, and Q-sort really helped me learn the most about myself.

When I took the Myers-Briggs, my results came out as Introversion, Intuition, Thinking, Judging. All personality test that I have taken have all told me I was a little more extroverted than introverted, but have never been introverted. After taking the quiz, I understood introversion/extroversion as how outgoing a person was. We learned in class that Jung’s idea of introversion and extroversion had to do with where someone puts their energy. After discussing what Jung’s version of introverted was, I understood more about why I was considered introverted on this particular assessment. I do focus more energy on myself during social situations and need more time to get this energy back. I appreciated learning this because it helped me understand my surroundings and how I felt in different situations.

The life story assignment certainly helped me figure out why I am who I am today. It helped me find the roots of my beliefs and my interests, which was very self-fulfilling. I though the way that I separated the chapters of my life were interesting. I feel as though my college life would have taken more time and importance, however, it really did not take up as much of my story. That makes me feel better about the personality theories that say that personalities are more stable over time. It also makes me believe that our personalities are shaped more when we are younger, as theorists hypothesize. It was truly helpful to pin down the parts of my life that largely influenced who I am. It helped me give reason to why I react certain ways and why I am so passionate about different various topics. For example, the reason why I am independent is because I had to be when I was an adolescent going to college. I am very independent and like to have control of events and situations. This independence I have, over time, turned more into leadership, but it is nice to know that this part of me is not a controlling personality, but it actually came from my history. To be honest, I did not expect to learn this much about myself through this assessment. I thought it was going to take a long time and that I would not learn a lot from it. While it did take a long time, it was very informative. I never really sit down and think about my life. I was very surprised at how much this assessment taught me about myself.

The Q-sort helped further my understanding of who I am, and then who I would like to be, so that I can set goals for myself to achieve my ideal self. This idea helped with my counseling, where I was trying to figure out who I was, not based off of others’ opinions. I successfully was able to say what was and what was not part of my personality. This empowered my knowledge of myself and my ability to trust my own opinion for my actions. I also was more empowered to make my own decisions. With this empowerment, I felt more confident to reach my ideal self because I can make my own decisions and then trust them. My correlation findings did surprise me. I have been in a state where I do not completely feel comfortable with myself and who I am. The fact that my correlation between who I am and who I would like to be made me feel better of myself. I was relatively happy with my correlation. It was good to know that I still have some room to grow, but that I am relatively well off.

Through this assessment, I feel as though personality is relatively steady over time. There may be some fluctuations if someone has specific characteristics that they want to work on changing or a traumatic event. I also believe, to an extent, the theorists that state that a personality is fixed year on in life. I do not agree that personalities cannot change, as I stated previously with the fluctuation possibilities.

Before this class, I certainly did not believe that personality was stable. I thought it was possible to change someone and who they are. Now, I have more respect for people and who they are because of their stable personality. While I may not like a person, I can respect that they have their own opinion and personality based on their past experiences or genes.

When it comes to the question of free will or deterministic in personality, I do not believe in one over the other, especially after this class. I can understand the Freudians in how the unconscious plays a role in personality based on previous experiences, but I do not feel as though it is the only influence on our personality. I think the behaviorists also have a largely valid point, but not to the point of Skinner thinking that everything is based on observable behaviors. With these impacts from society, I also believes in epigenetics and the passing along of genes to impact personality. Connected to my Christian beliefs, God gave us free will, but has aspects determined for us. I think this can be applied to personality. We have some deterministic factors in our genes, but we are molded by society into who we end up being.

With all of these aspects considered, I think people are certainly all different. Everyone may have similar genetic backgrounds, but they are then molded and shaped by their surroundings. This shaping by the environment then impacts the person’s behavior and unconscious. The interactions with environment is never going to be the same. With the interactions being different for everyone, I would never believe that people are too much alike. I think people are very independent in their personalities. While people may share Jung’s personality types, they then will present them differently depending on the situation, like reactions in behaviorism.

Overall, I really appreciated this class, personally and educationally. I was able to educate my family through this process because they are very into personality types. I also was able to grow myself, as previously expressed. I was very happy that I took this class and I learned more in this course than I thought I would.
