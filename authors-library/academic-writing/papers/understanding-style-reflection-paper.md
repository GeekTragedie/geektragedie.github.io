<p class="paper-title">Understanding Style Reflection Paper</p>

Before working together in class, I would have defined style as how someone expresses themselves visually dependent upon their own personality, which is not particularly wrong, but now I know there are a lot more aspects included in the definition. Style is a lot more broader than what I had imagined, and there are a lot more aspects that need to be considered then I thought of before.

How I view style now is that it is people’s expression based on the work of external forces and given circumstances. Style has individuality, but also is largely influenced by what is happening around that individual. Style depends on how that individual or group wants to express themselves in the environment they are in. After discussing in class about how style relates to given circumstances, I was able to more fully understand what style meant. Having had to study given circumstances in other courses, I was able to relate what influences style. The questions based off of the Barton reading were also familiar to me as a director.

I would have to say the difficulty with the recent eras research and scenes was that the time period was so broad. We wanted to research what was happening during the time period, but there was a lot of different events and movements that happened. This idea made it difficult to narrow down a scene and what approach to take. When we decided to take two ideas and put them together and read the scene, we realized that the scene worked out a lot better than what we had thought. It was combining the two ideas and putting the scene on its feet that was rewarding. Seeing [one colleague] in her costume was also very rewarding.

When looking at the keys to our world, we realized that there was a lot happening. We wanted to combine the ideas we had, so we realized that the idea of change happened in most of the movements. We decided to take the idea of change and implement it into our scene. We implemented this idea into the scene through [one colleague]’s character deciding to see how it felt to try the life of someone outside of her class. She said she had thought about trying it for a long time, but had not actually done it. This verbalization showed that she had attempted to view the others’ side, but had not taken the full step. The Kennedy’s also played a role in our choices. The Kennedys gave hope to many Americans during the 1960s and many felt as though the hope had died with JFK’s assassination. In our scene, we wanted to give a hope that people can get along and work together, even if they are from different groups.

One other idea that we used was personal space. Many upper class people would try to help those in poverty, but it was from more of a distance, not directly. This idea was shown through the distance of [the two characters] in the scene towards the beginning. [One character] wanted to try and stay farther away from [the other], but as the scene progressed, the gap grew smaller as [she] tried an aspect of [the other character]’s life style.

While being a participant and an observer of the recent era scenes, I learned multiple aspects about style. I learned that there are very different styles, even during the same time period. This idea is understandable considering the different styles around currently, but I had never really considered the different styles of previous eras. I guess I just generalized too much and never attempted to look further. It is also very interesting to see the different tempos of each era and notice the space between people. All of the aspects that we researched for our paper were so different depending on the era; it was very interesting.

I do not really have any questions at this point in time. I usually seem to come up with questions during class and attempt to ask them at that point in time. If I have more questions along the way, I will be sure to address them. Overall, I thoroughly enjoyed this research process and look forward to our upcoming scenes.
