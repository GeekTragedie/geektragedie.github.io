<p class="paper-title">Directing New Work Reflection</p>

For my final project in my playwriting class, I directed a classmate’s new play. I feel as though I grew through this experience and I learned to focus more on certain aspects of directing that I have in the past. I think the reason I focused on different aspects of the play is because the playwright was there and asked me to focus on particular ideas. The playwright’s objectives in this process was to see the play on its feet and to see how his stage directions were executed. While I kept this goal in mind, because the playwright was in the room, I wanted to ensure that the actors fully stayed true to the text.

I approached this process as I would a normal play, but ensured to tell the actors what our final goals were (close to staged while keeping scripts in hand). I did not stick to my normal approach though. I started the process reading the script and then we blocked the show, which was normal, but I noticed that the actors were not sticking to the script that they were given. Due to the playwright being in the room, I found myself trying to make sure the actors stayed to the script more. To do this, I revised my schedule and went back to the beginning of reading through the script to make sure all of the lines are said correctly. I ran some sections multiple times to make sure that the lines were not missed because of the blocking. I also emphasized to the actors that the script was more important than any of the blocking that I gave them. One key moment that I noticed one actor adding words was when he was repeating words because he felt the character was nervous. He took my direction well, but I still feel as though another actor did not fully listen. I do think that was okay, because the playwright could then see what would happen if an actor later were to do this to the script.

There was one bump in the road that came about. The playwright had not attended a couple of rehearsal where we ran the blocking and then attended the final rehearsal. He commented that some of my blocking was not what he had envisioned, so I made some last minute changes. I think that helped me see that reading blocking can be different and that the specificity of language is important. I also learned that actors can take last-minute change pretty well when applying them to a performance. I do kind of wish there were more script changes throughout this process, but I think it went well even though there were very few.
