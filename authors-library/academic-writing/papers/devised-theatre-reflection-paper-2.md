<p class="paper-title">Reflection Paper 2</p>

I was rather uninterested in our final project and I feel as though this interfered with my ability to stay actively involved. There are certainly a lot more ways to obtain money for college than taking out a ton of loans. While there does seem to be one systematic way of doing college and I understand this, I was not able to connect to the performance. I was not connected to the story, which lessened my excitement about the performance and project. These feelings were probably a combination of multiple hours workings towards a separate performance going down the drain and the inability to contribute to the overall story topic. My group had really put a lot of time, thought, and effort into our group performance, and I had taken time from another professor that was also excited for our performance. This professor was rather interested in seeing our final project and seeing how theatre can tell the story of those with mental illness. Our work was then thrown away because other groups in the class had neglected to work on their projects, which seems unfair. All of our work ended up going nowhere and was a waste of time. The time was then replaced with a large group performance that I really did not have any feelings towards. Putting these aggravating feelings aside, I did learn multiple things from this experience.

It was a very different atmosphere to work with the entire class as a group than simply a group of four. I think having multiple voices and ideas putting the performance together was beneficial. There were also people that did not really voice their opinions that I wish would have. It seemed as though there were main directors of the project and some people did not voice their opinions because of this director atmosphere. While I saw myself as putting forth multiple ideas, I wish other people had felt confident and open enough to share their own ideas. This inability to share ideas I feel was one of the problems with having a really large group project. I do not know if this feeling was because we had done our group work at the beginning of the year and then moved to small groups. Switching to the small groups had separated our class a little bit, I feel, and had broken our connection from the beginning of the year.

I really appreciated our ability to connect a Greek tragedy to a modern day issue while keeping the general aspects that make up a Greek play. It goes to show that aspects of previous theatre can really help structure a modern day show. It creates a common ground for the audience to follow, if they know Greek theatre, and also helps the group know what needs to happen when. I also feel as though having to include the chorus was interesting as well. Utilizing the chorus and having to use them really changed the way we performed and thought of the steps we were taking.

I also liked the list of actions we had to include in the play. While it may seem as though it limits the way we do things, it really has the opposite effect. It really opened our mind to different ways of showing ideas to the audience. It also gave us ideas of what to do next in the scenes if we did not quite know which direction we wanted to take.

I do wish our group had gone over to Old Main and Slagle to set up our performance. I really wanted to know and feel how different it feels to perform something in a found space. Found spaces were one of the most interesting ideas that changed the way I think of life this semester. I was curious to seeing how much effort and changing words or blocking would have gone in to rehearsing in a found space.

Overall this class really taught me a lot. I feel as though this performance has taught me a lot as well. While I do wish my performance group had continued, I am good at learning from things I do not expect to happen. I have learned to go with the flow and overcome frustration in things that do not happen the way I expect.

## Group Planning Notes

Here is a list of the characters we have chosen and the mental illnesses we have linked to them:

- Cinderella’s Ugly Step Sister:
  - Anorexia
  - Body dysmorphic disorder
- Ursula from “The Little Mermaid”
  - Sadistic Personality Disorder
- Queen of Hearts from “Alice in Wonderland”
  - Intermittent Explosive Disorder
- Elsa from “Frozen”
  - Agoraphobia
  - Borderline Personality Disorder

Some of the questions we have about these different mental illnesses are:

- What are some of the triggers for these disorders?
- What are some of the symptoms people with these disorders may deal with?
- How would the sociocultural model look at the causes for these mental illnesses?
- How would the sociocultural model look at helping people dealing with these mental illnesses?
- What are some of the stigmas society has against people who deal with these mental illnesses?
- Is there any other information you think would be beneficial to our group as we move forward?
