## Lesson Plan

- Give students Arlecchino handout
- Sit in a circle on the floor and read through handout then discuss character and physicalizing
  - Show and discuss mask properties
  - Answer any questions
- Show class the basic position for Arlecchino and have them practice it
- Divide class into 3 groups and assign each group a different pose
  - Giraffe
  - Goat
  - The Arlecchino Cabriole
- After 5 minutes of practice, have groups demonstrate their poses and have the rest of the class mimics them.
- Have the class circle up and explain the game “Strut Your Stuff”
- Start the game with a basic Arlecchino walk to practice
  - [GeekTragedie] demonstrates “excited walk” and this walk is used in the circle game.
  - [My colleague] demonstrates ‘sneaky walk’ and again this walk is used in the circle game.
- Disband the circle and commence “tired walk” with vocalizations
- Have class give themselves a round of applause and do The Gherardi Bow
