<p class="paper-title">Self-Reflection Prompt 6b</p>

I enjoyed creating my own warm-up. It gave me the chance to listen to my body and to know what it needs. This cannot be done by anyone else but myself. I liked designing my own warm-up because it gave me the chance to really sit down and think about what certain exercises do to help relax certain parts of my body. To execute my warm-up was very fulfilling. My body became fully relaxed and I was able to really focus and listen to it. There did not seem to be a challenging part to this process at all; the entire thing was thoroughly enjoyable because I never get to be selfish. I guess a challenging part of the execution was the fact that I was continually getting distracted by everyone else doing different things than me, especially when I was doing constructive rest and other people were walking around.

The entire warm-up was rewarding, especially being able to focus on my specific problem areas. The warm-up was definitely specified to me and I was able to work for myself. I feel my warm-up did an amazing job at bringing me into NDA. Being able to do this myself gave me time to do the exercises by myself and redo any exercises if I feel I am still holding tension in that specific area. I know this warm-up will help me in the future because I know I get tense if I get stressed. Now I have something that will help me remove this tension and focus at the tasks I need to fulfill to become less stressed by my workload and surroundings.

I noticed that my ankles do not have tension in them as I was watching. My back seemed very relaxed and so did my stomach. I feel as though I was still holding a little tension in my left shoulder because it was higher than my right as I was walking forwards, but my warm-up really focused on releasing this tension. I might need to focus a little more on them. I feel as though my new walk was a lot lighter and I was not ‘clomping’ around. My walk is a lot slower, calmer and more relaxed than my other walk. My first walk was very quick and rushed. My shoulders are also not slouching forward as much in my new walk. What I noticed about my voice is that it is very tense in my first walk. During my second walk, my voice is clearer and it also sounds very open and free, less stressed.

I feel my body and mind are a lot closer to neutral than they were. At the beginning of the year I thought that my body just did what my mind told it to. Now I know that my body has a mind of its own and that my mind needs to listen to it.

I still need to work on releasing the tension in my shoulders and my neck so that it does not affect my jaw as much as it does. I feel the pain in my jaw because of the tension in my shoulders. This habit is going to take a long time to get used to, but it will happen eventually, I hope. To improve this, I need to keep my alignment in mind at all times. I have noticed me mentally doing this outside of class. I watch the way that I walk, especially from class to class with my backpack on. My backpack is usually really heavy and I try to keep my alignment neutral and my backpack centered. I also am very mindful of the way I sit when I am in class. I need to be more mindful of the way I sit when I am doing homework or hanging out with friends. I have noticed with becoming more connected to myself, I have become less involved with other things and more quiet. I do not feel as though I always have to have a say anymore. I believe that this has to do with my alignment because it connects with my mind listening to my body. My voice does not feel tired, it just feels more important and that it should be saved. I am very excited to get to continue this journey with my body and voice, outside of class especially.
