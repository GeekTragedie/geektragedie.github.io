# Reaction Paper to Special Event Attendance

**Degree / Program:** BS in Psychology  
**Course / Context:** Human Development (Dr. Jean Caraway), University of South Dakota  
**Date:** April 30, 2017  

---

- **Name of Event:** National Geographic’s Gender Revolution A Journey with Katie Couric
- **Date and Time of Event:** Wednesday, March 29 from 7pm – 9pm.
- **Duration of Time I Attended Event:** 7pm – 8:30pm
- **Summary of What Happened/ Information Shared:** Before the showing of the movie, the hosts passed out sheets of paper for you to consider while watching. At this event, we watched the movie shown above and then followed with a discussion. The movie covered the life of an LGBTQ person and the struggles they face. It also connects to the public many social issues that may arise with the community and shows the LGBTQ’s point of view, which is important.
- **Connection to Human Development:** The movie was very interesting and covered LGBTQ people from all ages. It was intriguing to learn about people from all ages that have come out. There is a portion of the movie that Couric interviews an older LGBTQ person and an LGBTQ person in the emerging adulthood years. They discussed different terminology and what the community means to them at different ages. The two didn’t particularly agree on all parts, but loved the opportunity to support their community. The emerging adult respected the older adult and the path they made for the LGBTQ community.
  - One other aspect that was the story of a transgender woman that went through her whole life as a male. She got married to a woman and had two children. Once the LGBTQ community was more accepted, she told her wife that she was transgender. The two, still loving each other, remained married and the family helped her transition. I think this really shows how separate sexual orientation and gender identity are.
  - Through these two stories, I really thought about how development impacts different communities in different ways. Different generations view situations differently, even if they are from the same minority group. All people grow up differently and are influenced in different ways, even if society puts them into the same minority group.
