## Drama Therapy

> “...the intentional use of drama and/or theater processes to achieve therapeutic goals”
>
> North American Drama Therapy Association

</section>

<section class="slide">

## What is Drama Therapy?

- It’s not “therapy for actors”
- Process not product
    - More than putting on plays
    - No acting required
- Therapy or Therapeutic?
- Embodiment

</section>

## How to Find a Drama Therapist

- NADTA Website
    - “Find a Drama Therapist”
- Psychology Today
    - “Find a Therapist”
    - Filter Types of Therapy: “Expressive Arts”
- Open Paths Psychotherapy Collective
    - “Find a Therapist”
    - Filter Therapeutic Orientation: “Drama Therapy” or “Expressive Arts Therapy”

</section>

<section class="slide">

## How to Become a Drama Therapist

**Attend a NADTA Affiliated Program**

- Antioch University Seattle
- Lesley University, Boston
- New York University
- California Institute of Integral Studies, San Francisco
- Kansas State University, Kansas City
- Concordia, Quebec, Canada

**The Alternative Route**

- Attend a Counseling Program
- Find a Drama Therapy Mentor
- Complete necessary hours, course requirements, and supervision with mentor
