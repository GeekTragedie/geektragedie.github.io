## Choral Residency Program

- Select songs
  - Songs that emphasize “the capacity for resilience and the possibility of social change”
- Worked with artists to write their own lyrics & music
- Rehearse songs
- Dress rehearsal
- Concert performance
  - Residents, families, & facility staff

<section class="slide">

## Choral Residency Program

- 2 – 3 hour sessions over two weeks
- 54 adolescents (20 Female, 34 Male)
- Two Secure detention facilities serving a large northeastern city
- Director of Professional Choir
  - Based in historically minority area of the same city
  - Assisted by 6 senior members of a choir (Peer Mentors)
- Benefit
  - Improved behavior
  - Internal strengths
  - Increased self-esteem
  - Increased mood

</section>

<section class="slide">

## Choral Residency Program

- Materials
  - Paper with music lines
  - Pencil
- Safety Concerns
  - Count materials brought in
  - Content matter within the songs being written
  - Behavior of adolescents
  - Keeping the group under control
  - Those who feel left out
- Dennie Wolf & Steven Holochwost, Music and Juvenile Justice: A Dynamic Systems Perspective, Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity & the Arts

## Music Education Program

- Have classical guitars
- Bring them to students in the residential juvenile justice facility
- Teach the adolescents guitar
  - Essentials of musicianship
  - Large ensemble
  - Increase repertoire
  - Performing frequently for audiences
  - Lessons: Warm-up to good strokes and tone
  - Half of the class is performing
- When they leave the facility, they can take their guitar with them

## Collage and Haiku Poetry

- Choose words or images
- Glue / Write on a paper however you want to arrange it
- Create a Haiku for each image
  - Poem that consists of three lines
  - First and last lines have 5 syllables
  - Middle line has 7 syllables
  - Lines rarely rhyme
- Give the work a title
- With adolescents, sometimes the participants rap their poems

</section>

## Character Creation

- Get participants paper and writing utensils
- Create a character
  - Walk participants through a series of character creation questions
- Let the participants partner up
- Each participant ask the other questions
  - Participants have to respond as their character

## I am a Magician

- Improvisational game
- Facilitator says “I am a magician and I turn us all into [noun]”
  - Example used airplane
- The participants move around the space as though they were that object

</section>

<section class="slide">

## I am a Magician

- Short game: 5-10 minutes
- Population: Juvenile females
- Vista Maria Juvenile Facility for Girls
  - Dearborn Heights, Michigan
- Julia Taylor
  - Prison Creative Arts Project course
- Benefit
  - Laughter!
  - Freedom
  - Focus
  - Control of your body movements

</section>

<section class="slide">

## I am a Magician

- Materials
  - Facility with space to move around
- Safety Concerns
  - Careful the floor is clean
  - Careful population does not get out of hand
  - Mind the objects being used as to not be a trigger for any of the participants
