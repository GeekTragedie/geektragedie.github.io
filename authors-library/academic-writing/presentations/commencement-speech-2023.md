## Character Development

I want to invite you to think about the type of person you want to become and all the different things you might want to do in your life. Oftentimes when folx are nearing graduation, whether it’s high school, college, university or something else like retirement, they are asked “what are you going to do now?” You may know exactly what you want to do, or what you are going to do. And it’s absolutely okay to not have a clear answer, too. You can say, “I’m taking time to explore my options” or simply, “I don’t know yet.” Maybe you just want no alarms going off to tell you when to wake up, no back-to-back classes or work or meetings or social events or homework. You want time to just “be” and to discover who you are and how you want to do this thing called Life.

One of the things I discovered while taking time off after grad school is that I love playing games, especially games that allow me to explore different parts of myself.

*Audience participation: With a show of hands, who here likes to play games? Where are my card game players? Board games? Video games? Role-playing games?*

Role-playing games, like Dungeons & Dragons, in particular are a great avenue in which to try on different personality traits, abilities, attitudes, beliefs, and a whole lot more while going on adventures with your friends.

Life is kind of like a Dungeons & Dragons campaign, or even your favorite show.

There’s no winning or losing the overall game but there are quests along the way, you’re always upgrading and evolving your character, and finding people and friends to help you along the way. It’s more about the process, not the product.

For example, when creating a character for D&D, there is a lot to think about:

- What name does your character go by?
- How old are they?
- What race are they – for folx not familiar with D&D, “race” could also be called “species”, like a human, tabaxi or cat-person, an elf, a gnome, or a goliath, to name a few.
- What is their “class” or profession or specialty? Are they a Bard who is someone who draws inspiration from the creative arts? Do they find their way with the help of a deity and thus choose to be a Cleric?
- What abilities and skills does your character have? Are they like you and are really good at athletics but not so great when it comes to identifying plants? Or do they have traits that you’d like to have – like being an active listener or more observant?

The knowledge and skills you have all worked so hard to acquire through the years will be incredibly valuable to you, too. You have learned how to read, write, do math, and that tomatoes are a fruit – though might not taste great if you add them to a fruit salad. These are great skills, and you’ve also learned about friendship, being in a community, problem solving, and critical thinking.

Like you, your character will evolve over time – who you are right now, in all your awesomeness, is going to be different from who you will be a year from now. You will have all sorts of experiences, some amazing, some not so amazing, and some just sort of “eh.” These experiences will shape you and some may completely change the direction you want to go. And that is okay.

## Supplies

Now, we don’t want to send you or your character off on a quest or adventure unprepared. When looking forward, whether we’re talking about you or your character, it’s helpful to think about what tools, equipment, and resources are available. Your character may choose to wield a longsword and a shield because they go into battle a lot, or maybe they study the arcane arts and like to use magic, so they carry a spellbook around with them. Like your character, you also have tools to help you on your journey. These could be anything like a fidget that helps you regulate or focus, a notebook you keep close by in case you want to write something down, a favorite item of clothing that helps you feel your best. You already possess so many unique and helpful tools and as you continue on in life, you will find other tools and resources that will help you along the way.

As you embark on your next adventure, I want you to remember something very important: You are not alone. Look at all these wonderful people around you who have supported and cheered you on so far.

## Team Mates

**GeekTragedie:**

One may not be able to complete a quest without the right supplies, but sometimes you aren’t 100% sure of all the supplies you may need on the way. That’s nothing to worry about, just remember where to go for the supplies you need. The Avatar Aang didn’t know how to bend all four elements, which is something he needed to finish his quest, but he sure knew who to go to for help to learn them all. He used his friends, mentors, and gained the supplies he needed along the way. There may be times in your life you don’t feel like you have all the supplies you need to do your quest. That’s okay. Raise your hand here if you have leaned on someone you know to help you gain new skills. Characters have many talents, but can’t do everything themselves. It takes a village. That’s why adventurers know where to go for supplies and take people along with them to help.

You may not be able to do everything yourself, so knowing what others’ strengths are can be helpful. Know what the people around you are good at. Ask others if they have done something before. While magical folk, or wixen, are split into different houses at Hogwarts, they all have their unique skills they bring to the table. Can you imagine the havoc that could happen at that school if all the houses worked together to accomplish goals? Now that’s a magical force to be reckoned with. So find your inquisitive Ravenclaw, clever *investigative* Slytherin, loyal Hufflepuff, and brave Gryffindor, because honestly we need them all on our team for this quest of life. You are the party leader. You hold your own tryouts/auditions. You have final ruling over the friends list.

Now, you’ve built your adventurer character, found your supplies, have created your squad. It’s time to embark on your next quest. This quest that is all yours. You are the leader. You decide what your party does.

## Questing

While on your quest, if you start to feel alone or lonely, remember you have your team with you. While they may not always be there to see, because some quests need to be solo missions, they are always cheering you on and believing in you. They know you’re doing your best. If you don’t seem to have the right team for a particular mission, see if you can make one. You don’t have to be alone on your quest. The grand Naruto swing animation where he’s looking at his fellow students wishing to fit in - that scene portrays that even though we feel alone and misunderstood, we can still be ourselves and strive for better. Naruto shows that even if we feel the world is against us, we should still fight for what we believe is right and just. Even if you feel alone on your quest, there are others that are willing to and want to help you along the way. Sometimes we just need to keep fighting for it or reaching out to ask for support.

## Comparison

You may come across others who are doing similar quests as you, but don’t compare your progress to theirs. They have a whole different team with different skills and different backstories. Captain America became a superhero not by accident, not because of his muscles, not because of his brain, but because of his resilience, perseverance, and determination. They kept telling him he couldn’t, but he eventually found someone who believed in him and told him he could. So don’t compare yourself to others or the expectations of others. Become yourself. Don’t compare your quest to theirs. In fact, admire others and support them because you may not be able to do everything yourself, which is why you have a team, right?

## Conclusion

Here you are, at a transitional part of your time. Having completed a quest! You’ve been on this one for a long time. Congratulations. You have built your character, gathered your supplies, and have gotten to know some of your team for your next quest. You’ll embark on your quest, and remember you are not alone, don’t compare yourself to others.

In the wise words of voice actors, Brian W. Foster and Matthew Mercer: Don’t forget to love each other and How do you want to do this?
