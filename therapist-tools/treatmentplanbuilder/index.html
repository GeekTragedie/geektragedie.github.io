Treatment Plan Builder

Write treatment plans with goals matched to the diagnosis, a SMART objective helper, interventions, and a record of achieved and archived goals (up to 5 active goals with 5 objectives each). Nothing is saved in your browser, so export a .json file before you close the page.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Choose the plan type, then pick diagnoses using ICD-10 or DSM-5-TR.
  2. Insert suggested goals or write your own, up to 5 goals with 5 objectives each.
  3. Use Help Make It SMART to tighten each objective.
  4. As your client makes progress, move finished goals to Achieved goals and fill in the discharge criteria.
  5. Copy the plan into your EHR, or print or export it from the tool menu.

Saving your work: Session-only: nothing is saved in your browser, and refreshing the page clears every field. Use Save .json if you want to keep a copy, and store it somewhere secure. Use initials or a client code, never full names, and keep final documents in your own secure EHR.
