How to use this tool▾
  1. Choose a letter: emotional support animal, academic accommodation, referral, gender-affirming care, or a release of information (ROI) form.
  2. Fill in the client and your details. Upload your practice info once to fill in your license, NPI, and contact details on every visit.
  3. Watch the preview: it writes the letter as you type and highlights anything still missing.
  4. Read every line, then print or copy the letter from the tool menu and sign it.

Fills the tool with a made-up example so you can see how it works.

Saving your work: Nothing saves on its own. Use Save .json to keep your entries and Import .json to load them next time.

Import, save, print, and clear are further down this menu.

Why this matters

Letters carry clinical weight outside the therapy room. Keep them factual, within your scope and your direct knowledge of the client, and limited to what the recipient needs (HIPAA’s minimum necessary standard). Get your client’s signed authorization (an ROI) before sending anything to a third party.

Your data▾

Everything you enter stays in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to or stored by this site, and nothing is saved: refreshing the page clears every field.

Use Save .json to keep a copy, and store it somewhere secure.

Practice data you upload is kept on this device until you click Forget practice data.

Use initials or client codes instead of full names whenever you can, and don’t use a shared or public computer for client information.
