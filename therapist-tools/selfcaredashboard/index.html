Scheduling rules of thumb▾
  • Match Acuity to Your Chronotype: Place high-acuity, complex trauma, or new 90791 intakes in your personal peak energy window—never in your final slot of the day if you experience afternoon energy dips.
  • The "Rule of 3" Buffer: Avoid scheduling more than 3 clinical hours in a row without at least a 30-minute movement/meal/admin buffer.
  • Soft-Landing Final Hour: Whenever possible, make your last session of the day a lower-acuity, resource-building, or familiar ongoing client so your nervous system isn't spiked right at 5:00 PM.
