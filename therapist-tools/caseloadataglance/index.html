Caseload at a Glance

See your de-identified caseload by diagnosis, phase of therapy, time in therapy, age, and any custom traits you add, so you can decide whether a new intake is a good fit. Turn off any fields you don’t use.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Uncheck any fields you don’t track under Fields to track, or add your own.
  2. Add each client under Add a client. Use initials or a client code.
  3. Use Slice pie chart by to see your caseload by diagnosis, phase, age, and more.
  4. Read the intake fit insights before saying yes to a new referral.

Saving your work: Nothing leaves your browser. The page clears when you refresh unless you Save .json or turn on Save in this browser in the tool menu. Leave browser saving off on shared computers.

Tip: Try Load a sample caseload in the tool menu to see how it works.

Import, save, print, and clear are in the tool menu.
