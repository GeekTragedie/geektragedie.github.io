📖 How to Use This Tool▾

What it does

It shows your de-identified caseload as a chart and table, so you can see your balance and decide whether a new intake is a good fit.

Step by step

  1. Under Choose What to Track, uncheck any fields you don’t use, or click + Add a custom field.
  2. Add each client under Add or Update a Client, using initials or a client code. The diagnosis family fills in from the diagnoses you pick, and you can change it. Click Add Client to Caseload.
  3. Use Slice the chart by to see your caseload by diagnosis family, phase, age, and more. For diagnoses, choose Primary only or All diagnoses.
  4. Check your headcount and estimated weekly hours, then read the intake fit insights before saying yes to a new referral.
  5. In the Caseload Table, use Columns to hide columns and the filter row to narrow the list. Use Print caseload report or Save .json in the tool menu.

Fills the page with ten made-up clients so you can see how it works.

Works well with

  • Self-Care Dashboard: import your caseload .json there to weigh your caseload by acuity.
  • Personal Referral Database: when a new inquiry isn’t a good fit, send a referral email.

Tips

  • Set Session length (minutes) to match your sessions so the weekly hours estimate is accurate.
  • Never use full names here. Initials or a client code are enough.

Saving your work

Nothing leaves your browser. The page clears when you refresh unless you Save .json or turn on Save in this browser in the tool menu. Leave browser saving off on shared computers.

Why this matters

Looking at your caseload as a whole helps you spot acuity build-up, competence gaps, and clinical drift before they turn into burnout. Balancing heavier clients across your week supports both client care and your own sustainability.
