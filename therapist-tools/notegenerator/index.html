How to use this tool▾
  1. Pick a note format: a standard progress note, a 90791 intake, DAP, SOAP, BIRP, GIRP, PIRP, narrative, or MSE.
  2. Fill in the session: date, time, length, who was there, location, and an optional CPT code. For telehealth, record where the client was and that they were in a private place.
  3. Pick diagnoses and approaches, then choose the specific skills you worked on. Add risk and safety, and an optional Mental Status Exam.
  4. Read the preview, then copy it into your EHR, print it, or use Create superbill to send the session to the Superbill Generator.

Saving your work: Nothing saves on its own. Use Save .json to keep your entries and Import .json to load them next time. Use initials or a client code, never full names, and keep final documents in your own secure EHR.

Import, save, print, and clear are further down this menu.

Why this matters

Clear, timely notes support continuity of care, document medical necessity, and protect both you and your client. Structured formats such as SOAP, DAP, and BIRP keep a note focused on what you observed, what you did, and how the client responded, which is what payers and auditors look for. Record only what care requires; HIPAA’s minimum necessary standard applies whenever a note is shared.
