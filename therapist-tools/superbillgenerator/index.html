How to use this tool▾
  1. Upload your practice data once (tool menu → Practice data) and your practice, NPI, tax ID, taxonomy, and license fill in on their own.
  2. Add the client and pick their diagnoses. The superbill always prints ICD-10-CM codes and names, because insurers require them.
  3. Add each service. + Add another service copies the one above it, so you only change the date. Import session note fills a service from a Session Note Generator superbill file.
  4. Watch the preview update as you type, then print it for your client. Only fields you filled in appear.

Why this matters

A superbill gives clients what they need to request out-of-network reimbursement: your NPI and tax ID, diagnosis codes, CPT codes, place of service, and fees. Complete, accurate codes mean fewer denied claims. Insurers require ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes.

Your data▾

Everything you enter stays in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to or stored by this site, and nothing is saved: refreshing the page clears every field.

Use Save .json to keep a copy, and store it somewhere secure.

Practice data you upload is kept on this device until you click Forget practice data.

Use initials or client codes instead of full names whenever you can, and don’t use a shared or public computer for client information.
