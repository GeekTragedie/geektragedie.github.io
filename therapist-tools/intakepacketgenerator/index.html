How to use this tool▾
  1. Fill in your practice details (or upload your practice info once), fees, insurance, and policies.
  2. Choose which sections go in the packet, including any assessments and your own uploaded section.
  3. Read the live preview. Open a section under Section wording to change its text; edited sections stop updating automatically until you reset them.
  4. Print the packet or save it as a PDF from the tool menu.

Fills the tool with a made-up example so you can see how it works.

Saving your work: Nothing leaves your browser. The page clears when you refresh unless you Save .json or turn on Save in this browser in the tool menu. Leave browser saving off on shared computers.

Why this matters

Informed consent is an ongoing conversation, not a single signature. A clear packet explains confidentiality and its limits, fees, cancellations, telehealth, and record keeping, so clients can make an informed choice before treatment starts. Licensing boards and the ACA and APA ethics codes require most of these disclosures.

Your data▾

Everything you enter stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to or stored by this site.

Save in this browser keeps your entries between visits. Turning it off erases them from this device. Use Save .json for a backup you control.

Practice data you upload is kept on this device until you click Forget practice data.

Use initials or client codes instead of full names whenever you can, and don’t use a shared or public computer for client information.
