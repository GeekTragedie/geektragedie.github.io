How federal and WA taxes differ▾

1) The IRS taxes your Net Profit (Gross Income minus Deductible Expenses) for Self-Employment Tax (15.3%) + Federal Income Tax. 2) If you practice in Washington State (My DOR), the state B&O tax applies to your Gross Income (before expenses), and WA has a Small Business B&O Tax Credit that automatically reduces or zeroes out B&O taxes in lower-revenue quarters.

Understanding Business Licenses: Federal vs. State vs. County vs. City

Clinicians are thoroughly trained on their Clinical DOH License, but private practice also requires Business & Municipal Licenses depending on where you physically sit when providing therapy (including your home address for telehealth!).

Federal, state, city, and county licenses explained▾
1. Federal (IRS & CMS)
  • EIN (Tax ID): Free from IRS.gov; never expires.
  • NPI Type 1 & Type 2: Free from NPPES; Type 1 is you, Type 2 is your PLLC/Group.
  • 1099-NEC Filings: Due Jan 31 if you paid any contractor/biller/supervisor $2,000+ in 2026 ($600+ for 2025 payments).
2. State Level (SOS, DOR & DOH)
  • Secretary of State (PLLC/LLC): Requires an Annual Report every year in your formation month.
  • State Dept. of Revenue (e.g., WA My DOR / UBI): State master business license, DBA/Trade Names, and B&O tax filings.
  • Clinical License (DOH): Your LMHC/LICSW/LMFT renewal.
3. City Level (Incorporated Limits)
  • City Business License: If your office OR home telehealth desk is inside incorporated city limits (e.g., Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue), you almost always need an annual City Business License endorsement.
  • Home Occupation Permit: Often bundled with your city license for home-based telehealth.
4. County Level (Unincorporated & Assessor)
  • Unincorporated Area License: If your address is in unincorporated county territory (outside city limits), the County governs your local business license instead of a city.
  • County Assessor Property Tax: Counties track business equipment/furniture over certain thresholds.
  • County Clerk DBA: In some states (outside WA), DBAs are filed at the County Clerk.
