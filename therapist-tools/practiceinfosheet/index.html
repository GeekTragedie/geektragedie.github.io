Practice Info Sheet

Your NPI, EIN, taxonomy codes, licenses, voicemail scripts, and tech stack in one printable place. The page clears on refresh unless you export a .json file or turn on browser saving.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Fill in your clinical credentials: NPI, licenses, taxonomy, CAQH, and ProviderOne.
  2. Add your business details: EIN, UBI, entity type, and finance tools.
  3. List your tech tools and whether each one has a signed BAA.
  4. Pick a view in the tool menu, then print or copy just that view.

Saving your work: This sheet holds sensitive numbers. Nothing leaves your browser. The page clears when you refresh unless you Save .json or turn on Save in this browser in the tool menu. Leave browser saving off on shared computers.

Tip: The Intake Packet Generator can import this sheet’s .json file to fill in your practice details.

Import, save, print, and clear are in the tool menu.

Select View Mode (Filters Screen & Print Output):

1. Therapist & Clinical Credentialing View

Quick-reference numbers for insurance credentialing, superbills, Medicaid/ProviderOne, CAQH, and clinical operations.

State Licenses & Specialty Credentials

Include active licenses, prior associate numbers (e.g., MC/LMHCA often needed for work history), and national certifications (NCC, RDT, EMDR).

CAQH ProView

Medicaid / State Provider ID (e.g., WA ProviderOne)

Professional Liability (Malpractice) Insurance

2. Business Entity, Tax & Contact View

Essential legal entity numbers, W-9 / tax identifiers, addresses, and financial administration details for practice owners.

Tax & Government Identifiers

Financial, Banking, Accounting & Tax Cadence

Track your business bank, bookkeeping software (e.g., QBO), quarterly B&O / IRS tax schedules, and business consultants.

3. Tech & Admin Stack Directory

Placed at the bottom so it never overwhelms daily clinical paperwork. Tracks domains, DNS, EHR, HIPAA BAAs, and recovery notes.

Domain & Web Architecture

Where your domains are registered/billed, where DNS is managed (e.g., Cloudflare), and your hosting/deployment pipeline.

Clinical & Practice Operations Software (with HIPAA BAA Tracker)

EHR/billing platforms (e.g., Headway, SimplePractice), telehealth, phone/SMS (Google Voice), email/storage (Google Workspace), and directories.

Security Tip: If printing this sheet or saving unencrypted backups, consider writing where your recovery codes are stored (e.g., "Bitwarden Vault") or only include non-PHI platform recovery keys.
