CEU Tracker

Log your continuing education and see what’s left before renewal, including the longer health equity and suicide prevention cycles. Nothing is sent to a server. The page clears on refresh unless you export a .json file or turn on browser saving in the tool menu.

How to use this tool▾
  1. Pick your state and license, then enter your renewal date and the dates of your last health equity and suicide trainings.
  2. Log each course with its hours, and check every requirement it counts toward. Hours only count once toward your total.
  3. Watch Progress to see what’s left this cycle and in the longer cycles.
  4. Attach certificates (a link or an embedded file) so your audit packet is ready if you’re selected.

Saving your work: Nothing leaves your browser. The page clears when you refresh unless you Save .json or turn on Save in this browser in the tool menu. Leave browser saving off on shared computers.
