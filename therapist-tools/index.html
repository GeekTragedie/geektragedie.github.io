Welcome to Your Therapist Toolkit!

A static web-based toolkit designed by a private practice clinician, for private practice clinicians. These tools help manage private practice workflows with the confidence of knowing all client data stays 100% local on your machine for HIPAA compliance and security.

Day-to-Day Operations

Tools to streamline clinical documentation, resource management, and client support.

Active Tool

Letter Generator

Fillable templates for professional clinical letters, including Emotional Support Animals (ESA), Academic Accommodations, and Clinical Referrals with diagnosis-based suggestions.
